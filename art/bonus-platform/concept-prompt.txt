One raised circular stone bonus platform for a polished cartoon skateboarding platform game. A solid flat walkable circular top assembled from seven chunky radial stone slabs around a central circular slab, tight fitted seams, very gently worn and chipped edges. The entire pedestal is a squat steep-sided cylinder, diameter about 3.2 metres and height about 0.9 metres. Clearly raised enough that a character must intentionally jump up onto it. Its side wall is almost vertical: absolutely no stairs, ramps, slope, low approach lip or surrounding terrain.
Weathered warm gray sandstone with softly painted surface variation, small moss traces in a few side cracks, thick sculpted chamfers, chunky angular silhouette details. Some restrained shallow carved geometric markings on the upper ring indicate a special platform, but no letters, numbers or readable text. The landing surface is broad, solid, level and unobstructed, not a hollow stone ring. Game-ready stylized low-poly sculpt language, not photogrammetry or noisy rubble. No floating effects, beacon, characters, plants sticking up, weapons, spikes, pillars or buildings.
A single complete isolated prop on a simple neutral gray background, three-quarter elevated view showing the whole circular top and vertical side wall. Soft neutral studio light, no hard cast shadow. Entire model in frame with generous margins. Clean consistent stone geometry suitable as a Meshy image-to-3D reference.
