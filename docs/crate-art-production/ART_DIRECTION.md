# Crate family — 5 November 2026

Source art direction: [Crash Bandicoot 4: Crates, Simona Sanin / Felipe Sanin](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/9mPL0a). Studied the modular sculpted boards, slightly bowed frame members, recessed seams, painted identifiers and complete face construction. No source artwork, texture or mesh is included in the game.

`concept.png` is an original built-in ImageGen direction sheet. The runtime kit is authored procedural geometry and deterministic canvas paint, not a generated mesh. No Tripo credits were used. The shapes stay identical at every viewing distance and in reflection passes.

The fourteen runtime variants are wood, empty, mystery, life, protection, five-hit, wooden bounce, metal bounce, plain metal, TNT, Nitro, outline switch, Nitro-clear switch and checkpoint. Repeated timber sections, chamfers, grain scale, hardware and pale stencil paint tie them together. The protection box retains the game's crossbones vocabulary. Tops and undersides have modeled planks, framing and hardware; upright marks appear on every side.

The collision cube, crate dimensions, stacking, edge grinding, rewards, blast radius, fuse duration and movement tuning retain their existing contracts. Visual recoil lives beneath the collision owner. Five-hit pips empty progressively. Explosives have independent fuse/idle motion. Spent switches depress and display a check. Run-mode numbers and boost marks use the modeled shell. A consumed checkpoint leaves a small non-solid compass seal at the saved point.

Splinters use the same stain atlas as their intact crate, with jagged light end grain and separate forged nails. A bounded, level-owned instance pool supplies tumble, ground/solid contact, rebound, settling and finite retirement. The existing puff renderer supplies dust and explosion clouds; brief impact stars and shock rings add the initial punch. Reset, bonus death and disposal clear the pool.
