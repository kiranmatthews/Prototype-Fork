# School · Miami reference reconstruction

Source-owned free skate level: `school-miami`. Open `/?playtest&level=school-miami`; add `&lite` for a quick preview. The local review page at `/tools/school-review.html` provides an overview, landmark views, a full-render switch, and repeatable live-game checks. The level is exported through `src/levels/codex-lab.ts` and registered in the built-in level registry. It is a local reference session opened through the direct playtest URL; it is not added to campaign progression.

## Reference and scale

The supplied reference is VGCartography's oblique map of the original **Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater — School, Miami**. Its layout, rather than the later School II level, is the basis for this reconstruction:

- [VGCartography: School map](https://www.deviantart.com/vgcartography/art/Tony-Hawk-s-Pro-Skater-School-Map-947728299).
- [Planet Tony Hawk: original School guide](https://planettonyhawk.gamespy.com/View6a21.html?id=3&view=thps_faqs_guides.Detail), used to verify the starting canopy, stair/green-rail route, pools, and hill → gym roof → air conditioner → plank → long awning route.
- [HGW 2017: original PS1 School all-gaps footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UC1iKl9Va8g), used for the separate roof landing and original obstacle relationships.
- [Tom Hayes: original THPS walkthrough](https://www.supercheats.com/playstation/walkthroughs/tonyhawksproskater-walkthrough01.txt), used to cross-check the named gap/rail relationships.

`schoolMapPoint()` reverses the map's oblique projection, including elevation displacement. World units remain metres. The scale anchors are a 28 × 15 m basketball court, 0.76 m picnic-table tops, 3.2 m deep-pool transitions, and the existing skater. The upper court is Y=0, lower campus Y=−5, gym roof Y=7, and starting canopy Y=3.4.

**Precision limit:** one oblique image does not establish original collision dimensions or traversal timings. This is a calibrated reconstruction of the visible arrangement, using inferred heights and widths; it is not an extracted original level or independently verified centimetre-for-centimetre copy. Original game textures, score/tape challenges and loading portals are not reproduced. The gym is physically connected beneath its roof.

## Playable features

- Starting blue canopy, eight traced planters and five picnic-table locations. Four planters sit between the canopy and classroom; four sit beneath/in front of it. The native canopy drop connects to the forward planter's coping for Gimme Gap. The four classroom-side planters support three successive ollie/grind transfers in one line.
- Main classroom block, winding classroom wings, graded lawns with low curbs beside the stairs, and the traced west descent with both planter edges. The low wedge jumps toward the classroom-side Long Ass Rail. Hall Pass rises from the opposing grass bank on a high crossing rail, then drops onto the alcove planter and roof.
- Smooth downhill stair/handicap corridor, separate descending green handrail, long blue canopy and facing Kicker Gap ramps.
- Two excavated swimming pools with continuous curved transitions, flush rims, diving boards and Swim Team transfer rail. A charged ollie released at the lip clears directly into the other basin in either direction. The foundation has real holes, so no invisible floor spans a basin.
- Outer perimeter quarters with the real Mini Gap beside the rear playground, plus the facing Huge Rail quarter around the classroom wing. A striped transition toe is not treated as a second coping. The broad riding lane connects to the pool deck; a low classroom planter transfers into the complete Huge Rail grind. The 1.25 m transition rise remains an inference from the map and skater, not an extracted measurement.
- Rear classroom with its pitched blue roof, raised front bank and two short grind rails traced separately from the roof seam. The 1.15 m bank has tapered side rollouts; its deck connects to the existing middle funbox. The short rails sit 0.45 m above the deck.
- Open drainage ditch with banked sides, low bed, end roll-outs and three arched footbridges. Ditch Slap clears the open trench between bridges in both directions. Crossing a bridge and skating beneath it use different real surfaces. A narrow wedge in the bed launches over the third footbridge and lands back on the yard.
- Accessible gym interior with a full-scale court, two long opposing quarterpipes and the small quarter beneath its single basketball hoop. The hoop quarter is 10 m wide with a 1.35 m transition radius, inferred from the inset and checked against the existing skater. Roof deck, perimeter quarters, air conditioner and facing kickers, skylight, vent hips, dumpster rail gap and rear service quarter. A curved row of low garbage crates and its separated final crate occupy the rear corner.
- Separate fenced bonus roof across a real 4.5 m roof-edge gap, with its north/east quarter and small wedge. A charged forward vert exit from the gym coping reaches it.
- The service hill climbs from the rear alley toward the front of the gym, then turns left through a split in the east coping toward the air-conditioner kicker. Brake to cruising speed for that turn. The plank clears the north roof edge before descending onto the long awning.
- Five native checkpoint crates and out-of-bounds recovery. As a native free skate level, it has no finish gate that would terminate a line.

The work adds level data, local review tools and a camera clearance correction for low roofs. It does not modify `src/tuning.ts`, the movement controller, or the shared component builders. Explicit grind paths cover real lips/rails; incidental platform edges are opted out. Planter, table, wall and plank paths use the visible ledge geometry without extra freestanding rail tubes. Hall Pass has the source grass-bank boundary fence. Cosmetic treads, windows, court paint and tile skins do not add collision obstacles.

## Validation

Run `node tools/test-school-miami.mjs`, `node tools/test-school-details.mjs`, `node tools/test-school-camera.mjs`, `npm run check:levels`, and `npm run build`. Do not substitute `check:all` for the focused workflow.

The School regression test builds the real level and player, validates editor JSON, raycasts 27 supports/clearances, drives fourteen route cases, clears twenty-seven jump/vert cases, and tests nine grind cases. It uses the same unit-clamped, two-decimal input axes as the game. It checks both directions across all three bridges, both Kicker Gap directions, both pool-air directions, the charged roof climb and braked top turn, the air-conditioner gap, Roof 2 Roof at two speeds, Over a Footbridge at two speeds, Mini Gap coping transfer and rollout, plank-to-awning landing, and both downhill-rail directions, both direct pool-to-pool transfers, both Ditch Slap directions, Garbage Ollie over the actual crate, and the complete dumpster rail transfer through a grounded rollout. Accepted cases have zero bails and finite positions. Tuning must remain unchanged.

The final browser sequence contains 59 live cases, including width coverage at the perimeter floor join, both stair-side curb grinds and the 19 detail cases below. Both lite and full modes pass all 8,928 fixed steps, including the banked checkpoint return, with no page errors and unchanged tuning. The review tab retains one older `MutationObserver.observe` error from 13:03:49 UTC whose cause was not isolated. That historical entry is retained separately (`school-miami-browser-review-console.json`). A fresh standalone full-render session with ordinary input also has no console errors or warnings (`school-miami-browser-fresh.json`).

`tools/test-school-details.mjs` adds seven support probes and nineteen native skating cases. All three gym quarters complete an air, transition landing and return at least one metre onto the flat court at approach speeds 12, 16 and 20. Direct Roof 2 Awning checks at speeds 16 and 18 require the first landing on the blue canopy. Four bank crossings stay grounded across the slope joins at speed 10, with a maximum step below 0.228 m and no loss below 10 m/s. Both short rails complete grounded approaches, full grinds and supported rollouts in both directions, without balance assistance. These cases share their controller code with the live browser suite. A slower direct roof attempt at speed 12 clips the descending plank; the accepted route requires the tested approach momentum.

Swim Team requires the first landing after leaving one pool to occur inside the other basin; an intermediate landing on the shared deck fails. Dumpster Rail Gap requires the intended takeoff rail, the intended receiving rail for at least eight frames, and a grounded rollout. Garbage Ollie must pass above the real last crate. The four street transfers and Park Gap likewise require at least eight frames on the exact receiving rail followed by a supported landing. Hall Pass requires the crossing rail, fourteen receiving grind frames and a roof landing. The upper chain requires all four planters in order; the lawn transfer follows the receiving corner before ollieing inside the lawn. These checks prevent a nearby landing or a transient rail catch from being counted as the named gap.

Both Mini Gap checks require the named takeoff and receiving copings, then a supported rollout. The perimeter case follows the receiving curve before a normal side ollie into the yard. Every full-grind check reaches the end of its specific rail and lands on supported ground, including both directions on the green handrail and the roof plank. All rail checks use the ordinary balance axis; none grants a balance boost or special meter. The classroom transfer begins on the ground, catches its low planter, transfers to Huge Rail and traverses its full 27.48 m before landing by the pools.

The browser review runs ordinary virtual-controller input through the real frame loop. A placement isolates each feature; the rider's trajectory is never corrected during a route. The one intentional exception is the fall probe after banking a checkpoint, which places the rider outside the campus and observes the native death/respawn cycle. The live sequence begins at the native spawn without a placement override, then covers the downhill, Kicker Gap, both pools, roof hill, winding halfpipe, gym entrance, roof gap, plank, rail and checkpoint recovery.

`tools/school-circuit.ts` adds a complete native-spawn circuit shared by the headless and live browser tests. It follows the canopy → Gimme Gap planter grind → downhill → Kicker Gap → rear perimeter → winding corridor → pool deck → west uphill → upper court. All 51 waypoints complete in 2,404 fixed steps, travelling 389.9 m with 21 frames on the intended planter rail and 555 frames grinding uphill on Long Ass Rail. A normal side ollie exits onto the upper court. There are no intermediate placements, velocity changes, or resets. The controller brakes to cruising speed before the narrow bends and to walking speed at the tight pool-end and final courtyard turns, using the ordinary grab/brake button.

The low-roof camera correction lowers the chase shot beneath the awning instead of pulling into the skater's head. Padded clearance probes anticipate the roof edge and ease the shot back up after leaving it. Fourteen stationary views, 722 moving stair samples in both directions, and recovery at 30/60/120 Hz retain full trailing distance and keep the tested body span in frame. The moving route's largest camera step is 0.280 m. Existing camera tests also pass: unchanged flat framing, 163 protected steep/vert samples, four real vert routes, sixteen rail catches and 553 grind frames. See `school-miami-camera.json`.

Validation ran against the shared working tree, which contains unrelated runtime edits outside the School commits. `docs/performance/school-miami-runtime.json` fingerprints the tested movement/input/level files; repeat the checks after integrating the local commits with the intended runtime.

## Remaining reference work

The eighth source comparison corrects the back corridor topology: the perimeter's striped toe had been mistaken for an opposing coping. The facing Huge Rail quarter now follows the classroom edge, while Mini Gap splits the outer quarter. The lower planter feeds that classroom quarter, and the pool-end route stays outside the oval basin. The new route checks pass without moving the rider during a run.

The lawns now descend beside the stairs, keeping their curb lines close to the road. Both curb grinds and the lawn-to-lawn transfer reach supported landings. The classroom foundation extends down behind the graded lawn. A full-width floor joins the perimeter quarter to the yard; five fixed ray probes and an actual cross-lane ride cover the formerly exposed strip. The camera/access audit now covers the low awning in both directions. The ninth comparison adds the hoop quarter and the rear bank/short rails/pitched shed roof. Remaining work includes the missing gym entrance annex and the original doorway arrangement, source dimension certainty and further boundary/detail comparison. The original map and footage establish obstacle relationships, but they do not uniquely determine elevation, collision radii or launch timing.

No original School `.psx`/`.trg` files or extracted mesh were found in this workspace. To remove dimensional uncertainty, the original School geometry or an extracted mesh would allow an independent comparison. [Neversoft Multitool](https://github.com/slfx77/neversoft-multitool) documents PSX geometry-to-glTF and TRG rail/spawn extraction. The [THPS native port](https://github.com/LANCommander/TonyWasAProSkater) likewise requires the user's own original game data and does not supply it. The older noclip viewer listed a THUGPro School port, but that renderer was removed and its historical public data URL is unavailable; it was not used as dimensional evidence.

With those files, compare a common court/roof scale, pool lips and floors, rail endpoints and slopes, jump takeoff/landing edges, and the service-hill/awning connections. Preserve the existing skater movement model while correcting geometry. The original geometry would resolve measurement uncertainty, but the visible gym entrance difference can still be corrected from the reference and original footage. Treat the current version as a validated playable map reconstruction; strict original 1:1 fidelity remains unverified.
