Its top is 843 units above the floor. The reconstructed bank's straight middle face is 23.824° and joins the floor smoothly. This is a shape difference that uniform world scaling cannot remove. Applying the source profile must retain the existing skater's usable approaches, rail catches and rollouts.

## Reference and scale

The supplied reference is VGCartography's oblique map of the original **Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater — School, Miami**. Its layout, rather than the later School II level, is the basis for this reconstruction:

- [VGCartography: School map](https://www.deviantart.com/vgcartography/art/Tony-Hawk-s-Pro-Skater-School-Map-947728299).
- [Planet Tony Hawk: original School guide](https://planettonyhawk.gamespy.com/View6a21.html?id=3&view=thps_faqs_guides.Detail), used to verify the starting canopy, stair/green-rail route, pools, and hill → gym roof → air conditioner → plank → long awning route.
- [HGW 2017: original PS1 School all-gaps footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UC1iKl9Va8g), used for the separate roof landing and original obstacle relationships.
- [GamesFirst, 1999 review](https://gamesfirst.com/articles/1999/tony-hawks-pro-skater/), which describes dropping from the School roof into the gym. The enlarged map independently shows the missing pane.
- [Hidden Palace: April 1999 prototype comparison](https://hiddenpalace.org/Tony_Hawk%27s_Pro_Skater_(Apr_9,_1999_prototype)), which distinguishes the early accessible side passage from the closed final version.
- [Tom Hayes: 2001 walkthrough](https://www.cheatcodes.com/guide/faq-walkthrough-tony-hawks-pro-skater-playstation-15891/), whose School expert exercise explicitly describes flat wooden banks with rails up the end, across the top and down the other end.
- [Tom Hayes: original THPS walkthrough](https://www.supercheats.com/playstation/walkthroughs/tonyhawksproskater-walkthrough01.txt), used to cross-check the named gap/rail relationships.

`schoolMapPoint()` reverses the map's oblique projection, including elevation displacement. The original authoring-coordinate scale anchors were a 28 × 15 m basketball court, 0.76 m picnic-table tops, 3.2 m deep-pool transitions, before the user-requested enlargement. In those authoring coordinates, the upper court is Y=0, lower campus Y=−5, gym roof Y=7, and starting canopy Y=3.4.

**Precision limit:** one oblique image does not establish original collision dimensions or traversal timings. This is a calibrated reconstruction of the visible arrangement, using inferred heights and widths; it is not an extracted original level or independently verified centimetre-for-centimetre copy. Original game textures, score/tape challenges and loading portals are not reproduced. The gym is physically connected beneath its roof.
