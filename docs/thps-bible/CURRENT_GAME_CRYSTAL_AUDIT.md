# Current game crystal audit

Generated by `node tools/crystal-placement-report.mjs` (commit `75c00bd1`); per-level records are in [`data/current-game-crystals.json`](data/current-game-crystals.json). This is a factual baseline of where the game places its level crystal (one per level) and what it takes to reach it, measured on the same courses and route lines as the crate and geometry audits ([../crate-bible/CURRENT_GAME_AUDIT.md](../crate-bible/CURRENT_GAME_AUDIT.md), [../crate-bible/CURRENT_GAME_GEOMETRY_AUDIT.md](../crate-bible/CURRENT_GAME_GEOMETRY_AUDIT.md)). It makes no comparison with Tony Hawk's Pro Skater; that belongs in the bible. Rerun the script after level changes; it takes about two minutes.

## Headline numbers

- **21 crystals are in play in 27 campaign courses**: 20 authored in level data or a native builder, 1 placed by the engine default (`Level.placeCampaignCrystal`, 6 m before the gate): `hollowwake-descent`. 6 campaign courses have no crystal in play: `crab-chief` (boss encounter: Level.isBossLevel), `jungle-gate-run` (Secret Island course: levelAllowsCrystal() is false), `skystream` (Secret Island course: levelAllowsCrystal() is false), and `sky`, `jungle-terraces`, `rolling-bones`, Secret Island courses that author one which `Level.crystal()` suppresses (measured separately below). The 3 puzzle-trilogy levels have none: they are not campaign levels, so the default never runs, and none is authored.

By lateral offset from the walked line: on-path 15, off-line 3, detour 3 (on-path ≤ 3 m, off-line ≤ 8 m, detour beyond).

## Method

- **Loading and identification.** Each level is built with `new Level(scene, entry)` in the headless `withSkateRuntime` harness after restoring the published pack. `Level.prototype.crystal` and `Level.prototype.placeCampaignCrystal` are wrapped for the run: every crystal call is recorded with its position, whether the level allows a crystal and whether the engine default made it. A crystal is *in-game* when `Level.crystalPickup` exists, *suppressed* when a level authors one but `levelAllowsCrystal()` / `Level.isBossLevel` stops `Level.crystal()` (Secret Island and bosses), *none* otherwise. *Authored* crystals come from a `crystal` component (level data, or a native builder's component list); the *default* is `Level.placeCampaignCrystal`.
- **Pickup envelope.** Box3.intersectsBox(playerBox, crystalBox): the rider's centre within ±1.5 m of the crystal on X and on Z, feet between p.y − 1.336 m and p.y + 3.0 m (rider box 0.936 m tall). While grinding the box reaches 0.35 m below the feet, which ride 0.15 m above the rail, so a rail line between p.y − 1.486 m and p.y + 3.2 m collects. The visible gem floats at p.y + 1.75 m. Source: `Level.crystal()` in `src/level.ts`, the collection test in `src/player.ts`, confirmed by the reach probe's `crystal-pickup-envelope` row. A crystal touched in mid-air counts at once, survives a soft death and is banked at the gate (probe row `crystal-death`), so a touch with a lethal landing is reported as a *fatal shortcut*.
- **Route line and walk.** As the geometry audit: the camnode lane when spawn and gate lie within 40 m of it and it spans at least half its length, otherwise spawn → checkpoints → gate; *progress* is arc length from the spawn's projection toward the gate's. The route floor is tracked by the geometry audit's walker (0.25 m steps, ±2.4 m corridor, ≤ 12 m drift). A stretch the walker reaches by leaping beyond every jump across a span that outline (switch-built) crates or surfaces would bridge is not used as a start for the reach search until a ride or a checkpoint; other unexplained leaps keep the route trusted. The floor around the finish pad and around each checkpoint is added as a start, because the course reaches them by design.
- **Reach grid.** Vertical probes every 0.5 m over a 80 m square around the crystal (240 m when the first pass finds no way in) collect every standable face (`Level.groundMeshes` minus movers, plus steel and ! crate lids), skipping faces in pits, under lethal water, below `killY`, lethal or out-of-bounds meshes, faces without 0.9 m of headroom, points inside wall colliders, the finish pad and its glow column. Neighbouring probes less than 0.6 m apart in height form walkable patches. Patches are linked by measured moves: a step up of ≤ 1.78 m is an ollie, ≤ 2.85 m a jump, ≤ 4.59 m a double jump, ≤ 6.99 m a ledge grab (lip ≤ 2.4 m above the feet; never crate tops); gaps up to 14 m use the measured flat ranges (foot 5.85 m, double 9.15 m, board 9.36 m from cruise, 13.83 m at 23 m/s), stretched or shortened for the height difference by a two-gravity arc through the measured apex (as the geometry audit). A bottleneck search from the start patches minimises the hardest move, then the number of moves. From every reachable probe the crystal is then collected by standing in the envelope, by dropping in from a surface edge above, or in the air: the arc of each move is tested over the 3 m pickup square at its distance. A touch is *survivable* when it is a vertical hop back onto its own surface or when safe ground lies under the crystal. The search is repeated with every outline crate and outline surface materialised; when only that succeeds the crystal *needs a switch*.
- **Grind lines.** Authored rails (including coping, ropes, moving and trick rails), the systemic edge grinds and crate rails are sampled every 0.1 m. A rail *passes through* the envelope when a point lies inside the pickup square at a collecting rail height; a *grind exit* reaches it when the rail passes within 3.248 m of the square and the exit apex (1.229 m tap, 2.301 m full above the grinding feet) reaches the band. The line must be catchable: a rail point within 2.1 m (horizontal) of a trusted route sample or a reachable probe, between 2 m below and 2.3 m above its feet, or one rail-to-rail hop (≤ 3 m) from such a line.
- **Vert.** Coping lines come from coping rails, else from the top edge of steep faces of vert meshes. Vert airs go straight up and land back on the same face, so a crystal is in vert reach only within 1.5 m of a coping line; the air needed above the coping is matched to the measured tiers (half pipe: pumped first air 12 m/s 0.567, 15 m/s 1.995, 18 m/s 3.846, 23 m/s 7.101 m, coasting 18 m/s 0.914, 23 m/s 4.541 m, pumped session 8.758 m; decked quarter: 15 m/s 1.635, 18 m/s 3.245, 23 m/s 6.647 m pumped, 3.536 m coasting at 23 m/s). A coping with transition on both sides is a spine (vert air or spine transfer from either pipe; class *transfer*). The flat in front of the coping must be reachable.
- **Launchers.** Crates (stomp 2.87 m, arrow 3.77 m, held arrow 5.92 m above the lid, each plus a 1.74 m double jump), Coral Crabs (2.67 m above the back) and turtles (5.17 m, held + double 9.05 m above the floor) within 4 m of the pickup square, carried forward at run speed; trampolines within 15 m (3.746 m above the deck, 5.895 m held, scaled with the square of the bounce speed). Kickers and launch ramps use the measured ollie-at-the-lip table (0.8, 1.2 and 1.8 m rises at 12, 18 and 23 m/s; a kicker needs at least √(90 × rise) m/s to crest), along the launch direction taken from the ramp face; the crystal must lie in the ramp's launch lane. Every launcher must itself be reachable.
- **Movers, swings, wallrides.** Movers are swept along their travel (and orbit/spin); a deck that passes through the envelope, or a basic move from the deck, counts. Rope swings: the hands hold anywhere along the rope and the feet hang 1 m below the grip. Wallrides use only wall colliders (`level.walls` boxes and wall paths; a wall whose visible face is off the 90° grid is skipped as a slab collider): riding the wall reaches 1.5 m above the floor in front of it, a tap-ollie entry + wallie 4 m, a full-ollie entry + 0.5 s pumped wallie 6.3 m, a kicker-fed entry 7.4 m at 12 m/s, 14.1 m at 18 m/s, 18.8 m at 23 m/s (a ≥ 1.5 m kicker within 12 m — PROVISIONAL), with the rider at most 2.8 m out from the face.
- **Classification.** The *reach class* is the easiest survivable way found, in the order walk < ollie < on-foot jump < double jump < ledge grab < grind line < launch aid < mover < vert air < transfer (spine) < wallride < unreachable / unclear. *Other ways* lists the other methods found (best one per kind). Thresholds are named constants at the top of the script with their sources (`docs/crate-bible/data/skate-crate-probe.json`, `docs/thps-bible/data/skate-reach-probe.json`); the only provisional value is the kicker-feed radius for wallrides.
- **Visibility.** (1) *Line of sight*: from the walked floor + 1.5 m every 1 m of progress to the gem centre (p.y + 1.75 m), for route samples within 60 m, against every visible, mostly opaque mesh built in the headless level. (2) *Course frame*: for route samples within 40 m with the crystal ahead of the travel direction, the camera sits camHeight above the walked floor and camDist behind along the route direction, pitched camPitch down with vertical field of view camFov and a 16:9 frame (the level's `cameraRig`, else the TUNING defaults: 5.05/5.1/25.35°/49°), exactly the course rig of `src/main.ts` outside side-scroll and boulder shots with the rider on the ground. The crystal centre must be inside the frustum (the frame top is at elevation camFov/2 − camPitch from the camera, so the centre is in frame only when p.y + 1.75 ≤ floor + camHeight + (camDist + d)·tan(camFov/2 − camPitch), d along the view) and unoccluded from the camera. (3) When a catchable grind line passes through the envelope, the same frame test from points of that line within 40 m, travelling toward the crystal, with the rig anchored to the grinding rider. (4) Views from checkpoints and the gate within 60 m.

## Limitations

- The route line is the camera lane (or a checkpoint chain) and the floor comes from the geometry audit's walker, not a recorded player path. The walker can follow a lower path or lose the course; levels where its walk does not end on the gate floor are flagged (`route.walkSuspect`), and their route-based floors and visibility are unreliable. Checkpoint and gate floors are added as starts so reach does not depend on the walk alone.
- Reach is a geometric search with measured apexes and ranges, not a simulation: run-up length, approach speed, air control, steering into a ledge and timing are not modelled; jump arcs are not tested for obstructions; ledge grabs are allowed on any non-crate surface lip; a 0.5 m probe grid can miss beams narrower than that. "Unclear" means nothing was found, not proven unreachable.
- Vert numbers were measured on a 3.6 m transition; other radii behave differently. Half or quarter pipe is read from the vertramp component when one is found (default half). Kicker airs come from 4 m ramps at three rises; other shapes are interpolated. Wallrides assume a flat approach floor in front of the wall; the wallie's travel along the wall is not modelled and the off-grid test reads the visible wall face.
- The state is the level at build time: movers at their base phase (their whole travel is swept, without timing), crumbles and phase pads solid, spin bridges closed, switches unpressed except in the switch pass. Trampoline air jumps and board double jumps are not included.
- Visibility uses the meshes built synchronously in the headless level; art that streams in later (GLB props, some dressing) is missing, so line of sight can be optimistic. Fog is not applied (the level's fog far distance is recorded). The camera model ignores camera collision, lane look-ahead, side-scroll zones, boulder shots and the speed field-of-view push, and assumes a 16:9 frame.
- The rider box is the default character's (0.936 m); Character Lab stature changes the band floor. Bonus rooms, races, competitions and parks are outside the scope (see Skipped); add a level with `--levels id1,id2`.
