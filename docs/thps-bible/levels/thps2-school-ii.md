# THPS2 — School II (Southern California (a composite of real San Diego-area school spots: Rancho Buena Vista crest, the Carlsbad gap and 11-set))

THPS2's second career level and its first big street level: a Southern California high school of nine districts around courtyards, with a roof layer and two hidden districts. The secret tape hangs in the gap beyond the library roof, between the East Wing's flagpoles, on the doorway into Carlsbad. Reaching it takes a pumped run across the courtyard stage and a planter launch onto a two-storey roof. The gym is a secret area opened by a timed event: grind the Opunsezmee rail while the school bell rings at the start of a run. Evidence: the VG render, which carries all labels plus a note on the gym rule; four LP2 runs (11:39–18:17) with 1-s sampling of the tape line; the RPGClassics shrine (PS1); and Push Square, Halfglass, Thumbsticks, Screenrant, Gamertweak, a Steam thread and LP12 for the remake.

**Vertical layers (heights are estimates)**

- Ground: courts, table court, hall, lower courtyard; Carlsbad sits lower, behind a wall.
- Low: planters, benches, tables, barriers, the courtyard stage (≈ 0.5–1 m).
- Terrace and stairs: the start terrace ≈ 2–3 m above the table court (Leap Of Faith!!!, Nightmare rail); library stairs (Opunsezmee rail).
- Awnings: covered walkways at ≈ 3 m, reached by wallride + wallie or a small ramp.
- One-storey roofs: portables (≈ 3 m) and centre roofs (≈ 4 m).
- Two-storey roofs: library and East Wing (≈ 6–8 m); flagpoles project from the East Wing wall below the roofline.
- Interior: gym court and pool, with low, mid and high diving boards.

**Vert and street areas**

- Vert: courtyard stage quarter pipe (K), wall quarter pipes in the lower courtyard (T, E, Huge Transfer pair), arch quarter pipe at the start, gym court quarter pipes and pool, Carlsbad quarter pipe.
- Street: everything else; this is a street level dressed as a school.

**Secret areas and how they open**

- Gym (Z7): timed rail trigger (Opunsezmee rail while the bell rings; VG says within 19 s).
- Carlsbad (Z5): geometric, by crossing from the library roof toward the East Wing, which is the tape line.

**Loops**

- Central loop: start → hall → courtyard → library stairs → table court → back up the Nightmare stairs.
- Lower loop: gym front → lower courtyard → portables → back along the gym.
- Roof spurs: start roofs → centre roofs → TC's roofs; library roof → East Wing → Carlsbad → over the wall → lower courtyard (a one-way loop back to the ground).

Landmarks: Rancho Buena Vista crest wall with the big wooden quarter pipe; figure-eight courtyard stage; library façade with blue doors and planter; East Wing with angled flagpoles; gym (Neversoft High court, diving pool); TC's portables; Bendy's dumpster curb; Carlsbad 11-set and grass gap.

**Route (original PS1, observed in LP2 run 3, 16:59–17:20; LP2 reaches the courtyard via the hall and the south awning, picking up the $100 at 16:55):**

1. **vert-air** — courtyard stage → the large wooden quarter pipe in front of the Rancho Buena Vista crest building (the K ramp). Two airs to pump speed and fill the special bar: FS shove-it, then 360 FS shove-it + stalefish (LP2 17:00–17:07, timer 1:09–1:02). Shrine: use the quarter pipe with the K to gain speed
2. **ride** — quarter pipe → west end of the stage. Straight across the stage over the faint tire treads, which point at the library (shrine; LP2 17:08–17:10). The run-up is ≈ 35–45 m (VG)
3. **kicker** — low planter at the base of the library façade (two shrubs between blue doors) → library roof. The planter edge works as a launcher: airborne at door-top height in front of the façade (LP2 17:11–17:12), landing on the roof for 2 Da Roof!!! (750) plus a $50 (LP2 17:13). Height gain ≈ 6–8 m
4. **ride** — roof landing → far roof edge (raised block). ≈ 10–15 m; the tape appears over the gap ahead (LP2 17:14)
5. **ollie** — roof edge → tape. Launch into the gap: 'Find the secret tape' with the Big Drop icon at timer 0:54 (LP2 17:15)
6. **grind (exit)** — air → an angled flagpole on the East Wing wall. Flyin' The Flag! + FS 50-50 (LP2 17:16), then down into Carlsbad (LP2 17:17–17:20). Clearing the gap onto the flag roof instead scores Mad Skeelz Roof Gap!!! (1,000; PS12)

**Alternative approach described for THPS 1+2 (Thumbsticks, Screenrant; not seen in LP2):**

1. From the start, go right down the hall into the courtyard (Thumbsticks).
2. With a full special meter, boost off the leftmost planter ramp onto the awning (Thumbsticks; Screenrant mentions grinding a landscaping box for speed).
3. Wallride into a grind up to the library roof ledge, then stop (Thumbsticks; Screenrant).
4. Back up for run-up and ride off the roof's ramp across the gap to the tape at the East Wing (Thumbsticks; Screenrant).

## Guidance and sightlines

- Hall passes are big orange signs on posts readable from across a court (LP2 11:46, 11:52); the shrine calls them 'big and obvious'.
- Bells sit on walls at wallride height, each beside a small ramp or flat run-up; the first is 5 s from spawn.
- The letter order S→K→A→T→E follows one continuous path through the hall, stage, library stairs and lower courtyard (shrine).
- The courtyard's crest wall and big quarter pipe are the K landmark; faint tire treads on the stage point toward the library and its planter, a built-in arrow for the tape line (shrine).
- The tape is deliberately hidden from the ground: it appears only after the player is on the library roof (LP2 17:14).
- Carlsbad is walled off and seen from the roofs as a green lower district (LP2 17:17); its exit ramp is visible from the lower courtyard as a ramp over the wall.
- The gym's closed blue doors are shown to everyone collecting pass 3. The only in-game clue to opening them is the bell ringing at the run's start; the rule itself comes from guides (the VG annotation).

## Design logic

1. Letters teach the route and the gym tests it: the S→K→A order runs from spawn to the Opunsezmee rail. Skated within ≈ 19 s, while the bell rings, that same line opens the gym.
2. The tape is the entrance exam to Carlsbad: getting it and discovering the level's last district are one action, and a miss drops you into that district anyway (exploration as consolation).
3. The tape line reuses the main court's furniture: the letter K's quarter pipe for speed, the stage for a straight run-up (tire treads as guide), the planter as a kicker. Nothing new is built for it; the target is simply higher.
4. Two commitments in sequence: the launch (2 Da Roof!!!) is the hard part but fails cheaply; the roof-edge gap is easier but fails expensively (Carlsbad). Risk is pushed after the skill check.
5. Roofs are a second reading of the school: a low roof chain from the start (Crazy and Suicidal Roof Gaps) and a high pair (library, East Wing). The tape uses the highest pair; the TC's Roof kickflip goal teaches roof gaps first on ≈ 3 m portables.
6. Wallride is taught by goal: five bells, the first beside spawn. THPS2's new move becomes navigation for the awning route (LP2 16:48–16:49).
7. Goal objects tour the districts: hall passes cover start, table court, gym front and lower courtyard (twice); bells cover start, centre, two far corners and the tape's flagpole area; roll-call rails cover three courts.
8. The secret gym is timing-gated and optional: failing it costs a whole new run because the window is at the run's start. It holds no goal or cash in the original, only the level's biggest lip gaps (High Dive Extension 5,000).
9. Cash forces exploration: Carlsbad holds $150 of the $750, so 100% requires the tape line or a roof crossing; LP2's final cash comes off the centre roofs (18:04–18:16).
10. Size and the 2:00 limit force run planning: LP2 needs four runs (scores, letters, bells and passes; TC's gap and rails; tape; leftover cash), against one run for the Hangar.
11. Spawn is a decision point with three exits (hall, stairs/drop, roofs), each starting a different line within 5 s.
12. The tape is placed over a big drop: THPS2 shows its Big Drop warning at the pickup (LP2 17:15), so the reward moment is also the moment of greatest exposure.

## Original vs remake

- Scores: Pro 40,000 → 70,000, Sick 100,000 → 125,000; a High Combo goal (10,000) is added (LP2 checklist vs PS12).
- Tape: PS12 describes the original line (raised quarter pipe for speed, straight at the library, planter launch, tape between the flags). Thumbsticks and Screenrant (1+2) describe another: boost off a planter ramp onto the awning, wallride/grind up to the library roof, stop, then ride a ramp off the roof to the tape. Halfglass places it between the library and the East Wing. The remake keeps the location and supports a slower, more controllable approach via the awnings (inference from the texts).
- LP12 shows an EAST WING sign over a door (0:46:28), naming the building the tape gap faces.
- Gym: 1+2 still opens it by grinding the Opunsezmee rail as the bell rings, and it stays unlocked afterwards (Gamertweak; the THPS HD Steam thread reports the same for HD). LP12's last School II run is inside the pool (0:48:28).
- 1+2 adds collectibles inside the formerly empty secret: stat points above the gym pool and on its wall ledge, and the Vicarious Visions logo above the gym hoop. The secret gym gains collectible value in the remake.
- 1+2 adds an alien plushie on the courtyard awning and several roof-top stat points, including one reached by jumping from the library roof across to the next roof.
- Gap list: VG's 'Are You Serious?!!' (AC-unit roof) is absent from PS12's 1+2 list; all other VG names appear in PS12.

## Transfer to this game

1. Roof-doorway crystal: hang the crystal ≈ 0.5–1 m above roof level in the gap between two tall roofs that lead into a walled bonus district. The approach is a quarter-pipe pump (two vert airs), a straight 35–45 m run (a speed pad can stand in for THPS2's special), a launch onto the first roof, a 10–15 m roll, then an ollie off the edge. This game's full ollie (≈ 9.4 m long at cruise, ≈ 13.8 m at charge) can cover a 6–10 m gap.
2. Height budget: THPS2 jumps ≈ 6–8 m from a planter. Here, put a vert quarter pipe or kicker ramp against the façade (or a trampoline disguised as a planter) to reach the first roof; do not rely on a flat ollie (1.78 m).
3. Angled flagpoles as exit rails: two or three trick rails jutting from the far wall below the crystal give a grind catch for a short jump, and the grind dumps the player into the bonus district.
4. Timed bell gate: a trick gate that opens only if a named rail is ground during a window early in the run, announced by an audio cue (the bell). Mark the rail with a pickup so players ride it anyway.
5. Breadcrumbs that become a speed test: place 3 pickups along the route from spawn to that rail (hall ledge, stage quarter pipe, stair rail) so collecting them teaches the line the timed gate later demands.
6. Wallride targets as teachers: put the first wallride target within 5 s of spawn, then use wallride-plus-wallie onto an awning as a real route to the crystal's roof.
7. Asymmetric failure: make the launch the hard part with a short retry (≈ 10 s) and the final gap the easy part with a long consequence (dropped into the bonus district), so failure still shows new space.
