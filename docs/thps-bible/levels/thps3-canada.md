Layers, low to high: stream bed and corral floor; lots and park deck (with pools sunk below); the cabin roof
and lower walkways (≈ 3–5 m); upper walkways and ropes in the trees (≈ 5–10 m); the raised loader and the
lookout tower (≈ 8–15 m); the mountain track (≈ 15–25 m above the forest floor). Heights are estimates;
low confidence. Below the cliffs is fog: G3 p057 (Saved by the Generator) mentions the skater
"plunges into the fog".

Loops: the lots and the park form a ring; the forest walkways loop round the Horn and back to the start
blade; the mountain is a one-way descent: up through the loader and tower, down along the track (to the tape,
or to the Bully's cabin and the power lines back to the parking lot).

Route (designed, G3 p045; observed in LP3 0:14:08–0:15:25, second attempt from 0:14:58):

1. **From the spawn, turn round and charge the start ramp the other way, leaping onto the wooden walkway
   beyond it** (kicker; Climb the Tree 100, then the short Tree Air Gap 50). LP3 0:14:58–0:15:03. Sidebar
   "Raise the Roof" (G3 p045): or skate up the large snowbank beside the cabin, ollie onto the walkway and
   grind the log at once.
2. **Take the walkway that goes through the hole in the tree, away from the parking lot** (G3 p045). LP3
   0:14:21 passes the wooden arch ("Tree Air Gap", hidden combo).
3. **Bear right at the fork and grind the log that extends off the walkway to the left; hold it down onto the
   lower walkway** (grind). LP3 0:14:25–0:14:26 (BS 50-50 on a log).
4. **Grind the rope in front of the generator on the left** (grind → unlock). The log loader in the distance
   rises and opens into a large elevated vert ramp. LP3 0:14:30–0:14:32 grinds past the red generator box. J3:
   "a red metal box with a lever on the left; grind it so the half pipe rises"; PS34: "grind the rail to
   activate it".
5. **Charge the banked wall and alley-oop into the log loader; land close to its near edge** (bank,
   vert-air; Load and Go 250). LP3 0:15:15–0:15:17 ("Cannonball + Load and Go").
6. **Transfer into the raised half pipe on the lookout tower** (transfer; Smooth as Silk 100 = loader →
   tower coping). LP3 0:15:17–0:15:18 (fastplant).
7. **Line up and ollie off the ramp to the covered half pipe above** (ollie). LP3 0:15:18–0:15:19
   ("Fastplant + Double Impossible", hidden combo).
8. **Line up straight and jump to the large ramp leading upward** (transfer; Pickaxe Sluice 50). LP3
   0:15:19–0:15:22. This is where LP3's first attempt failed (0:14:44–0:14:46).
9. **At the top of the ramp, grind the right-hand rail of the mine track downhill to the right** (grind).
   LP3 0:15:23 (FS boardslide). The left-hand rail leads instead to the Mine Cart Ride.
10. **The rail splits off towards the tape just ahead**: "SECRET TAPE! COMPLETE" at LP3 0:15:25 (timer 0:48).
11. **Exit**: grind on past the tape and leap for the walkway below, landing in a grind on the rope (Mountain
    Man Mine Stomp 250), which runs into Look Ma! No Talent! and Around the Horn (LP3 0:15:26–0:15:28; TAS3
    1:31–1:34).

Route (TAS3 1:22–1:30, 8 s, no generator):

1. Grind the fence by the start, heelflip, tailslide (1:22–1:23).
2. Up the snowbank onto the cabin roof (1:23–1:24).
3. From the roof, leap onto a cable that runs up the cliff face (1:24–1:25; "Kissed the Rail", 360 melon).
4. Grind the cable up the cliff, with a special grind (layback FS feeble) to keep speed (1:25–1:29).
5. Drop onto the mine track; the tape is collected at 1:30 (timer 1:52). Then Mountain Man Mine Stomp → Look
   Ma! No Talent! → Around the Horn (1:31–1:34).

No guide documents this cable; it may be the power line that the Mine Cart Launch gap uses in the other
direction (G3 p052), ridden uphill. Remake text: PCGamesN gives the cabin roof → walkway → switch "to the left"
of the half pipe → platform above → "grind the rightmost rail"; PS34: "follow the same path as for the SKATE
letters ... lever on the left ... elevated half pipe ... series of platforms up to a track".

### C-O-M-B-O letters

None in the original (G3 p042 and VG goal lists; J3); the goal type belongs to THPS4 (G4m p002, p004). The
2025 remake adds it as a pro goal (PS34): at the back of the level by the raised quarter pipe (the loader),
grind the rail (C); round the corner, jump to the next rail (O); jump onto the walkway (M); manual across and
jump off the left side (B); manual up the ramp (O). The line links grinds by hops and the last letters by
manuals, so it must flow as one combo, and it starts where the tape's climb starts.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The letters walk the route.** The G3 set (remake: the only set) runs fence → cabin roof → branch → log →
  loader, the first half of the tape route; the A sits on the branch directly below the tape (VG PS2).
- **Cause and effect in view.** The generator's effect is seen "in the distance": the loader rises and opens
  (G3 p045). The switch is a red box with a lever (J3).
- **The climb is a visible staircase.** Loader, tower, covered half pipe and ramp stand one above the other at
  the far end of the forest; each next ramp is in view from the last (G3: "line up and ollie", "line up
  straight for the next jump").
- **Pickups reward the walkways.** Stat points on the Horn rope and on the generator rope back towards the
  park bring players to the generator before they know what it does (G3 p048).
- **Hidden on purpose:** the tape is behind the spawn and above the treetops; the cable up the cliff is not
  signposted (TAS only).

## Design logic

1. **First switch-gated tape.** Canada (career 2) adds an unlock; the switch lies on the walkway everyone uses,
   and its effect is visible from it.
2. **A staircase of transfers.** Banked wall → loader → tower half pipe → covered half pipe → ramp → rail: each
   step is a vert transfer upward, so the climb converts vert skill into height.
3. **Little overlap, high stakes.** G3 warns there is "not much overlap" between loader and tower; the jumps are
   tuned to demand line-up and speed, and a miss falls 10–20 m.
4. **State persists, so failure is affordable.** The loader stays raised; LP3's failed first ascent cost ≈ 29 s
   and the second attempt still fitted in the run.
5. **The letters are the rehearsal.** The G3 set covers the route up to the climb; the remake keeps only that
   set and says "follow the same path as for the SKATE letters".
6. **Tape at a fork, not a dead end.** Right rail → tape → exit onto the Around the Horn rope (a goal); left
   rail → the level's longest grind (Mine Cart Ride, 500) and a launch back to the lot.
7. **Exit into a goal.** Both the TAS and LP3 chain the tape into Mountain Man Mine Stomp → Look Ma! No
   Talent! → Around the Horn: the tape's descent is the start of the treetop scoring line.
8. **Behind the spawn.** The spawn faces the lot; the tape lies behind and above, so it is found by turning
   round, the opposite of Foundry's tape over the middle.
9. **Three areas, three letter tours.** Each skater group's letters tour one area (lot, park, forest), so the
   level is learnt area by area across the cast.
10. **Expert bypass.** The TAS climbs a cable from the cabin roof up the cliff to the track in 8 s, skipping
    the switch: lines (ropes, cables) connect the layers for those who see them.
11. **Difficulty step from Foundry.** Foundry: no unlock, two leaps, ≈ 6–9 m. Canada: unlock + four transfers,
    ≈ 15–25 m, a rail choice.
12. **The PS1 edition keeps the idea.** A smaller, different layout still puts the tape at the top of a
    mine-cart track up the mountain (VG PS1).
13. **The remake extends the branches.** Cash and the Iron Galaxy logo on the left branch, COMBO at the foot of
    the climb, a man waiting by the raised quarter pipe (thermos goal): the climb becomes a hub.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Switch-raised ramp.** A trick rail or lever on the walkway route that raises a quarter-pipe platform
   (moving platform) 4–6 m, visible from the switch; it stays up for the run.
2. **Staircase of vert transfers.** Three vert ramps at rising heights (each ≈ 3–4 m higher), linked by
   transfers/spine transfers; keep each landing deck ≥ 3 m deep on the near side so line-up, not luck, decides.
3. **Crystal at a rail fork.** At the top, a grindable track that splits: right branch carries the crystal,
   left branch is a long descending grind (travelling-rail feel) back towards the start.
4. **Exit into a line.** After the crystal, a short ollie from the rail's end onto a rope/rail circling a big
   tree, so the crystal run flows into a scoring line.
5. **Letters as rehearsal.** Put the level's lesser pickups along the lower half of the crystal route (fence,
   roof, branch, log, ramp).
6. **Expert cable.** A long grindable cable (snapping rope or travelling rail) from a roof near the spawn up a
   cliff to the track: fast for those who see it, risky (a fall ≥ 10 m).
7. **Keep walkers off.** The track ≥ 15 m above the forest floor with no standable ledge within the 4.6 m
   double-jump + ledge-grab reach.
