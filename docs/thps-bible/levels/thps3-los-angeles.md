Layers, low to high: tunnels and the subterranean half pipe (below the street, opened by They're Grate!); street and plazas; ledges, benches, fountain lip, handrails (≈ 0.5–1 m); quarter pipes, half pipes, the carwash walls; awnings and the fire-truck ladder; low roofs (lobby roof with its half pipe, carwash roof, pink building); the freeway deck (≈ 6–10 m, estimate); the theatre-block roofs and vertical sign; the purple tower top with the tape; cables strung between these at several heights. All heights are estimates; no frame gave a measurable reference.

Circulation: the streets form loops round the library block (the quake lap, the Ill Scoring loop). Cables and wires form a second network across the streets. After the quake the freeway adds an elevated spine from the carwash to the library end, connected to the roofs of the theatre block.

## Spawn and first view

Spawn is at the top of the curvy white stairs by the library (G3 p117 "the curvy white rails at the starting point"; TAS3 5:31-5:33 opens with a grind down the start stairs). Behind the start is a short banked wall / QP used for special-meter tricks (G3 p116, p122). In front, a cable rises from the concrete walkway at the top of the grassy hill to the library roof (G3 p122; J3 p023 "le filin en face de vous au départ"). To the left, the rails behind the library run down the hill (Eastern rail); down the stairs to the right is the Northern rail toward the fountain (G3 p120).

Visible or one move away: the Northern Tremor rail with the S for Hawk's group, the fountain across the street with that group's K, and the freeway on the skyline. The opening invites a rail line down the stairs, and with it the first quake rail. The tape is not reported visible from spawn; it is first seen from the purple area below it.

**Route (designed line):**

1. **unlock** — from four Quake rails round the library (Z1) to earthquake. Precondition: "The Start the Earthquake goal must be completed before attempting the Secret Tape" (G3 p119). Grind the Northern, Southern, Eastern and Western Quake rails; the freeway breaks up.
2. **wire** — from top of the grassy hill / concrete walkway by the start to library roof. Gain speed on the banked wall or QP behind the start, then grind the cable up to the roof; "ollie into short grinds should momentum fail" (G3 p122; J3 p023).
3. **ollie** — from library roof to broken freeway deck. Jump from the roof onto the freeway section (J3 p023 "sautez sur le tronçon d'autoroute"; LP3 0:57:14). Alternative: from the carwash roof onto the tipped slab (G3 p121; remake spine transfer, PS34).
4. **kicker** — from plywood sheets at the library end of the freeway to office building with the green ledge. Overpass Leap (200): launch off the plywood ramp; land on the roof or grind its edge to get onto the flat (G3 p119, p128; J3 p023; LP3 0:57:16-0:57:18 "Fastplant + Overpass Leap").
5. **ledge** — from office roof to edge of the red building. Leap into a grind on the red building's edge (G3 p119); LP3 0:57:20-0:57:22 shows the terracotta roof ridge.
6. **grind** — from red building edge to Busters Cherry sign. Hold the grind through the bend onto the theatre's vertical sign: Don't Fall!!! (200) when entered from the first building after the freeway (G3 p119, p128).
7. **rail-transfer** — from Busters Cherry sign to black pipe on the purple tower. Leap across the street gap to the pipe and hold the grind: Bustin' Cherries! (100) → tape (G3 p119, p128; LP3 0:57:24; LP34 0:48:06-0:48:08).
8. **drop** — from pipe to purple deck / half pipe below. Drop off to safety (G3 p119).

Observed vs inferred. The precondition, the route and the pickup point are printed in G3 p119 and J3 p023-p024, and observed in LP3 (0:57:12 brick-roof nosegrind and landing on the library roof; 0:57:16-0:57:18 "Fastplant + Overpass Leap" onto a green roof; 0:57:20-0:57:22 terracotta roof ridge; 0:57:24 "Bustin' Cherries! + FS 50-50"; 0:57:26 a bail on the drop) and in LP34 (0:47:58 plywood kicker, 0:48:00-0:48:06 roof and sign grinds, 0:48:08 "Complete! Get the secret tape"). The precise roof order between the freeway kicker and the sign is from G3; the heights are estimates.

How the earthquake reshapes routes. Before the quake no G3 route or gap touches the freeway (inferred: out of reach), and the carwash roof is a closed line of its own (To Get to the Other Side, Baby!). After the quake: (1) the freeway is broken into slabs that act as ramps from the carwash roof; (2) the deck becomes a corridor from the carwash to the library end, with two breaks to grind across (Overpass Aftershock); (3) a car hangs at a break, which turns the car chase into a grind goal (Bye-Bye); (4) the plywood kicker at the library end becomes the launch to the theatre-block roofs and the tape; (5) the pre-quake carwash-roof gap is gone. The state persists into the next run (LP3). They're Grate! and Free Ballin' are smaller events of the same kind: one opens an underground half pipe and roof QPs, the other turns two props into a rolling hazard.

## Guidance and sightlines

- The library is the hub and the start; the four quake rails are on its four sides and are labelled ("appropriately labeled 'Quake' rails", G3 p120). The trigger is signposted geometry, not a hidden switch.
- The freeway is on the skyline from the plaza (TAS3 6:03 shows it behind the start stairs). After the quake the broken deck reads as a road that leads somewhere, and its plywood kicker points at the roofs (inferred from LP3 0:57:16 and LP34 0:47:58).
- The tape is shown from below: on a pillar above the purple half pipe, "way out of reach from a ground attack" (G3 p119). The player sees it from a place they visit for other goals (Free Ballin', purple transfers, a letter), and the solution is on the other side of the street.
- Cables crossing streets are visible lines that are also routes: the start cable to the library roof (tape route, three stat points), the purple-pipe cable (Wire Ridin', decks), power lines (Electrified!).
- Landmark props double as ramps: the fire-truck ladder is an inclined rail to roof level; the carwash's banked walls and white sign stack a vert spot onto a street corner.
- The theatre's vertical Busters Cherry sign is both a landmark and the last grind before the tape.
- Hidden on purpose: the underground half pipe and the library-roof QPs (behind They're Grate!), the window-washer scaffolds (above the plaza), the yellow house's interior holes.
- Remake: the pro goals and collectibles lean on the same infrastructure (seagulls on the freeway-to-roof leap, COMBO on the tower line, cash and deck on rooftops reached by wallrides).

## Design logic

1. The tape is gated by the level's set piece: the earthquake turns the freeway from scenery into a route, and the tape route runs over it. The player must cause the event and then use what it leaves (G3 p119; J3 p023).
2. The trigger teaches the loop: Start the Earthquake needs one rail on each side of the library, so the first goal walks the player round the central block, the level's main circulation (G3 p120; TAS3 5:36-5:46).
3. An event that adds and removes: the quake opens the freeway and the roofs but deletes the pre-quake carwash-roof gap. The order of play matters (G3 p127).
4. Persistence makes a two-run plan: the broken freeway stays broken in later runs. The human player starts the quake in run 2 and goes straight up the roofs in run 3, reaching the tape ≈ 14 s after the run starts (LP3 0:55:20, 0:57:10-0:57:24).
5. Every link of the tape route is a named gap (Overpass Leap, Don't Fall!!!, Bustin' Cherries!). The gap list confirms each step, and the score rewards the same chain.
6. Seen from below, solved from above: the tape is set where it cannot be reached from the purple half pipe, so the player has to plan an approach from the roofs across the street.
7. Three skill bands for one pickup: the designed rooftop chain; an alternative freeway access from the carwash roof (G3 p121; J3 p024); and an expert climb straight up the purple tower with boneless, wallride and wallie (TAS3 6:04-6:07).
8. Shared climb: the start cable to the library roof carries the tape route, three stat points (G3 p122) and, in the remake, the car-chase approach (hardcoregamer). One piece of geometry teaches the climb.
9. A stat point on the tape route (Glifberg group, "between the first roof and the Busters Cherry sign", G3 p122) rewards scouting the roofs before the tape is attempted.
10. Moving hazards are part of the event chain: lowriders and the van chase fill the streets until the post-quake geometry is used to stop the chase; the freed balls add a rolling hazard to the purple area (G3 p116, p120-p121).
11. Skater groups tour different districts: Hawk's letters cover the library, fountain, purple alley and hotel; Caballero's the back rails, theatre, fire truck and carwash; Campbell's go inside the library, up the towers, along the kiosk cable and onto the yellow house (G3 p118).
12. Failure is expensive in height, cheap in distance: a miss on the roofs means a long fall, but the start cable is close to spawn, so a retry costs ≈ 15–25 s.

## Original vs remake

THPS 3+4 keeps the layout, the earthquake and the tape. The PS1 edition is a different level built for the older hardware.

- Tape unchanged in place and logic: still needs the earthquake; the remake text uses the carwash access ("spine transfer onto the highway ... ramp up onto a rooftop ... jump to the next rooftop ... grind the end and jump to the rail sticking out") (PS34). LP34 shows the same chain: freeway plywood kicker → roof → building edge → theatre sign → purple tower pipe → tape (0:47:58-0:48:08). The theatre's vertical sign appears to carry different lettering in the remake (low confidence from a 480-px frame).
- Quake trigger unchanged (four rails round the main building); the cutscene now shows a panicking driver, then a car left hanging, and the remake adds a shop notification (LP34 0:46:48-0:47:02).
- Letters: one fixed set — S above a red rail to the right of the start, K on the fountain edge, A up the stairs above a rail, T on a purple structure by wallride + wallie, E between two QPs inside an archway (transfer) (PS34). Four of five follow the original Hawk group; E is new.
- Pro goals add COMBO letters on the tower line, a Monty/Smith grind on Wire Ridin', a Passin' Gas wallride and five seagull groups, one of them on the freeway-to-roof leap (PS34).
- Collectibles added: cash ×4, hidden deck (Yeti shopfront → roof → wire to the theatre roof), Iron Galaxy logo (library roof area opened by They're Grate!), panda (inside the grate pit), stat points ×4 (PS34).
- Score thresholds unchanged (100k/190k/400k) plus High Combo 40,000; the longplay's runs start at 10:00 (PS34; LP34 0:44:20).
- PS1 edition (VG) is another level: goals are Transformers Shutdown, Grind the Electric Rail, Elevator Grind, Kickflip over the Elevator Lobby; office towers with an elevator; the freeway is already drawn broken (no earthquake) and the tape icon is on the purple tower beside it, next to the K. ## Transfer to this game

1. Event-gated crystal: four switch-rails, one on each side of a central block, must be ground in one run. Grinding all four collapses an elevated bridge into tilted slabs (kickers) and a broken deck. The crystal sits on a short rail sticking out of a tower across the street, reached only from the collapsed deck via a roof chain.
2. Roof chain from the broken deck: plank kicker (full ollie ≈ 1.8 m high, ≈ 9–14 m long) → flat roof → grind a roof edge → hold the grind round a bend onto a vertical sign → grind-ollie (≈ 2.4 m pop) across a street-width gap onto the tower rail. Name each link (sign or sound cue) so the player gets feedback per step.
3. Seen from below: put the crystal directly above a small vert park the player must use for another objective, high enough that vert air (and double jump on foot, ≈ 4.6 m) cannot reach it, so the player looks for another way up.
4. Expert bypass: allow wallride up the tower face + wallie into the crystal rail, at full speed only. It rewards mastery without replacing the designed chain.
5. Persistence through checkpoints: once the bridge collapses, keep it collapsed after respawn, so a failed roof chain only costs the climb back via a cable (rope or rail) near the start.
6. Add-and-remove: a rooftop line that only works before the collapse (e.g. a manual across an intact roof) makes the trigger a choice.
