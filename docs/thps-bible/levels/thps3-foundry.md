Layers, low to high: half-pipe and molten-tub floors; the main floor; ledges, tank rims and booth roofs
(≈ 1–3 m); catwalks (≈ 3–5 m); the balcony (≈ 4–6 m); the suspended platforms, the tape beam and the
glass observation box (≈ 6–9 m); the ductwork (highest). Heights are estimates against the skater and
the vert ramps; low confidence.

Loops: the floor circulates round the half pipe and the machines and returns along the curved guardrail
at the back (Round the Bend). The upper layer is a second, one-way-ish loop: catwalk from the start →
observation box → beam → platforms → balcony → down at the back (J3 p011 describes it from the beam
side: the beam leads to "two platforms", which lead to "a raised zone at the back").

Route (designed main route, G3 p031; observed in LP3 0:08:03–0:08:17):

1. **Cross to the Molten Operations area at the back and trick off the banked walls flanking the control
   room** (bank, vert-air) until the Special Meter is full (G3 p031). The balcony quarter pipe also hides
   a stat point (G3 p034) and a deck (G3 p035), so players come here before they know about the tape.
2. **Sky off the banked wall into a grind on the ledge high above** (vert-air → grind). The named version
   is Porch Rail Tap (500): "transfer from the quarter pipe near the Molten Operations office into a grind on
   the balcony above" (G3 p039). LP3 is on the balcony's yellow rail at 0:08:06–0:08:08.
3. **Pick up speed off the quarter pipe against the wall on the balcony** (vert-air, G3 p031).
4. **Leap off the balcony's edge onto either suspended platform** (ollie; gap CG's SKDK 2 STFK, 250). LP3
   bails on landing (0:08:09–0:08:12) but is on the slab at 0:08:14.
5. **Leap towards the centre of the room and land in a grind on the beam** (ollie → grind; Walkin' a Thin
   Line!, 500). LP3 rolls onto the beam at 0:08:15.
6. **Hold the grind to the tape** ("SECRET TAPE! COMPLETE", LP3 0:08:17, timer 0:13).
7. **Exit**: ride on along the beam, or leap from a platform to the ductwork above the cooling tank (Don't
   Look Down!, 500, and a stat point, G3 p034).

Route (alternate, G3 p031 sidebar "Use the Back Door"; J3 p009; PS34; WEB pcgamesn):

1. **From the spawn, take the kicker or ledge beside the start onto the catwalk** (kicker; J3: "use the
   ledges on the right or left for impulse and land on the suspended walkway with yellow rails"; PS34: "use
   the short ramp").
2. **Follow the catwalk (right-hand path, J3) to the glass-encased observation area** (grind or roll).
3. **Stop in the centre of the platform, facing the beam** (G3: "pause"; J3: "position yourself in the axis of
   the beam").
4. **Break the glass and ollie off the small ramp into a grind on the beam** (break-glass, ollie, grind;
   Walkin' A Thin Line). PS34: "jump through the glass to get onto the rafters".
5. **Hold the grind; the tape is just ahead.**

Route (TAS3 0:46–0:53, fastest):

1. Special grind along the back wall rail (layback BS smith), wallie off the wall unjamming a valve on the
   way (0:46–0:47).
2. FS noseslide along the curved yellow guardrail round the back (0:48–0:49).
3. Boneless off it over the molten tank (0:49) into CG's SKDK 2 STFK: a special grind on the platform's
   edge (0:50–0:51). The balcony is skipped.
4. Leap from the platform towards the centre; the tape is collected in mid-air (0:52, timer 1:48), without
   grinding the beam. The TAS lands by the press, next to letter A (0:53).

### C-O-M-B-O letters

None in the original. The PS2 goal list (G3 p028), the French walkthrough (J3 p009–p010) and the PS1 goal
list (VG) have no C-O-M-B-O goal; the goal type appears in THPS4 (G4m p002, p004). The 2025 remake adds it as a
pro goal (PS34): from the start, head right onto the raised catwalk; grind the yellow rails for C, O and M;
jump off the end of the catwalk and grind again for B; jump off at the end and grind the rail ahead for the
last O. The whole word lies on the back-door catwalk, the same structure as the easy tape route, so the
remake turns the tape's approach into a single scored line.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The catwalk starts at the spawn.** Kickers beside the start deck put the catwalk's yellow rails right
  in front of the player; following them leads to the observation box and the beam (G3 p034 stat point,
  p031 sidebar).
- **The beam hangs over the busy middle.** The tape is over the press and cooling tank, the zones every
  floor line crosses, so it is seen from below many times before it is reached (inferred from its position).
- **Pickups seed the hard route.** A stat point at the balcony quarter pipe, another on the ductwork, and a
  deck on the balcony handrail give reasons to climb the back of the hall before the tape route is known
  (G3 p034–p035).
- **The guide's sidebar says it outright.** "Bette Would Dig It" tells players to spend early stat points on
  Air, Hang Time and Speed for the tape (G3 p031): the first tape doubles as the stat-point tutorial.
- **Hidden on purpose:** the observation box's link to the beam (a small ramp behind glass) and the
  platforms' link to the beam are only obvious once you stand on the upper layer.

## Design logic

1. **One tape, two ends.** The tape sits mid-way along an upper loop; the back end needs meter, a sky and two
   leaps, the front end needs only exploration and a stop-and-ollie. The guide prints the hard way first
   and the easy way as a sidebar; the remake's guides print only the easy way (PS34, pcgamesn).
2. **The easy end starts at the spawn.** The catwalk kicker is one of the three choices the player faces at
   the start, so a curious first run can reach the observation box in about 11 s (G3 shot at 1:49) and the tape a few seconds later.
3. **The hard end tests THPS3's air tools.** Fill the Special Meter, sky off a banked wall into a grind,
   speed from a quarter pipe, leap, leap. It is the career's first lesson in "go up first, then across".
4. **Above the main line, not beside it.** The beam is over the press and the cooling tank, where the floor
   lines and two letters per group pass; the tape is visible from where it cannot be reached.
5. **Pickups pre-teach the climb.** Stat points and a deck on the balcony and ductwork reward the first
   visit to the upper layer (G3 p034–p035).
6. **The tape needs no unlock.** The level's two events (valves → core, bucket, press) are kept off the tape
   route, so the first tape tests movement only.
7. **Letters stay on the floor.** Every letter set tours the four floor zones; none touches the upper loop.
   The letters teach the floor lines, the tape teaches the second layer.
8. **Failure costs a climb, not a run.** A fall drops 6–9 m to the floor; the back door is ≈ 11 s from the
   spawn, so several attempts fit in two minutes. LP3's bail on the platform still reached the tape.
9. **Expert shortcut by gap knowledge.** The TAS reaches a platform from the back guardrail with a boneless
   over the molten tank (12 s from spawn). The level lets mastery skip the designed staircase.
10. **The revert is the new link of the floor lines.** All three of G3's lines join vert to manual with a
    revert (Wrap Around → revert → manual; stall → revert → manual; handplant → revert). The tape route
    itself does not need it.
11. **Compact first level, vertical not wide.** G3 calls the Foundry compact; its growth is in height (five
    layers), which prepares the much bigger Canada.
12. **The remake scores the approach.** COMBO letters on the back-door catwalk, cash on the tape's
    platform and a Hidden Deck on the rafters turn the easy route into a scored, populated line.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Mid-loop crystal.** Build an upper loop of narrow platforms ≈ 6–8 m above the floor: catwalk from the
   spawn → small glass box → long beam (grindable platform edge, ≈ 0.4 m wide) → two hanging slabs →
   balcony. Put the crystal on the beam's midpoint, ≥ 7 m above any floor, over the level's busiest zone.
2. **Two ends, two skill levels.** Easy end: a kicker beside the spawn onto the catwalk, then a short ramp at
   the box that ollies straight into the beam grind (grind snap radius 2.1 m forgives the line-up). Hard end:
   a banked wall to wallride/vert-air up to the balcony, then two ollies across ≈ 6–8 m gaps (inside the
   ≈ 9.4 m cruise-ollie range) to a slab and to the beam.
3. **Breakable box.** Close the box with smashable glass (break-glass) so the easy end is visible but
   reads as "something to break into".
4. **Seed the climb.** Put two lesser pickups (or a trick-gate reward) on the balcony quarter pipe and on a
   high duct above the slabs, so the hard end is visited before the crystal route is known.
5. **Keep walkers off.** Hold the beam ≥ 7 m above the floor and ≥ 3 m from any ledge a walker can grab;
   the slabs and box are the only standable surfaces nearby, and both lie on the designed routes.
6. **Cheap retries.** Land a miss on flat floor beside the spawn-side catwalk kicker, so a retry is a
   ≈ 10–15 s climb inside the run.
