## Spawn and first view

The level opens with Mat Hoffman (BMX) offering the High Score (LP4 4:24:36). The S-K-A-T-E giver stands in front of a grey building in a plaza (LP4 4:26:30), and **the S is visible at the goal start**, above the end of the upper ramp ahead and to the right (LP4 4:26:36). The opening invites a QP transfer onto the upper ramp and an air off its end.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **Givers point at the goal.** The Tollbooth Operator gestures from the bridge toward the ferry (LP4 4:31:22–4:31:28); the S is visible from the S-K-A-T-E giver (4:26:36).
- **The El as a navigational spine.** Red pillars and overhead tracks run through much of the level; the E sits where "the railtrack crosses above" (THG).
- **Cyan arrows** over givers and red arrows for the pizza run (LP4 4:28:28, 4:35:28).
- **Letters on visible structure:** the A on a red rail against a pale wall, the T at the end of a wire against the sky (LP4 4:26:54, 4:27:22). The K is the exception, hidden inside a building (4:26:46–4:26:50).

## Design logic

1. **The finale is about scale.** Chicago's showpiece is a 900 off a raised drawbridge onto a moving ferry: the biggest drop and the only moving landing target in these five levels.
2. **Rising geometry.** The Bridge Challenge raises the drawbridge leaves each step ("Even higher this time!"), so the same gap becomes harder. This is a reusable way to grade one obstacle.
3. **One letter inside, one at the top of a wire.** The K is through a doorway inside a building and the T is at the top of a climbing wire, two different "hidden" grammars in one S-K-A-T-E.
4. **The uphill wire teaches the grind-ollie boost.** The T's wire needs "an ollie or two to grind all the way up" (THG). A collectible is placed to teach a technique tied to that piece of geometry.
5. **Cash rides the hard line.** A $100 icon sits on the T's wire just after the letter (LP4 4:27:26), rewarding a player who holds the grind past the letter.
6. **The El is a second street.** The elevated tracks are grindable from a kicker and carry the first pizza (THG), giving a big map a fast upper-layer route.
7. **Targets strung on rails.** Ten ballplayers along one railing and four black cars in a row (THG) turn crowds into grind lines.
8. **Late-career thresholds.** High Score 900,000 and Sick Score 1,500,000 (LP4) assume the full skill set; the last goal is a flatland ledge combo, a skill test rather than an exploration test.
9. **The river is the failure plane** under the bridge and ferry goals ("You big drip!", LP4 4:31:36), so retries are quick.
10. **Skitching is the big-map answer.** THG's hydrant advice is "Skitch on a car if you're low on time", and the goals are spread along long roads under bridges (THG; LP4 4:32:40–4:33:16). THPS4's new verb doubles as fast travel for timed collect goals.
11. **The city cast stages each challenge at a landmark.** Mat Hoffman at the plaza, the Tollbooth Operator at the bridge, the Undercover Cop with the mafiosos, Ollie the Bum with the letters, the Local Photographer at the ledge (LP4 4:24:36–4:44:22). Each giver anchors a goal to a place, which helps navigation on a large map.

## Original vs remake

Chicago is **absent from THPS 3+4** (PS34 index) and from the VGMaps PS1 set. Only the 2002 PS2 design exists.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Bridge-to-boat crystal.** A tilting bridge leaf (a moving platform that rises in steps) over water, with a boat-shaped moving platform travelling below. Put the crystal on the boat's top deck so the player must launch off the raised leaf and land on the moving deck. A charged ollie from the leaf's lip (≈ 13.8 m long at the 23 m/s charge) sets the reach; water is a respawn.
2. **Rising gap.** One channel gap whose banks rise between attempts or stages, with the crystal at the top stage over the gap.
3. **Climbing cable with boosts.** A long uphill grindable cable from a building ledge, where grind ollies (≈ 2.4 m pop) keep momentum. Put the crystal at the top and a shard just past it to reward holding the grind.
4. **Interior letter.** A shard or the crystal inside a building reached through one of three doors, with the right door marked by its position (the middle one) or a light.
5. **Elevated track shortcut.** A kicker up to an elevated rail (an El) that crosses the map, with the crystal at a junction of two tracks ("Over TWO Tracks") and an optional passing-train hazard.
