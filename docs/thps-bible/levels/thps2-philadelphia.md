The level's signature element is **the long wire**: it starts at the tip of the curved planter (Z4),
crosses high over the fountain (Z3) and rises to the brick building's roof (Z6). The tape hangs on it
just past the fountain's south-east rim.

Compass words in this file (north, east …) describe the VG image with its top as north, not real-world
bearings.

Loops: the plaza circulates round the fountain (stairs on all sides). The roof network is itself a
loop: up by the wire (or by stairs → kicker → planter → balcony), down by the balcony railing to a
planter, the telephone wire to the street, or a drop.

## Spawn and first view

- You start at the south-west edge of the plaza by the street, facing north-east across it (VG START;
  LP2 0:48:50, 0:51:00). In view: the funbox on the left, the long kicker ahead, and in the distance the
  **fountain's water jets and the red THPS sign**.
- **Visible almost at once:** bell B1 floating between the two kicker ramps ahead-right (LP2 1:56).
- **Not visible from spawn:** the tape. It is small and far off at the fountain's far side, and the LP2
  spawn frames do not show it. The fountain jets draw the eye toward the centre, where the wire crosses.
- The opening invites a right-hand loop: kickers (B1) → east planter (S) → awning (K). LP2 run 1 takes
  it, and LP2 run 2 takes the stairs-kicker-balcony chain to the valve.

**Route (observed in LP2 run 2, original; the clock counts down):**

1. 1:12 From the pavilion side of the plaza (Z4; a $50 icon by the pavilion), skate toward the pavilion
   steps.
2. 1:11–1:09 Ollie up the small step set ("Up the Small Step Set").
3. 1:08–1:03 Link south-east toward the fountain along the planter edges (no comply → wall ride →
   BS 5-0 → BS 50-50 → manual).
4. 1:02–1:00 Ollie up onto the **curved elevated planter** and ride its grass top toward its tip at the
   fountain's north-west rim ("ollie up to the grass", thumbsticks). PS12's version from spawn: "head
   left and find the planter that curves around to the right. At the end you should find a long wire."
5. 0:59 Ollie off the planter tip onto the **wire**.
6. 0:58–0:56 Grind **up** the wire across the fountain (drained in this run: its yellow centre disc and
   floor rails show below), re-catching the grind with small hops, and cross the fountain's south-east
   rim. The tape glows ahead.
7. 0:55 **FIND THE SECRET TAPE**, mid-wire.
8. 0:54–0:53 A $250 follows; the wire carries on up to the brick building's roof. From there you can drop
   right onto the valve balcony (absolutcheats) or, in the remake, ollie right onto the roof for the VV
   logo (PS12).

Reverse direction (original): from the valve roof, grind the wire **down** and the tape is on the way
(RPGC). The full descent is "Fly by Wire" (500), and ollieing off to grind the fountain's centre piece is
"Death from Above" (500) (PS12).

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The wire leads the eye.** It is a long line running from a planter, over the fountain, to a roof,
  and the tape glows on it above the most-visited spot in the plaza. Thumbsticks' advice is just
  "follow the wire to its source".
- **Bells teach launch points.** B4 sits at the very tip of the planter where the wire starts, so a
  player collecting bells has already stood at the tape's launch point.
- **The fountain is the centre of gravity:** the water jets and the red THPS sign are visible from
  spawn and draw the player to the middle, under the wire.
- **The unlock is shown by consequence:** grinding the line drops telephone poles across the fence.
  The fence and white pillars along the street already hint at something behind them.
- **Hidden on purpose:** the skatepark, behind the fence, until the poles fall; the valve, out of sight
  on the balcony until you reach the roof chain.

## Design logic

1. **The tape hangs over the level's centrepiece**, so the challenge is execution (balance, commitment),
   not discovery. That suits level 7, where the player already knows wires.
2. **Mid-span placement:** the tape is about two-thirds along the wire, not at its end. A short grind
   gets nothing; you must hold the line past the fountain.
3. **One line serves two goals.** The wire is also the original's main route to the valve roof
   (absolutcheats), so the tape run and the drain run share a line and can be chained.
4. **Two directions:** up from the planter (LP2, PS12) or down from the roof (RPGC). A player who
   reached the roof another way still meets the tape.
5. **Launch from an elevated planter:** you must first ollie onto a raised grass planter and ride its
   top, a small skill step before the wire.
6. **Cheap failure:** a fall lands you in the fountain bowl or on the steps, a few seconds from the
   planter. Retrying the tape costs ≈ 10 s.
7. **The wire family is taught in the same level:** the overhead line (unlock), the telephone wire
   (exit), the long wire (tape). Each wire changes something or leads somewhere.
8. **State change as reward:** draining the fountain turns the central water feature into a new rail
   arena (Grind of Faith, 1500) and releases floor cash.
9. **Secret area holds a goal, not the tape.** The hidden Phillyside park carries the liptrick goal and
   $850, so the unlock pays off through a goal list item.
10. **Bells and letters are placed on basic features** (kickers, planters, ramps, steps, stair rails),
    arranged as one loop from the start (counter-clockwise on the VG image).
11. **Feature reuse:** the awning carries the K, the Bluntside goal, a bell and a 500-point gap.
12. **Cash rises with demand** (high/pro/sick $2500–$4000; drain and lips $2500; tape, SKATE, bells,
    awning $2000). The tape is a mid-value goal here: late in the career it is the "easy" secret.
13. **The roof network loops:** up by the wire or the balcony chain, down by the telephone wire, the
    railing-to-planter or a drop, so the roof never strands the player.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Rising wire over a pit.** Run a 30–40 m sloped rail or rope from the tip of a raised planter
   (≈ 1 m, ollie-able) to a two-storey roof (≈ 7 m), crossing a sunken pool. Put the crystal ≈ 60%
   along, ≈ 5 m above the ground under it, just past the far rim. Our grind has no balance meter, so
   add one mid-span break that needs a grind ollie (≈ 2.4 m pop), or make the span a **snapping rope**
   that gives way behind the rider, so the player must keep moving.
2. **Shared roof chain.** End the rope at a roof whose balcony holds a switch or valve (a trick gate)
   that changes the central pool: drained, it shows new rails and a bonus. The crystal sits on the way
   to the second objective, so the chain pays twice.
3. **Teach the launch point first.** Put a minor pickup at the planter tip where the rope starts, so
   players visit it before they know why.
4. **Two directions.** Keep a second roof route (stair rail → kicker → planter → balcony ledge, or an
   on-foot ledge grab ≤ 2.4 m) so the crystal can also be taken grinding down.
5. **Wire that changes the world.** A rail off a ramp continues onto an overhead line; grinding it drops
   poles (moving objects) that flatten a fence and open a vert park beside the street.
6. **Cheap retry under a visible target.** Keep the crystal over the level's busiest space, with the
   launch planter a few seconds from any fall.
