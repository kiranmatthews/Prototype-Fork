Vertical layers: street; ledges, planters, balcony rails and steps (≈ 0.5–4 m); second-storey walkways and
display windows (≈ 4–7 m); rooftops and the ski-jump scaffold (≈ 12–18 m); the Pagoda roof (≈ 4–7 m); two
closed secret rooms (T room, clocktower bowl). Vert: Bendy's Lip, the Hubba halfpipes, the clocktower bowl,
the Chinatown quarter pipes, the handicap-walkway quarter pipes. Loops: the streets ring the plaza and the
blocks; the rooftop route is a one-way spur that ends by falling back into the plaza.

## Spawn and first view

Spawn: Z1, at the top of Lombard, facing down the hill (LP1 23:10–23:11). Cop car 1 is "right behind you when
you start the level. Just turn around rather than skating off and do a quick ollie to grind on its hood"
(G1 p119; LP1 23:12–23:13 "1 OF 5 COP CARS").

Visible from spawn: the Lombard descent, its ledges and balconies. No letter is in the opening view in LP1;
the K is found at the bottom of Lombard (LP1 23:23). The tape is not visible: it is ≈ 100–120 m away to the
north-west, behind the fountain-side blocks.

What the opening invites: two ways down Lombard (left ledges or right balconies), plus the cop car behind you.
G1 p116 builds the high-score line here; G1 p121 builds the K route on the balconies.

Route (designed line; G1 p122–p124, read from the page images; LP1 25:17–25:53):

1. **Get speed from the corner quarter pipe** (vert-air): "Head over to the quarterpipe in which curves
   around the corner and hit it to get up some speed" (G1 p122; Bendy's Lip area). LP1 25:20–25:24.
2. **Ollie into the trench at the back of the fountain sculpture** (ollie): "aim for the groove running
   through the back of the fountain and with a big ollie jump to it" (G1 p122). Caption, p123: "Use the
   quarterpipe in the corner to get your speed up enough to ollie into the trench running along the back of
   the fountain." LP1 25:29–25:31 rides up the sculpture's sloped side.
3. **Ride the trench and jump to the walkway** (transfer): "Without losing any speed, cruise along the trench,
   up then down then ollie off the lip at the end across the a gap to the walkway on the building. This jump
   will give you the Fountain Gap bonus" (G1 p122; Fountain Gap 750). LP1 25:33 "360 shove it + Fountain Gap".
4. **Slow down and follow the first walkway left** (ledge): "On the walkway of the first building press down
   to slow down, then carefully follow the path left" (G1 p122). LP1 25:33–25:35.
5. **Ollie to the next building's walkway** (ollie): "Get some speed back and ollie to the walkway of the next
   building" (G1 p122). LP1 25:35–25:37.
6. **Along the second building's back and up the narrow ramp** (ledge): "Follow that walkway along the second
   building's back and up the narrow ramp to the third building" (G1 p122). LP1 25:38–25:40.
7. **Round the third building to its roof** (ledge): "Turn left and follow the path up the ramp then around
   right. The whole time you want to keep pressing Down … Follow the path as it continues around the third
   building going right" (G1 p122). Caption, p123: "Continue to follow the path as it leads up to the roof of
   the third building." LP1 25:40–25:47 (a bonus icon on the roof at 25:42).
8. **Up the scaffold ski jump and launch** (kicker): "When you reach the roof head up the ramp on the
   scaffolding and with some speed, and a hefty ollie, launch off the lip at the end as straight as possible"
   (G1 p122). Caption, p124: "use the giant ski jump style ramp to launch out over the Embarcadero and down
   through the Pagoda". LP1 25:47–25:50.
9. **Fall through the glass roof** (break-glass): "you should fall right through the glass roof of the Pagoda,
   snagging the hidden tape along the way" (G1 p122). Pagoda Gap 1000 (G1 p127). LP1 25:52: "540 kickflip to
   indy + Madonna + Pagoda Gap", "HIDDEN TAPE!", landing on the plaza at 25:53.

Remake line (PS12): "find the sculpture/fountain on the opposite side. You can ollie onto it, and then onto
the side of a building. Follow the ledge around, and jump to the next building. Keep following this path all
the way up to the roof. With as much speed as you can, ramp off the big ramp". LP12 34:18–34:33 follows it:
a 50-50 on the sculpture, a ledge with arched windows, across a glass skylight with planks, rooftops, the
striped ski jump, the Ferry Building and the Bay Bridge ahead, then through the Pagoda.

TAS1 line (N64, 4:56–5:03): the run grinds the Backwoods Ledge behind the trees (4:56), crashes into the T
secret room (T at 4:58), nollies out of its exit (4:59), flies high over the plaza (4:59.5–5:00), fastplants
onto the Pagoda's plinth and roof rim (5:00.5–5:01) and smashes down through the glass roof (5:02,
"HIDDEN TAPE!"). The T room and spiral top serve as a second, higher launch platform. Medium confidence on
the exact path between the room and the Pagoda.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The centrepiece holds the tape.** The Pagoda's glass dome sits on a plinth at the plaza's corner with the
  tape glowing above it; the player sees it while working the plaza, Bendy's Lip and the fountain.
- **The ski jump is an arrow.** On the third building's roof a long wooden ramp points straight down at the
  Pagoda. The remake adds red and green stripes along its edges (LP12 34:29).
- **The fountain as a ramp.** The sculpture's trench runs towards the neighbouring building's walkway, and the
  gap's name (Fountain Gap) is on the checklist.
- **Letters behind glass.** K glows behind a display window at the bottom of Lombard; T and E sit in rooms
  entered through windows. The level teaches early that windows are doors.
- **Hidden on purpose.** The walkway route up the buildings, the T room, the clocktower bowl and cop car 3
  ("tucked away in the shadows").
- **The guide's warning is part of the design.** G1 p115: "Getting all five tapes in a single run will be
  impossible"; G1 p120: dedicate a run to S-K-A-T-E; G1 p122: every second of the clock for the tape. The
  level is built for several runs, each with its own goal set (LP1: run 1 cop cars + S-K-A-T-E; run 2 high
  score, hidden tape, pro score).

## Design logic

1. **Climb then drop.** The tape is lower than its launch. Height is earned by a long climb on walkways and
   narrow ramps and then spent in one falling air through glass.
2. **Show the target, hide the door.** The tape is on the most visible object in the level, but the entrance
   to its route is another landmark (the fountain) a block away.
3. **A named gap opens the route.** Fountain Gap is the first move of the tape line, so the gap checklist
   points at the entrance; Pagoda Gap closes it.
4. **Speed management is the skill.** Gain (corner quarter pipe), keep (the trench, "without losing any
   speed"), brake (walkways, "keep pressing Down"), regain (between buildings), and finally the ski jump's own
   drop. Each phase needs a different input.
5. **A stats gate on top of the skill gate.** "Only characters with their attributes built up will be able to
   get to the tape" (G1 p122): the career's eighth tape assumes the player has spent stat points.
6. **A time gate.** "Every second of time on the clock to get into position" (G1 p122). The level is designed
   for dedicated runs; the five tapes cannot be taken in one (G1 p115).
7. **Secret rooms interlock.** The T room and the spiral top are a second high platform: the TAS uses them to
   reach the Pagoda without the ski jump. The level's secret areas are connected in three dimensions.
8. **Letters spread over districts.** One letter per district (Lombard, Hubba, Chinatown, spiral, clocktower)
   tours the whole level; G1's efficient order is not the spelling order.
9. **Failure is visible.** A short ski jump lands the skater in the plaza beneath the tape he just missed; the
   cost is the whole climb again.
10. **The career's synthesis.** The route combines a quarter pipe for speed, walkway precision, roof access by
    a hidden path (as in Downtown), a long committed launch (as in Downhill Jam) and flying through glass (as
    in Warehouse).
11. **Scores are not the reward.** Lombard Gap (5000) and the spiral grinds (2000/2500) are the big scoring
    items; the tape's Pagoda Gap is 1000. The tape is its own reward.
12. **Remake keeps the route and signs it.** Stripes on the ski jump, a stat point and the VV logo on the
    first leg; better air control shortens the fountain-to-tape time to ≈ 15 s (LP12).

## Transfer to this game

1. **Climb-then-drop crystal.** Hang the crystal ≈ 0.5–1 m above a glass dome at the plaza's centre, ≈ 5–6 m
   above the ground; put a rooftop launch ramp ≈ 10–12 m higher and ≈ 15–20 m back, aimed at it. A charged
   ollie off a downhill ramp at ≈ 20–23 m/s covers that easily; the challenge is the climb, not the jump.
2. **Walkway chain on building faces.** Second-storey ledges ≈ 1.5–2 m wide with ollie gaps ≤ 6–8 m between
   buildings (within the ≈ 9.4 m cruise ollie) and one narrow ramp up; add an edge rail so a grind (2.1 m snap)
   can rescue a drifting line.
3. **Sculpture as the entrance.** A curved trench in a fountain sculpture that launches onto the first walkway
   (a named gap), so the route's door is a readable object.
4. **Speed phases.** Gain on a corner quarter pipe, keep through the trench, brake on the walkways (a slowing
   surface or tight corners), regain on a downhill roof ramp; a speed pad on the roof can replace THPS's
   stats gate.
5. **Arrow geometry.** Point the final ramp straight at the crystal and mark its edges with coloured stripes,
   as the remake does.
6. **Interlocking secret rooms.** Let a letter room's exit door open onto a high platform from which experts
   can reach the dome directly (TAS-style), so mastering one secret shortens another.
7. **Dedicated run design.** If the crystal takes most of the 2 minutes, keep the other goals elsewhere so the
   level reads as "one run per goal set", or add a checkpoint at the fountain to keep the climb repeatable.
