# THPS2 — Skate Heaven (bonus level)

Sources: `VG` TonyHawk_sProSkater2-SkateHeaven.png (whole map at 1800 px; three zones at native
resolution; the house and halfpipe complex upscaled ×2); `PS12 skate-heaven` (remake: volcano route,
VV logo, alien plushie, full gap list with points); `LP12` 1:12:50–1:14:00 (remake run; the volcano
trigger observed at 1 s and 1.5 s sampling, 1:13:07–1:13:29); `WEB absolutcheats` (PS1 secrets: the
volcano route, the "Enima Chen" ramp island, Skip-to-Restart points, the clock exit, unlock condition);
`WEB gamegenie` (PS1 unlock condition); `WEB trophy-tom` (remake unlock; no goals). LP2 does not visit
this level.

Evidence level: the original has **no goals, no tape and no cash icons** (VG shows no checklist and no
icons; the THPS2 goal lists cover career levels only). This file records the layout, the level's
secret area (the volcano → Animal Chin ramp island, which plays the tape's role) and the remake's two
pickups. The volcano trigger is **observed** in the remake (LP12). The original route is text-sourced
(absolutcheats). The secret island is not clearly drawn on VG.

Layers: the void of space all round; **pink neon island edges are grind lines** (the "… 2 Edge" gaps).
Height order from VG and the gap names: the snake run's top and the house/hall roofs are highest; the
start island and Tony's island are the middle tier; the volcano platform and Sadlands are low
("Sadlands Up 2 Isle of Tony" 1000 vs "Isle of Tony 2 Sadlands" 300).

## Spawn and first view

- You start in the radramp halfpipe on the start island (VG START). PS12: "head down the half pipe in
  front of you and follow it to the right". LP12 drops straight into the Combi bowl (1:56).
- In view within seconds: the wooden halfpipes and Tony's house, and the **volcano, smoking** at the
  left of the frame (LP12 1:13:11, "1:55" on the clock). The volcano is the level's standing tease.
- Nothing to collect in the original. The invitation is pure flow: the snake run to the full pipe, or
  the bowl and halfpipes.

**Route (remake, PS12 with LP12's observed opening; original steps from absolutcheats in brackets):**

1. From the start, ride the halfpipe in front of you and follow it right (PS12). [Original: follow the
   snake run and go through the full pipe, then jump off the hip to the top of the halfpipe.]
2. Get onto the **house roof** beside the wooden halfpipe. LP12: BS wallride + wallie up the wall at
   1:44–1:43. [Original: grind the rail on top of the halfpipe to your right, then jump onto the roof.]
3. **Grind the neon-blue gutter** along the roof edge toward the second roof: "Time 2 Feed the
   Volcano!!!" (4000) scores at 1:40 and the volcano erupts (LP12 1:13:25–1:13:28).
4. Drop off the roof, go down and left past a neon-blue pole and a neon-pink pole to the platform with
   the angled pink neon rail (PS12). [Original: "proceed to the Combibowl area but do not enter".]
5. With lots of speed, use the side of the channel to **ollie into the middle of the crater** ("Feed
   Me!!!", 2000) before the rumbling stops (≈ 30 s in the remake).
6. You arrive on the **Chin ramp island**. Its gaps: "Blowin It Out the Hole!" (transfer across the
   main halfpipe; it opens the "Tunnel of Luvin"), "The Holy Crail", "Chen Rail Series". Leave by
   hitting the clock (absolutcheats).

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The volcano is the tease:** visible and smoking from the start, then erupting with plume and rumble
  once triggered. Its gap names spell out what to do.
- **Neon outlines** (blue and pink) mark every grindable edge and the island borders; in the remake they
  also frame the paths (LP12).
- **Hidden:** the secret island has no visible link to the map (you reach it by teleport), and the
  remake's pickups sit there. VG does not appear to draw the island separately.
- The gap names double as signposts ("Up 2 Combi", "Down 2 Tonys Island", "Sadlands Up 2 Isle of Tony").

## Design logic

1. **A bonus level with no checklist still keeps one secret.** The hidden island takes the tape's role
   as the thing to discover.
2. **The secret opens through a two-step chain** (trigger, then a timed entry), and both steps are
   named gaps worth 4000 and 2000, so the score display rewards discovery.
3. **A timed window** (≈ 30 s in the remake) turns a one-off discovery into a route the player must
   plan.
4. **The entry is a commit jump into a hazard-looking hole.** A lava crater invites caution, and the
   game rewards trust.
5. **The level is built as an anthology** of famous skate spots and objects (bowl, snake run, full
   pipe, Chin ramp, a dirt-hill spot). As a reward level it rewards with **places**, not pickups
   (inference).
6. **Gap ladders teach transfer width and direction:** each snake curve has "Weak Sauce" (narrow, 50),
   normal (800–2000) and "Reverse" (uphill, 1000–2000) versions.
7. **Climbing pays more than dropping:** "Sadlands Up 2 Isle of Tony" pays 1000 against 300 for the
   drop.
8. **The highest values go to sustained or rare lines:** the full-pipe loop (5000), the bench series
   (5000), the volcano trigger (4000), the wallride-to-platform (2500).
9. **Island edges are rails**, so falling-off risk is also a line ("… 2 Edge" gaps).
10. **The remake hangs both new collectibles behind the old secret**, turning an Easter egg into a
    collection target without moving any geometry.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Timed secret door.** Grinding a rooftop gutter (or a trick rail that needs a named trick) starts
   an "eruption" timer of about 30 s. During it a crater becomes a trampoline or launcher that throws
   the rider to a separate floating island holding the crystal. Show the timer with sound and a plume.
2. **Commit jump.** The crater should look dangerous. Approach from a platform with a kicker and an
   angled rail, so a full-speed ollie (≈ 13.8 m at the charge maximum) clears the rim.
3. **A pickup that rewards the place.** On the secret island, float the crystal above the end of a
   halfpipe so it is visible on arrival but needs a vert air at the far end.
4. **Return device.** Give the secret island a clear exit object (a clock or a sign that teleports
   back), so the timed trip is never a soft-lock.
5. **Gap ladder for practice.** Build a snake run where each bend has a narrow and a wide transfer, so
   players learn spine transfers before the crystal route needs one.
