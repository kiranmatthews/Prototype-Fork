## Spawn and first view

You spawn in the dirt yard facing the farmhouse (LP4 4:06:05). The S-K-A-T-E goal-giver stands just behind you with a cyan arrow (4:06:07): "Hey bub – collect the S-K-A-T-E letters!" (4:06:09). **The S floats over the food-stall counters in view of the giver** (LP4 4:06:09, letter at top left). The midway's counters are the first line the level offers.

## Collectibles

### Secret tape

**None.** The 2002 THPS4 has no tape (LP4's full 190-goal run, 4:45:58), and Carnival is not in the remake. The tape-equivalents below are the level's hardest-to-reach collectible routes.

**Route (THG and LP4):**
1. Drop in from the wooden platform (LP4 4:23:29).
2. **Spine transfer over the next two obstacles** (THG; LP4 4:23:31 "Spine Transfer", 4:23:33 "2 of 10").
3. **Jump into a grind on the rollercoaster track**; hop off just after rounding the corner (THG; LP4 4:23:35, 3/10).
4. **Grind the outer wall** on the right and follow it behind the portacabins (THG; LP4 4:23:37–4:23:41, 4–6/10).
5. Continue onto the **Rocket ride**; hop down to the left for the 7th (THG; LP4 4:23:43, 7/10, castle flames in view).
6. Pick up the 8th, swerve left, use the **kicker up onto the castle** (9th) (THG; LP4 4:23:45–4:23:49).
7. The **last ticket is on the opposite side of the bowl** (THG).

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The goal-giver at the spawn and the S in view** (LP4 4:06:09) start the letter tour immediately.
- **Ride landmarks** (ferris wheel, spinning bowl, coaster, castle with flames, water tower) are visible from most of the fair and anchor navigation (LP4 4:09:24, 4:20:24).
- **Each ticket is visible from the previous one** (LP4 4:23:29–4:23:49), and the HUD counter confirms the line.
- **Photo cutouts are oversized cartoon boards** in front of fences (LP4 4:15:18, 4:16:42), so the grind behind each one is obvious.
- **Hidden on purpose:** nothing structural. The challenge is time (ticket clock, boot chain), not finding a hidden room.

## Design logic

1. **A bought level without a tape**, whose hardest content is timed lines on moving rides: a 0:30 ten-ticket run, the coaster grind with a car behind you, a lip ride to the top of the ferris wheel, and a 900 inside a moving rocket.
2. **Rides replace architecture.** The vert pieces (Rocket ride, Thundercups base, spinning bowl QPs) and rails (coaster track) are rides; fair geometry turns into skate geometry.
3. **The letter tour is short and teaches the vertical stack**: counter → logs → QP transfer → roof → roof ring (≈ 36 s). This late-career level assumes fluency and keeps S-K-A-T-E quick.
4. **Tight clocks make failure cheap but frequent.** The ticket clock (≈ 0:30) and the boot chain's ≈ 10 s steps mean many short retries rather than long climbs.
5. **Moving surfaces carry you to height.** The ferris wheel is the level's highest point and you get there by holding a lip, not by an air. The same idea appears in Shipyard (crane), Zoo (elephant) and Chicago (bridge).
6. **Cutouts mark fences.** The photo goal turns grindable fences into collectibles by putting a big painted board in front of each.
7. **The castle window is the one "through" moment.** The amateur C-O-M-B-O wallrides onto the castle wall and passes through a window (THG), a small secret-passage beat inside a combo.
8. **Arena dressing doubles as targets.** The stockade banners over the comp arena are lip targets (THG), so decoration becomes a goal.
9. **Late-career scoring is high**: High Score 750,000, Sick Score 1,500,000, 500,000 combo (LP4). Carnival is tuned for players who finished the main career.
10. **Easy line first, hard line later, same ground.** S-K-A-T-E is a gentle 36 s tour starting at the spawn with the S in view (LP4 4:06:09–4:06:45). The ten-ticket run reuses the same fair as a 0:30 test (LP4 4:23:29–4:23:50). Repetition of place builds the knowledge the hard line needs.
11. **The theme supplies the collectibles.** Fair games (the bell, "Win a Prize"), photo cutouts and ride tickets are literal fairground objects (THG), so every pickup reads in-world without a floating icon.

## Original vs remake

Carnival is **absent from THPS 3+4** (PS34 index lists no Carnival). Only the 2002 PS2 design exists; the VGMaps PS1 set also has no Carnival.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Ferris-wheel crystal.** A rotating wheel of moving platforms (carriages). Lip-stall on a carriage at its lowest point and hold it as the wheel carries you up; the crystal sits just above the top carriage, so you collect it at the apex and drop. This engine has moving platforms and lip stalls.
2. **Coaster travelling rail.** A long rail shaped like a coaster track with a moving car (a hazard) following the hero. The crystal sits at the bottom of the big hill, so you must start just ahead of the car and keep the grind.
3. **Ticket-line crystal.** A 25–30 s line of shards (spine ×2 → travelling rail → wall top → kicker onto a castle → bowl) ending with the crystal across the bowl; the shards set the pace.
4. **Rocket-ride vert.** A swinging vert car (a moving halfpipe) with the crystal above its rim at the swing's peak. It needs vert air timed with the swing.
5. **Castle window.** A wallride onto a castle wall whose grind line passes through a window opening into an inner yard holding the crystal, with a "through" moment inside a line.
