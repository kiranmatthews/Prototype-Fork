## Spawn and first view

Spawn: Z1, on a small wooden kicker in the start alley (VG START label 1000, 553), facing south down the
alley towards the south street and the fountain park (observed LP1 14:00–14:05: the skater reaches the S on
the south-west ledge in ≈ 5 s; PS12: "From starting position, use kicker ramp to get on the right-hand ledge,
then grind the rail" for the S; G1 p088: sign 1 is "diagonal across the intersection after the S").

What is visible (LP1 14:02, native frame): the alley floor, low walls lined with lit window strips, brick
above, a streetlight and the street at the far end. The loading tip reads "LOOK FOR GAPS TO JUMP FOR EXTRA
POINTS". The S is ahead on the right-hand ledge, ≈ 35–40 m away. **The tape is not in frame**: it hangs
≈ 16 m further down the alley and ≈ 10–15 m up, over the left (east) wall's roofline, which the low chase
camera does not show. Medium confidence.

What the opening invites: the kickers in the alley floor point at the ledges (Kicker Gap, Kicker 2 Edge),
so the first move is a kicker to a ledge grind into the S, then across the intersection to sign 1. The
opening line therefore starts the spiral (see Guidance).

Route (designed line; G1 p088–p093, LP1 15:06–15:34):

1. **Work the spiral** (optional for the tape alone). S on the alley's south ledge → sign 1 → K over the
   fountain → sign 2 → sign 3 on the east deck → E on the north high rail → sign 4 → A at the west quarter
   pipes → sign 5 at the office. G1 p089: you should "still have at least a minute left on the clock at this
   point, plenty of time to get to the tunnel leading to the roof with the letter T and the Hidden Tape".
2. **Ride up the empty car carrier** (kicker). The truck is parked at the north-west corner (VG 340–420 ×
   395–420), "to the right of the letter A" (G1 p091), its ramp-like bed aimed at a window. LP1 15:09–15:10.
3. **Ollie through the window into the hidden tunnel** (break-glass): T 2 T Gap, 500. Alternatively the big
   street ramp puts you through the tunnel window (Secret Tunnel Entrance, 500), or through the whole tunnel
   without landing (Tunnel Gap, 1000). LP1 15:11.
4. **Grind the cement barriers along the climbing corridor** (grind, rail-transfer): "With enough speed you
   can ollie from one to the next" (G1 p092). LP1 15:11–15:16 grinds five or six in one combo (11,184).
5. **Ride up the corridor ramp into the glass skyway over the north street** (ramp): T floats inside.
   LP1 15:17–15:21 ("S-K-A-T-E TAPE" at 15:21).
6. **Roll out onto roof A** (ground): greenhouse, the launch ramp to the sucky room, and the bowl.
   LP1 15:22–15:26.
7. **Session the bowl for speed** (vert-air): LP1 15:26–15:32, ≈ 6 s (540 switch kickflip + Madonna).
8. **Exit through the bowl's left-hand ramp and launch east across the alley** (transfer): the gap is the
   ≈ 12–13 m alley plus roof margins, ≈ 14–17 m lip to far lip on VG. "When you go for it, make sure you're
   lined up to intersect the hidden tape" (G1 p092). LP1 15:33.5 airborne with the tape ahead.
9. **Tape in mid-gap; land on roof B**: Roof 2 Roof, 2000 (G1 p098). LP1 15:34: "HIDDEN TAPE!" then
   "360 switch kickflip to indy + Roof 2 Roof" (7,341). Roof B's pool and rails then serve the pro score
   (LP1 15:36 "PRO SCORE TAPE").

TAS1 line (N64, 2:37.5–2:43): from spawn the skater fastplants off an alley kicker, wallrides and grinds up
the alley-side wall onto roof B (2:39.5–2:40.5), crosses it (rocket air, Smith grind, a benihana at a wooden
kicker), then Smith-grinds the roof's lip above the alley and takes the tape at 2:42.5 (Smith grind + Japan
air + nosegrind). The tape is ≈ 20 m from spawn in a straight line; the designed route makes it ≈ 90 s away.
One detail does not match VG: a lit NEVERSOFT sign appears beside the grind, while VG shows that sign on the
west side near A. The N64 build or a second sign may explain it; low confidence on that detail only.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **A spiral of goal objects.** G1 p088: the letters and signs make "a nice counterclockwise spiral through
  the level". On VG the order spawn → S (south) → park (south-east) → east deck → north street → west →
  north-west truck → skyway → roof → tape walks south, east, north, west and back to the centre, ending over
  the spawn alley. Every sign sits on the path to the next goal.
- **A ramp that points at a window.** The car carrier's bed reads as a ramp aimed at the tunnel window
  (thumbsticks: "suspiciously ramp-like"); the guide places it "right of the letter A" (G1 p091).
- **A letter behind glass.** The skyway bridges the north street; VG draws T inside it. From the street the
  skater can see a letter floating in a glass corridor over his head: the secret area is advertised.
- **Speed source on the roof.** The bowl is the obvious thing to do on roof A, and doing it supplies the
  speed the jump needs.
- **Tape glow at the moment of commitment.** The tape is first seen from the bowl exit, 1–2 s before the
  pickup (LP1 15:32.5–15:33). The remake adds a big painted green arrow on the correct ramp (LP12 19:48;
  PS12: "the ramp with an arrow on it").
- **Night lighting.** Lit windows, the lit NEVERSOFT sign (A), the video screen (Billboard Grind) and glowing
  icons stand out against dark streets.
- **Hidden on purpose.** The tunnel interior, the sucky room and the office interior are invisible from the
  street. The tape is never shown from the ground in the sampled frames, although it is ≈ 20 m from spawn.

## Design logic

1. **The tape closes a spiral.** Downtown is the first level where the guide calls out an intended order:
   objectives "lined up one after the next from the starting point" (G1 p085), a counterclockwise spiral of
   letters and signs (G1 p088) that ends at the tunnel to the roof and the tape (G1 p089). The hardest
   collectible is the last stop of a tour.
2. **Close in space, far in route.** The tape hangs ≈ 20 m from the spawn point, over the alley the player
   starts in, but the designed access is ≈ 90 s of play. Distance is created by access (a one-way secret
   corridor), not by placing the tape far away. The TAS proves the gap: 5 s by wallride climbing.
3. **A letter marks the door.** T is in the tunnel, so finishing S-K-A-T-E teaches the tape's access route
   and both are taken in one pass (G1 p089, p091).
4. **The roof bowl is a speed battery.** A roof-to-roof jump from a standing start would be a stats lottery;
   a bowl right before it turns the check into a skill the player already has (pump, then exit).
5. **One hub, several spokes.** Roof A's bowl and kicker lead to four destinations (Roof 2 Roof with the tape,
   Billboard Grind, Sucky Room Gap, and via roof B the Death Grind). The tape exit is one plausible choice
   among several; the remake disambiguates it with a painted arrow.
6. **Tape on the arc, not on a surface.** Placing it mid-gap means it can only be taken by committing to the
   transfer, and the same arc pays 2000 points (Roof 2 Roof): the risk is rewarded twice.
7. **Gap names hint at secrets.** T 2 T Gap, Secret Tunnel Entrance, Tunnel Gap, Over The Tunnel and Roof 2
   Roof are all on the gap checklist; a player reading the list learns that a tunnel and a second roof exist.
8. **Glass is the level's door material.** The tunnel window, the office windows and the sucky room are all
   entered by flying through glass, a rule taught in Warehouse (break the start-room window, G1 p045).
9. **Costly failure late in the run.** A short jump drops the skater into the alley, the farthest point by
   route from the tunnel; with the tape taken ≈ 90 s in, a miss costs the tape for that run. The spiral is
   therefore a time budget: G1 asks for "at least a minute left" at the last sign.
10. **Layered height.** Street → ledges and rails (≈ 1–5 m) → tunnel and skyway (≈ 6–12 m) → roofs
    (≈ 10–15 m). The tape is on the top layer, reached by the only continuous ramp between layers.
11. **The reward on the landing.** Roof B has a pool and rails "strategically arranged around it" (G1 p094)
    for the pro score; the remake adds stat points, the alien plushie and a gap goal there.
12. **Career progression.** Downtown's tape combines moves taught one at a time earlier (fly through glass,
    grind a rail chain, pump vert, transfer to a deck) into one route, and it is the first tape that needs a
    secret corridor rather than a single trick.
13. **One-run design.** Unlike Streets, everything fits in one 2-minute run if done in order (LP1 does it with
    the last sign at the buzzer).

## Transfer to this game

1. **Spiral to the centre (crystal over the spawn street).** Hang the crystal mid-air between two roofs
   directly above the spawn street, ≈ 10–12 m up. Order the level's other goals as a ring round the blocks
   that ends at a hidden entrance. Keep the straight-line distance short (≈ 20 m) and the route long.
2. **Ramp-shaped vehicle into a window.** A parked truck with a sloped bed aimed at a breakable window
   (≈ 2–3 m above the street; a charged ollie off the bed gives ≈ 1.8 m + ramp height) leads into an inclined
   corridor with a row of grindable barriers (spacing ≤ 2 m so grind ollies, popping ≈ 2.4 m, can chain them).
3. **Speed battery before the roof gap.** Put a bowl or halfpipe on the first roof and size the roof gap
   between this game's cruise ollie (≈ 9.4 m at 12 m/s) and the charged maximum (≈ 13.8 m at 23 m/s): ≈ 11–12 m
   lip to lip. The jump fails from a roll-in and succeeds after two or three pumps. Place the crystal at the
   arc apex, ≈ 1–1.5 m above lip height and ≈ 4–6 m out.
4. **Letter as the door marker.** Put the fifth level collectible (or a trail of shards) inside the corridor
   and make it visible through glass from the street below, so the secret area advertises itself.
5. **Hub with labelled spokes.** Give the roof bowl three exits (crystal gap, a trick rail to a billboard,
   a kicker into a glass room across the street). On easier settings mark the crystal exit with a painted
   arrow, as the remake does.
6. **Expert climb.** Leave a wallride-and-wallie climb up the alley wall (two wall panels ≈ 3 m apart
   vertically, each within a wallie's reach) so experts can skip the corridor, as the TAS does.
7. **Cheaper recovery than THPS.** If a miss should cost ≈ 15 s instead of ≈ 45 s, add a rope swing or speed
   pad from the alley back to the truck; keep the long route for first-time discovery.
