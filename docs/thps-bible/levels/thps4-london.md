## Spawn and first view

- **2002 PS2:** you start at the fountain square with the columned gallery and the fountains in view. The first goal-giver, Atiba ("Bust a phatty over them fountains"), stands right there (LP4 1:46:24). The S-K-A-T-E giver later says "Why don't you try and get those letters up there?" (LP4 1:49:12), so the letters are framed as being on the rooftops.
- **2025:** you face the column plinth with the QP at its base. The wire from the plinth top runs off to the right toward the rooftops (PS34 #32), and two fountains are in front (PS34 Benihana). **The tape is not shown in the spawn image.** It comes into view once you are on the wire (PS34 #33). The opening line (QP → wire) is the tape route.
- **PS1:** you start in the middle of the fountain (CC1), with the bus, the column and the stone building around you (VG START label). The column and its wires are the dominant shape in view.

**Route (PS34 and WEB, from spawn):**
1. Go straight to the **QP at the base of the column plinth** (PS34 #32 "go up the quarter pipe straight ahead").
2. Launch **up and to the right** and catch the **wire at the apex** of the air (WEB pcgamesn "catching the grind at the apex of your jump").
3. Grind the wire ("Yet Another Wire", PS34 #33) up and right across the square to the far rooftop. **The tape is visible ahead** during this grind.
4. On that rooftop (a roof "pool" with QP walls), **spine transfer up to the next, higher rooftop** (PS34 #34; WEB pcgamesn "spine transfer over to the next roof").
5. On the upper roof, **air off the QP or spine transfer again** to reach the tape floating above the coping (PS34 #35; WEB pcgamesn "spine transfer once more to collect the secret tape").

**LP34 human line (2:00:44–2:01:23):** plaza barriers and the lit plinth (2:00:53–2:00:55) → pavement rails "Kissed the Rail – Varial Heelflip" (2:00:58–2:01:02) → "Boneless" up a building face (2:01:04) → "540 Indy" at a rooftop edge (2:01:05–2:01:07) → rolls across a balustraded roof (2:01:10–2:01:19) → drops into a spine with "The O2" billboard in view ("Spine Transfer – Indy", 2:01:20) → blue pickup burst mid-air (2:01:22) → lands on the glowing skylight roof to "COMPLETE! Get the secret tape" (2:01:23). The last element, a rooftop spine transfer near the O2 billboard, is the same as in PS34.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The column is the beacon in every version.** The 2002 load card shows it (LP4 1:46:12). In PS1 its wires radiate to the balcony, the bus stop and the plaza ledges (VG). In 2025 its plinth QP is the first thing you face, and its wire carries you to the roofs (PS34 #32).
- **The 2025 tape is shown mid-route, not at the spawn.** It appears from the wire (PS34 #33), when you are already committed to the right line.
- **Night lighting marks the 2025 roofs.** The glowing skylight panels, searchlights and large billboards (O2, YETI/NIXON) light up the roof chain (LP34 2:01:08–2:01:24), so the route up reads as the lit path.
- **2002 letters are framed as being up high.** "Why don't you try and get those letters up there?" (LP4 1:49:12). The S is seen from the street high above the square (LP4 1:49:24).
- **PS1 uses score as a signpost.** The 1,500-point red wire is worth more than any other gap (100–300) and is coloured red (CC1).
- **Hidden on purpose:** the balcony in PS1 (reached only by a rail above a QP); the 2025 deck behind car-park glass and an elevator; the 2025 stat point behind restaurant glass.

## Design logic

1. **One landmark, three tapes' worth of logic.** All three versions build their best line on Nelson's column. 2002 already names wire and column gaps ("Yet Another Wire", "Ring Around the Column", "Nelson's Lip"). PS1 hangs its tape on the column wire. 2025 starts its tape route with the column's QP and wire.
2. **A lift answers the big map in 2025.** The tape is up on the roofs, but the route starts at the spawn: QP → wire turns flat speed into height in seconds. It is the London counterpart of Shipyard's door.
3. **Tease while riding.** The tape becomes visible during the uphill wire grind (PS34 #33), which tells the player the route is right before the spines.
4. **The vert finish is on the roofs.** The last 10 s are spine transfers between rooftop ramps (PS34 #34–#35; LP34). The roofs behave like a small vert park above the city.
5. **Score and secret reinforce each other in PS1.** The tape sits on a 1,500-point wire, 5–15 times any other gap, so a gap-hunter finds the tape and the tape-hunter scores the gap (CC1).
6. **The 2002 hard letters use two-pool geometry.** The T hangs between an upper and a lower rooftop pool and the E is reached by leaving the pool onto a facing ledge (THG). Pool-to-pool air plus a wallride exit is the original's hardest collectible move here.
7. **The river is the failure line.** The A and a cash icon sit on the bulb string over the river (LP4 1:50:40–1:50:42), and two water bails in 20 s (LP4 1:50:48, 1:50:54) show how the line punishes a slip.
8. **Cash marks lines, not corners.** The 2002 cash sits mid-line on the light string. The 2025 cash ends wire, arch and lamppost lines (PS34). Small rewards confirm that you are on a designed line.
9. **Moving people and vehicles are London's events.** There are buses to wallride, coppers to grab over, a police bike and a van to skitch, and a constable who relocates. Collectible-like targets move, so routes become interception problems (THG; PS34; LP34).
10. **Goals rehearse the tape route.** Constable Richard's last spot is reached by the same column-wire-roof line (LP34 2:02:56–2:03:31), and the 2025 S-K-A-T-E also runs QP → wire → roofs → spine (PS34). The tape becomes a variation on lines the player already knows.
11. **2025 hides secrets behind breakable glass and transitions.** The deck is behind car-park glass and an elevator; a stat point is behind restaurant glass (PS34). As in Shipyard (door), the remake uses transitions to fold large spaces.
12. **Day to night.** The 2002 London is daylit (LP4); the 2025 London is night and rain with the roofs lit (LP34). The lighting itself becomes route guidance.

## Original vs remake

- **Tape:** none in 2002. PS1 puts an unlisted tape on the red balcony-to-column wire (1,500 points). 2025 adds a listed tape above an upper-rooftop QP, reached from the column's QP and wire. Both later tapes depend on **the column wire**; the PS1 tape is on it, the 2025 tape at the far end of it.
- **Goals:** S-K-A-T-E, buses (5 → 2 in one combo), coppers (5 → 4 plus a moving Constable Richard), Free Stompy (4 nets → skitching a van's two doors), the fountains (Japan → Benihana) and the South Bank lights (amateur Ghetto Tag → pro full-gap) survive. The race, flatland goals, competition, "Nail the combos", the police-bike skitch and the 100k combo are dropped. The tape, Impress the Pro Skaters and Stompy plushies are added (THG; PS34).
- **Setting:** day (2002) → night with rain (2025, "Beautiful night, innit?"). The remake also places "THE ROYAL ZOO" in London: a "WELCOME TO THE ROYAL ZOO" sign appears in London (LP34 1:54:40) and the Zoo competition heat is labelled "Zoo – London" (LP34 4:29:50).
- **What it suggests:** the remake's tape design reuses the original's strongest lines (wire, rooftops, column) and makes the first move start at the spawn, rather than adding a new hidden room.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Monument lift.** A QP built against the base of a central monument launches the hero onto a sloped cable that climbs to the rooftops. Make the crystal visible from the cable. Finish on the roof with a spine transfer whose apex reaches the crystal ≈ 2–3 m above the coping (vert air plus spine transfer). Tune the cable so the grind ollie (≈ 2.4 m pop) is never required on it.
2. **Red wire.** Put the crystal on the most valuable or most decorated cable in the level, running from a hidden balcony (reached by vert air into a rail above a QP) to the central landmark. Give that cable a distinct colour and a big score so it reads as special.
3. **Two-pool drop.** Hang the crystal between an upper and a lower rooftop pool, ≈ 3–4 m above the lower coping. A charged ollie off the upper pool's lip (≈ 1.78 m high, up to ≈ 13.8 m long at the 23 m/s charge) carries the hero through it into the lower pool. Add a facing ledge for a wallride exit.
4. **River bulb string.** A long sagging grindable light string over water from a bridge, with a shard mid-line and the crystal at the lowest sag. A fall is a water respawn on the embankment, so retries are cheap.
5. **Moving bus platform.** A bus-sized moving platform on a loop that the hero can wallride and wallie off, with the crystal on a bus-stop roof only reachable from the moving bus's height.
