## Spawn and first view

You start at the NW corner beside a long wooden box with rails, facing E across the park (VG START; LP2 0:20:08 and 0:23:07, clock 1:00). The opening frame shows the Bluetorch stage with tricolour flags on the left, the bunting gate on the right, the main pool ahead and the distant bowls. The stage railing, with icon C1, is 16–21 m away and the obvious first target; LP2 takes it in 6 s. The crossbar (C2) is 32–41 m away across the main pool. The dead tree and the secret room are not visible from anywhere in the main park.

## Collectibles

THPS2 competitions have no tape, letters or goal list, but they do have **cash icons**, and the invite card makes them the level’s completion goal (“Find all cash icons to 100% the level”). Marseille has 8 icons worth $500 in total. Two are on the park’s highest grind objects; **six are inside the secret fountain room**, which you reach by knocking over a dead tree that exposes a warp pit. In this competition park, the secret room is what a tape would be in a career level. The remake replaces the cash icons with stat points, a logo and a plushie (the plushie also goes in the secret room).

**C1 — Cash icon $50** (original, value $50)

1. `ollie` spawn → stage front — roll N/NE to the stage (≈ 5 s)
2. `wallride` stage front wall → stage front — wallride the stage front (LP2 0:23:12 “Wall Ride”)
3. `wallie` wallride → railing on top — wallie up onto the railing (0:23:13 “Wall ride + Wallie”, “50 bucks”)
4. `grind` railing → railing end — BS 50-50 along the railing (0:23:14)
5. `grind` railing → dumpsters / speakers or rail below — optional: drop into Dumpster Stomp 1000 or Kink Stomp 1000 (0:23:16)

- Launch from: stage front wall (wallride, then wallie). Distance from first sighting: ≈ 16–21 (VG).
- Skill gates: wallride + wallie onto a railing. Coupling: none.
- Failure and recovery: A missed wallie drops you at the stage foot; ≈ 3 s to retry.
- Difficulty 2: one wallride + wallie close to spawn
- Why: The nearest icon teaches the wallride → wallie → grind chain on the park’s landmark; the gaps on the same railing (1000 each) pay for continuing the line.
- Evidence: VG; LP2 0:23:11–0:23:16; PS12 (Kink/Dumpster Stomp) (confidence high).

**C2 — Cash icon $100** (original, value $100)

2. `vert-air` main pool → main pool — pump for speed
3. `vert-air` pool wall under the crossbar → air — air out of the E wall below the crossbar (PS12: “use a quarter pipe”)

- Launch from: main-pool wall under the crossbar. Distance from first sighting: ≈ 32–41 from spawn (VG).

- Failure and recovery: Missing the bar drops you back into the pool or the east bowls; re-pump at once.
- Difficulty 3: vert air into a grind on a thin bar
- Why: The highest grind object in the open park spans the hip between the two bowl districts, and its icon is worth double a railing’s.
- Evidence: VG; LP2 0:23:24–0:23:32; PS12 (Crossbar Stomp) (confidence high).

## Guidance and sightlines

- The Bluetorch stage, with its flags and speaker stacks, fills the left of the opening view; its railing carries the nearest icon.
- Bunting and pennants mark the overhead bars (gate, crossbar, lampposts). Everything with bunting is a grind target.
- Cash icons are floating banknotes; each pickup prints “50 bucks” or “100 bucks!” on screen (LP2).
- Hidden on purpose: the dead tree stands on a grass strip outside the bowls, behind a wooden fence. Nothing points to it (LP2 0:23:41–0:23:50).
- Arrival feedback: the warp prints “Where am I?” (LP2 0:23:53), confirming the discovery.
- Inside the room, the fountain sits in the centre and the icons ring the rim, so the reward layout reads at a glance.
- Sunset lighting (LP2) silhouettes the crossbar and lampposts against the sky.

## Design logic

1. A competition park becomes an exploration level through a single rule printed on the invite: “Find all cash icons to 100% the level.”
2. The secret area holds 6 of the 8 icons ($350 of $500). The hidden room is not a bonus on the side; it is the collectible hub.
3. The trigger is physical and in the world (a dead tree) and sits off the obvious skating surface. You must leave the bowls and cross the fence line, which tests curiosity rather than skill.
4. The warp detaches the reward room from the park map (VG draws it apart). A hidden area does not need to be physically adjacent to its trigger.
5. The reward room is a mini-park. Every icon is on a distinct element (stair rail, fountain, rim ledges), and a wallride gap rings the wall, so the room is a self-contained combo course.
6. The two open-park icons sit on the park’s two highest grind objects: the stage railing (wallride + wallie) and the bunting crossbar (air from the pool). Height and value line up.
7. Value tracks difficulty and centrality: $100 for the crossbar and for the fountain at the room’s centre, $50 for railings and ledges.
8. The nearest icon teaches the verbs. C1 is 6 s from spawn and needs exactly the wallride → wallie → grind chain that the stage’s gaps then pay 1000 each for.
9. Bunting is a visual grammar: every bar with pennants (gate, crossbar, lampposts) is grindable and scored (250–2000).
10. One heat, one secret visit. LP2 spends heat 3 on loot: stage icon (6 s), crossbar (23 s), warp (46 s), room icons until the buzzer. The 1:00 heat fits exactly one full collection pass, and best-2-of-3 judging lets one heat be a loot heat.
11. Tiered hang-time gaps on one narrow quarterpipe (Up! 250, Up!! 500, And Away!!! 1000) teach that height itself is scored.
12. Failure is cheap: misses drop you into a bowl. The only costly mistake is time, because the secret room is ≈ 45 s from spawn and is reached through a one-way warp.
13. The remake keeps the secret room and puts the plushie in it, adds manual gaps (Steppin Out, The Trashman Gap, Waking the dead) and bowl-to-street transfers (Le Rail Saut, Le Dumpster Saut), and drops cash icons.
14. The remake’s logo crowns a bunting crossbar: the original’s high-object grammar is kept for the new collectible.

## Original vs remake

- Cash icons: PS12 lists none for the remake. Its collectibles are 2 stat points per style, the VV logo and the alien plushie; LP12’s level panel shows only “Stat Points 0/2” under Collectibles.
- The secret area remains; PS12 says it opens by “knocking into the wooden pole on the right” and puts the plushie in its corner beneath the slope.
- New gaps: Waking the dead (secret-area manual), Steppin Out, The Trashman Gap (manuals), Le Rail Saut and Le Dumpster Saut (bowl → street grinds).
- Competition text: “To earn a medal, finish with one of the Top 3 Scores. The average of your top two runs is your final Score for this competition.” (LP12 0:48:45).
- LP12 shows the remake’s “Overtime!” finish (0:49:55).

## Transfer to this game

1. Dead-tree trick gate: a lone dead tree on a grass strip outside the main bowls is a trick gate (or a snapping-rope prop) that falls when hit and exposes a pit. The pit drops into a separate fountain room, and the crystal floats above the fountain, collected with one ollie. Print a discovery line on arrival.
2. Crossbar over the hip: span a bunting bar across the hip between two bowl districts. The crystal sits mid-span and is reached by vert air out of the bowl into a grind (2.1 m snap radius). A miss falls back into the bowl.
3. Stage railing opener: put a stage with a wallride-able front and a railing on top near spawn. Wallride → wallie → grind the railing; use it as the teaching line that points at the crystal route rather than as the crystal itself.
4. Room of rims: if the crystal is in a secret room, ring the room with curved ledges so a circling grind line is the natural way to explore it, and place the crystal where the ring meets the room’s centrepiece.
5. Timing budget: keep the secret room within about 45 s of spawn on a direct route, so one run can include the discovery and the exit.
