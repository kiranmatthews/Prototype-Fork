Layers: floor; funboxes, stepped boxes, stair sets; quarter pipes, the big half pipe, the corner vert ramp and the bowls; decks and platforms (half-pipe deck, observation platform); overhead lights, sprinkler pipes, the speaker shelf and wires; outside, the ship deck, masts, beams and crow's nest. Heights are not measured.

Circulation: the indoor park loops round its perimeter of wall QPs (Heartbreaker / Jersey Nun coping lap). The beach and ship are a dead-end annex reached through the doors; after the cannon blast a second link (2x4 → palm tree) joins inside and outside.

## Spawn and first view

Spawn is in the street course (TAS3 5:02: by a chain-link fence near the start funbox). G3 p104: double heelflip over the nearest funbox, second funbox, then manual up the vert ramp on the left. Hawk's stat point is directly behind the start (G3 p108; J3 p021). The big half pipe and the flags that open the secret area are across the building, not in the first view (inferred from G3 p107 map).

## Guidance and sightlines

- The switch is a flag in plain view of the half pipe, next to the US flag; both are gaps, so the player is likely to grind them while skating the pipe (G3 p105, p112).
- The cutscene when you first go outside shows the ship; the cannon blast makes the event obvious by damaging the park (G3 p104-p105).
- Every skater group's stat point is on the bow beam: one shared destination that draws everyone to the ship (G3 p105, p108).
- Overhead lines (lights above the start, sprinklers, speaker shelf, wires) are the hidden layer for decks and stat points.
- Remake: the logo is in the crow's nest and the respawn after jumping in the water takes you there (PS34).

## Design logic

1. No tape in a competition, but a whole hidden stage: the pirate ship is the competition's exploration reward (G3 p105; PS34 all-tapes list omits Skater Island).
2. A decorative switch: the flag that opens the doors is a scored gap on the half pipe's natural line, so players can find it by chance (G3 p112).
3. The event has a cost: going outside makes the pirates fire and destroys part of the street course. G3 documents the alternative deck route for the damaged park and suggests staying inside to keep it intact (G3 p105-p106).
4. Falling as transport: the bow-beam stat point drops you in the sea, and the respawn puts you high on the mast, the only way to reach some ropes and beams (G3 p105). The remake hangs a collectible on that mechanic (PS34).
5. A graded transfer family (At the 50/10/5/Touchdown!) teaches distance control on one geometry and pays 20× for the extreme version (G3 p111).
6. Exploring costs score: the human spent a heat reaching the bow and scored 0.0 / 9.5 / 0.0 from the judges (LP3 0:50:35). Competition hides reward exploration, not the medal.
7. One clean combo wins: the TAS takes gold with two 3-second heats from the start funbox and the wall QP next to it (TAS3 5:02-5:16).
8. Real-park fidelity: G3 says the 20,000 sq ft park was "faithfully recreated" (p103); the fantasy layer (beach, ship, cannons) is added behind a door.
9. A side-by-side transition classroom: peanut bowl with hips (deep and shallow ends), mini snakerun, vert-less shallow pool, a pool next to the big half pipe and an immense corner vert ramp (G3 p103).
10. Through-gaps teach aiming: Drive Thru Window (through the platform window), Rollerboy (through the platform opening), Sail Rip (through the sail), Touchdown! (into the half pipe's flat) (G3 p111-p114).
11. The secret area loops back: the ship has ropes from its mast beams down to the beach (Shore Leave, Land Lubber) and a beam grind back aboard from the bow (Front Mast Back Grind), so it is a small park with its own exits (G3 p114).

## Original vs remake

THPS 3+4 keeps the park, the flag switch and the ship. The PS1 edition has no ship.

- Still a medal competition; no tape added (PS34).
- Platinum pro goal: lip trick on the mast next to the pirate skeleton (PS34).
- Collectibles added: cash ×3 (one on the palm tree outside), hidden deck on a wire from the back-left corner QP, Iron Galaxy logo in the crow's nest (reached by jumping in the water from the bowsprit and respawning there), panda behind the small boat, stat points ×3 (one on the bowsprit) (PS34).
- LP34 shows the ship visited during heats (0:39:50-0:40:30) and a "Several Days Later" title card during a heat (0:41:00), likely the remake's version of the water respawn (inferred; low confidence).
- PS1 edition (VG): the same warehouse park (half pipe with a logo, peanut bowl with a skull logo, funboxes, cables), but the outside area is an asphalt lot with a wooden funbox, a wooden ramp, curbs and cables from the roof; no beach and no ship.

## Transfer to this game

1. Flag switch crystal: hang two flags on a wire beside a big half pipe; grinding the skull flag opens a gate to an outdoor area with a ship; the crystal sits at the end of the bowsprit (grind out, then ollie off the end to collect).
2. Fall-to-climb: falling into the sea respawns the player on the mast top; from there, ropes (snapping or swinging) and beams reach a second crystal-style reward that cannot be reached any other way.
3. Distance ladder: a pool beside a half pipe with gems placed at 3, 6 and 9 m into the pipe, the last needing a full-speed transfer (≈ 13.8 m at maximum charge).
4. Consequence event: opening the outdoor area triggers cannons that destroy one set of ramps inside; leave a documented fallback line (lights → wall rail) so the indoor reward stays reachable.
