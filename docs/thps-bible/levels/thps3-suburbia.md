G3 page numbers are scan indices (printed page = scan − 1). Orientation follows G3's PS2 map (p075): the
cul-de-sac in the centre; trailer park north-west; gray house with the "nude" bowl north-east; the Richey's
orange-roofed house and its backyard mini-park east; construction site and a two-lobed pool south; the
haunted house on a rise to the west. VG coordinates are pixels of the PS1 render (2843 × 1764), a different
layout in which the mansion stands outside the play area to the north.

Layers, low to high: the pools and bowls; the street and the dirt lot; ledges, walls and funboxes
(≈ 0.5–2 m); trailers, the fire truck, porch roofs and fences (≈ 2–4 m); house roofs, the haunted front yard
on its rise and the flagpole (≈ 5–12 m above the street); power lines between the roofs; the haunted roof
half pipe and steeple (highest). Heights are estimates; low confidence.

Loops: the cul-de-sac is the hub; every area opens off its ring (trailer park, gray house, Richey's house,
construction site, haunted house). The power lines link the gray house, the Richey's roof and the haunted
house across the sac at roof height.

Route (designed, G3 p073–p074; observed in LP3 0:27:28–0:27:53):

0. **Prerequisite — Help the Thin Man** (G3 p074). At the construction site, grind the row of wood on
   sawhorses nearest the fire truck to pick up an axe (TAS3 2:50 "You've found an axe!" during Plywood Pop).
   Take it to the Thin Man on the path from the trailer park up to the haunted house; he chops the barricade
   (LP34 0:28:36–0:28:46 cutscene). J3 p016–p017: he asks for a key, but an axe "would do better".
1. **Ollie up the haunted front stairs to the door** (ollie). LP3 0:27:28–0:27:31.
2. **Roll down the hallway to the wall at its end** (LP3 0:27:32–0:27:34). G3: "At first it appears that the
   hallway leads to a dead end."
3. **Transfer over the wall on the right into the backyard** (transfer). G3 p073 calls it the Haunted Backyard
   Transfer; the gap list names it I Got a Bad Feeling About This! (100). LP3 0:27:34–0:27:36.
4. **Trick off the back wall of the yard to gain speed** (vert-air). LP3 0:27:37–0:27:48 links "540 Cannonball +
   I Got A Bad Feeling About This! + BS Revert" into one combo and takes a stat point (0:27:45).
5. **Leap up and over the tall ramp against the back of the house and crash through the upstairs window**
   (kicker, break-glass). LP3 0:27:49–0:27:52; J3: "climb with speed onto the convex part facing it: you break
   a window and go along a corridor".
6. **Bats appear; slow down and align with the centre of the upstairs hallway** (G3). LP3 0:27:52 (timer 0:07):
   a room with a large gridded window and bats.
7. **Leap through the glass windows at the front** (break-glass).
8. **Land in a grind on the flagpole** (grind): "SECRET TAPE! COMPLETE", LP3 0:27:53 ("FS Nosegrind + Spooky
   Drop", timer 0:06).
9. **Ollie off the end of the pole** (Spooky Drop, 100) into the front yard and the sac.

Remake route (PS34; LP34 0:28:48–0:28:58): into the house, over the wall to the right by its quarter pipe,
"skate up the side of the house and go through the hall", the glass, the flagpole — the same sequence.

### C-O-M-B-O letters

None in the original (G3 p070, LP3 goal cards, VG PS1 goals); the goal type belongs to THPS4 (G4m p002, p004).
The 2025 remake adds it as a pro goal (PS34): from the start, cross to the fence and break through it (the gray
house's broken fence); use the quarter pipe on the right to get up and grind (C); hop to the fence (O); drop
down and manual (M); go across to the handrail that leads up to the roof; at the top, jump off to the right
for the last letter(s). A grind → fence → manual → handrail-to-roof chain, i.e. the gray-house line of G3 p071
rebuilt as a letter line.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The mansion overlooks the hub.** The haunted house stands on a rise at the west edge of the sac; its
  flagpole faces the circle every line crosses (inferred from LP3 0:27:53's view).
- **The NPC points at the door.** The Thin Man stands on the path to the boarded house; the goal card shows
  him by the gate (LP3 0:23:21–0:23:22). The axe is an item the HUD tracks (G3 p074).
- **Letters and lines lead to the front wall.** G1's T is on the wall in front of the house; G3's E above its
  side banked wall; G3's long scoring line ends at the house; the fifth pumpkin is on its porch.
- **Inside, glass marks the way.** The upstairs window is the target of the backyard ramp; the front glass
  frames the pole ("On the other side of the glass ahead", PS34).
- **Hidden on purpose:** the hallway "seems to lead to a dead end" (G3 p074); the backyard and the upstairs
  are out of sight until the wall transfer.

## Design logic

1. **Locked-house tape.** The tape hangs on the front of the building that overlooks the hub, but can only be
   reached from inside, after an errand. It is the first THPS3 tape behind a fetch quest.
2. **The errand tours the level.** The axe is at the construction site (south), the Thin Man by the trailer
   park path (west): the errand crosses the sac and introduces two areas.
3. **An inside-out loop.** In at the front door, through the hallway, out to the back, up the back wall, in at
   the top, out through the front window: one building, walked through completely.
4. **Speed, then precision.** The backyard gives speed for the tall ramp; the hallway asks to "slow down and
   align" before the last jump to the pole (G3 p073).
5. **Glass as doors.** Two breakable windows bracket the upstairs; each is a commitment point.
6. **A soft finish at the start.** The pole is over the front yard by the start tree; a miss or the exit (Spooky
   Drop) returns the player to the hub.
7. **Lines rehearse the route.** G3's long scoring line ends with the wall transfer into the backyard (step 3)
   and Look Out Behind You! (the backyard wall launch).
8. **The revert links the backyard.** LP3 keeps the combo through the yard with a revert after a 540
   cannonball; the tape's speed-building step is THPS3's new vert-to-manual link in action.
9. **A secret area that is a park.** The backyard has its own gaps (six), a stat point and the launch to the
   roof wires; the tape route uses its geometry rather than adding a special path.
10. **Progression across THPS3's first three tapes.** Foundry: no unlock, open upper layer. Canada: a switch on
    the route. Suburbia: a fetch quest across the level and a building interior.
11. **The PS1 edition had no interior.** Its tape floats over the sac by a boom; the PS2 build moved the
    challenge into a gated building with an NPC.
12. **The remake keeps the route and fills the house.** Cash, a plushie and a stat point in the backyard, and
    a periodic door into "the Abyss" (pro goal).

## Transfer to this game

1. **Locked-house crystal.** A two-storey house on a rise at the hub's edge, with a short pole (grindable,
   ≈ 3 m long) projecting from the upper floor over the front yard; the crystal on the pole, ≥ 7 m above the
   yard below.
2. **Errand gate.** The door is boarded; an NPC opens it when the player brings an item found on a rail at the
   far side of the level (pickup shown on the HUD).
3. **Inside-out route.** Hallway → a wall to vert-transfer over (quarter pipe + vert-air) → a bowl-like
   backyard → a tall launch ramp into a smashable upper window → a short hallway → smashable front window →
   pole grind (snap radius 2.1 m makes the short pole fair).
4. **Speed then calm.** Put a speed pad or bowl wall before the launch ramp; make the upstairs hallway short
   and straight so players line up before the window.
5. **Soft exit.** The fall from the pole lands in the front yard next to the spawn.
6. **Keep walkers off.** The porch roof below the pole must be unstandable or ≥ 3 m below it with no grab edge;
   the pole ≥ 6.5 m above any standable surface outside the house.
