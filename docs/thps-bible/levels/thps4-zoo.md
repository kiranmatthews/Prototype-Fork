**Route (THG + LP4):**
1. Go to the elephant enclosure. The trainer warns about Stompy's head (LP4 2:24:00).
2. Ride up onto the **snack hut's sloped roof** beside the enclosure (LP4 2:24:39–2:24:45; THG "the roof of the snack hut near Nelly the elephant").

## Guidance and sightlines

- **NPC hints.** The Elephant Trainer warns you away from Stompy's head (LP4 2:24:00), and that is where the trigger is. The Lion Tamer and Zookeeper announce events (LP4 2:16:08, 2:17:08, 2:25:36).
- **Banners visible from the start.** Three of the four banner goals can be seen from the goal start (THG).
- **Animals as markers.** Hippos mark the A, the elephant marks the aquarium trigger, and the lions and Kong mark the pro events. You find the places by finding the animals.
- **2025:** night arena with lit bowls and a clock tower; the penguins sit above QPs and in the middle of open spaces (PS34).

## Design logic

1. **No tape in either version.** The 2002 Zoo's secret-area role is played by the **locked aquarium**, and the remake turns Zoo into a competition without a tape (PS34). In the 2025 tour Zoo is one of three tape-less THPS4 stops; PS34's tape list skips Movie Studio, Kona and Zoo.
2. **Animals are the machinery.** Where Shipyard has cranes and London has buses, Zoo has hippos (a trampoline-like boost for the A), an elephant (skitch; the aquarium trigger), lions (chase), Kong (switches), birds (combo scare) and Kenny (a moving mascot). Collectibles and unlocks are tied to animal behaviour.
3. **The hint is in dialogue, not geometry.** The aquarium trigger is near the elephant's head, and the trainer's warning points there (LP4 2:24:00). Players are told where not to go, which is exactly where the secret is.
4. **One locked room, used once.** Two of five penguins are inside the aquarium (THG), so the unlock pays off immediately, much as the remake's Alcatraz tape repays its unlock.
5. **The highest letter crowns the cage climb.** The E is in the air at the edge of the monkey-cage roof, reached by wallride, QP launch, grind and roof hop (THG). The letter sequence teaches the climb.
6. **Water and pools as soft failure.** The hippo water and the brown pools give cheap retries; a miss costs a bail and seconds, never a climb.
7. **Pro goals go higher.** The pro C-O-M-B-O strings roof → wire → bridge → hut roof → lion-den wall → small roof (THG). The pro tier uses the roof and wire layer that the amateur goals only touch.
8. **Remake: secrets on the wire and wall layers.** The 2025 hidden deck is on the wire between two rooftops and the IG logo is on an upper platform reached by wallride/wallie (PS34). Even in a competition level the collectibles teach the wire and wall layers.
9. **2025 platinum penguins are a tour of the arena.** The five penguins hit the aviary, the half pipe, the enclosure, the clock tower and a smashed barricade (PS34). It is a collect-five circuit inside a 1-minute heat (LP34 4:34:20–4:35:15).
10. **Goal starts and skitches fold a big map.** The Pro Score goal starts you on the snack-bar roof (THG; LP4 2:15:32), which shows a roof layer without a climb. Whether it is the same snack hut that triggers the aquarium is not confirmed. LP4 also skitches a zoo cart to reach the aquarium quickly after the trigger (2:25:20–2:25:24). These are THPS4's two answers to distance: goal-start placement and skitching.
11. **Escape stories justify roaming targets.** Penguins, lions, the monkey and Kong have all "escaped" (LP4 2:11:08, 2:17:08, 2:25:36, 3:12:18). The fiction turns collectibles into moving or scattered targets and gives each a reason to be in an odd place.

## Original vs remake

- **2002:** a free-roam street level with 16 + 5 goals, animals as moving pieces, and a secret aquarium opened by jumping onto the elephant.
- **2025:** a **competition** arena ("Zoo – London", "THE ROYAL ZOO", LP34 4:29:50), redesigned at night with bowls, rocks and a clock tower. ## Transfer to this game

1. **Animal trampoline.** Moving creatures in a pool act as trampolines (this engine has trampolines) to cross water. Put a letter or the crystal above them at a height only reachable with the bounce, ≈ 1–2 m above a plain ollie's 1.78 m.
2. **Gate behind a big animal.** A large moving platform (an animal) beside a sloped roof. Jumping onto it from the roof triggers a trick gate or unlock that opens a closed room with the crystal. An NPC line warns the player away from the spot.
3. **Cage-roof crown.** Wallride a booth wall, vert-air off a QP on the cage's outer wall into a grind, hop onto the cage roof, and place the crystal in the air past the roof's far edge (a grind ollie of ≈ 2.4 m reaches it).
4. **Lion-house launch.** A QP transfer onto a small roof between QPs, then a kicker over a cage wall, with the crystal over the cage. A charged ollie (≈ 9.4–13.8 m long) clears a 6–8 m cage.
5. **Chimney switches.** Two lip-stall switches inside a vertical chute, a trick gate opened by a named lip trick, which drop a cage or open a hatch to the crystal.
