# THPS2 — Chopper Drop (Hawaii) (bonus level)

Sources: `VG` TonyHawk_sProSkater2-ChopperDrop-Hawaii.png (whole map at native 1018 × 1150; halfpipe
area upscaled ×1.6); `PS12 chopper-drop` (remake gap list with points, VV logo, alien plushie); `LP12`
1:12:05–1:12:45 (remake: intro, drop-in and about 25 s of play at 5 s sampling); `WEB absolutcheats` and
`WEB gamegenie` (PS1 unlock); `WEB trophy-tom` (remake unlock; no goals). LP2 does not visit this level.

Evidence level: the original has **no goals, no tape and no cash icons** (VG shows no checklist and no
icons). The level is one giant halfpipe with a hovering helicopter. This file records its layout, its
calibrated "reach" gaps (the helicopter, the potato rungs, the distance gaps) and the remake's two
pickups. No source shows any gap being performed.

## Spawn and first view

- You start in or at the **helicopter** (VG START at the helicopter). The remake opens inside the cabin
  looking out at the sea, cuts to the helicopter hovering over the mast with the skater at the door, and
  then puts you in the halfpipe at 1:56 (LP12 1:12:10–1:12:20).
- In view: the whole level, which is one halfpipe, the mast and rungs, and the helicopter overhead.
  Every target is visible from the start.

## Guidance and sightlines

- Everything is in view from the start, which suits a level built around calibrated targets:
  the rungs are bright yellow, the helicopter hovers overhead, and the distance deck is labelled in feet.
- The gap names describe the action ("Into the Heli", "Heli Grind"). The potato count rises with rung
  height.

## Design logic

1. **One structure, many calibrated targets.** With no checklist, the level turns a single halfpipe
   into a set of graded challenges: height (rungs), distance (deck) and precision (the cabin).
2. **Ladders make progress readable:** 1/2/3 Potato for 100/200/400 and 70/80/90 ft for 100/200/300
   double the reward for each step up in height.
3. **A hovering vehicle as the showpiece target:** air *into* an object, not just over it. It is the
   level's equivalent of a floating tape.
4. **Failure cost is nil.** A miss falls back into the pipe, so the level is a practice space for max
   vert.
5. **The remake places its pickups at the extremes** of the one structure (the top of the tower, the
   top of the low deck), using the geometry's range instead of adding new geometry.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Height-ladder crystal stand.** Beside a pumpable halfpipe, stack three trick rails ("rungs") above
   the coping at rising heights. The crystal floats just above the top rung, so only a fully pumped
   vert air plus a grind ollie off the top rung (≈ 2.4 m pop) reaches it. The lower rungs are the
   practice steps.
2. **Air into a moving object.** Hover a moving platform (a helicopter) beside the halfpipe on a short
   loop and put the crystal inside its cabin. The rider must time a vert air or transfer to the
   platform's position.
3. **Distance deck.** A detached landing deck with marked distances, so a launch shows how far it fell
   short. Put the crystal at the far mark (≈ 27 m), reachable only with a pad or kicker plus a full
   charged ollie (≈ 13.8 m at the 23 m/s maximum, so add a speed pad or ramp lip).
4. **Use the structure's extremes.** One halfpipe can hold a crystal at its tallest point (vert test)
   and a small pickup on its lowest deck (exploration), with no new geometry.
