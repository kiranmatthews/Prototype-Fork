Layers: water (fail plane) < pier decks and road < plaza steps, benches, curved steps < patio level 2 and tram-stop kickers < overhead walkway and wires (≈ 5-6 m) < upper hotel terraces and warehouse roofs < hotel roof. Loops: the road circulates from the hotel end to the pier end; the pier is a cul-de-sac reached by the waterfront and by wires across the water.

## Spawn and first view

**Remake.** The goal flythrough shows the S on a building corner above ivy, a package by a "For Lease" storefront with a QP, and Painter Neal's shark splash (LP34 1:13:05-1:13:15). The run starts in a palm-lined plaza by a wooden kicker structure (LP34 1:13:20). PS34 directions from the start: the hotel with the spiral slopes is immediately to the left (S, tape, IG logo), the rooftop QPs are ahead to the right (cash, stat point), the alley with a bus is ahead then right (package 1). The tape is therefore 20-30 s from the spawn and on the first building the player is pointed at.

**Original (PS2).** After the "Unlock San Francisco?" prompt and loading (LP4 0:25:20-0:25:24) the skater stands on a wide paved waterfront plaza beside a streetcar shelter, warehouses and piers ahead (LP4 0:25:28). A competition judge and the competition ramps (wooden quarter pipes with sponsor banners) are a few seconds away (LP4 0:25:32-0:26:20), and THG says the first BS Boneless pier sign is "right in front of you when you start". The first view therefore sells the waterfront and the competition, while the hotel block that later holds the remake tape stays behind and to the side.

**PS1.** VG marks START on the curving road beside the hotel block, next to a stat-point ball, with goal 7 and 8 icons a few metres away.

**Route — TAPE-R**

1. **ride** — remake spawn plaza → hotel steps. From the start go left to the hotel (PS34); LP34 arrives across the fountain plaza (1:14:07-1:14:10).
2. **ride** — spiral ramp → patio (level 2). Ride up the curved concrete spiral ramp under the hotel sign to the patio (LP34 1:14:11-1:14:13; PS34 screenshot 29).
3. **ride** — patio → corner QP. Cross the patio left to the quarter pipe in the corner; the tape is visible above (LP34 1:14:14-1:14:19; package 4 sits on this QP).
4. **transfer** — corner QP → upper terrace. Air the QP and transfer right onto the upper terrace walkway (PS34: "use the quarter pipe to transfer to an upper level to the right"; LP34 1:14:20-1:14:23).
5. **ride** — upper terrace walkway → tape corner. Ride the walkway past the planters and glass panels; LP34 airs a QP at the end (540 Melon, 1:14:30) to turn back along the facade.
6. **wallride** — stone wall beside the planter → tape. Wallride the wall and wallie off it into the tape above the planter (LP34 1:14:35 "Wallride + Wallie + Impossible", COMPLETE at 8:45; WEB gamerant method).
7. **ledge** — planter ledge → patio edge. Land in a nosegrind on the planter ledge (LP34 1:14:36) and continue.

**Alternative route — hotel sign → planter (Push Square, TheGamer, Screen Rant)** (PS34; WEB thegamer; WEB screenrant; WEB operationsports)

1. **transfer** — patio corner QP → upper level to the right. Transfer up as above.
2. **ollie** — upper level → hotel sign. Hop the gap and grind the angled hotel sign slab.
3. **rail-transfer** — hotel sign → planter. Hop the next gap into a grind on the planter; the tape sits over it (PS34 screenshot 32 shows a BS 50-50 on the planter with the pier beyond).

## Guidance and sightlines

- Remake flythrough shows S, a package and the Painter Neal event before the run (LP34 1:13:05-1:13:15).
- The tape is visible from the hotel patio above the next tier (LP34 1:14:15-1:14:16): the player sees the goal and must work out the tier change.
- Big signage frames the tape building: the "Boardwalk View Hotel" sign over the spiral ramp (PS34 screenshot 29; LP34 1:14:12).
- Spiral ramps with glass rails read as an invitation upward; planters and glass panels mark the terrace edges as grindable.
- Original goal givers stand at the waterfront (Painter Bob, judges, fishermen); the first pier sign sits in front of the spawn (THG).
- Water is the fail plane and is always visible, which makes the pier wires and the T wire feel risky (LP4 0:37:36).
- Hidden on purpose: hotel terraces (reachable only by transfer or wallride), the warehouse roof chain, the remake IG logo behind glass.

## Design logic

1. The remake tape is on the first building the spawn points the player to ("go left immediately"), so it is a skill test rather than a search: visible, close, one tier out of reach.
2. Tiered building: each level of the hotel has a different entry move (ramp, QP transfer, wallride + wallie), and the tape sits on the last tier; this teaches vertical route-reading in a city level.
3. Two valid tape solutions (wallride + wallie up the wall, or grind sign → planter): the tape accepts either a vertical or a horizontal approach.
4. The PS1 port drew its tape on the same building's upper ledge (VG); the remake keeps the building and the idea, which suggests Iron Galaxy reused the earlier tape concept.
5. Original letters build a roof chain from the waterfront: QP → curved ledge → QP-to-roof → roof gap → wire → roof edge. The only fall risk is water, which costs ≈ 20 s on a 2:00 clock (LP4 0:37:36).
6. Wires are placed over the road (pro COMBO, camera race, remake COMBO) and over the water (PS1 COMBO, remake Rude Dudes): the higher the stakes below, the later in the career the goal.
7. Goal-object routes (packages) teach the facade ledge grammar later reused by pro goals (manual setup, pads).
8. Timed unlocks (red alarm button) make the pro goal a race against a guard, a different kind of difficulty from height.
9. The level is long and linear along the waterfront; the remake's 10:00 run gives time to commute, and LP34 cleared all goals with ≈ 6 minutes to spare (1:17:35).
10. Lines repeat at different heights (benches → ledges → walkway → wires → roofs), so a player learns one grammar and climbs it.
11. The pier is a cul-de-sac with high-value goals (signs, sea lions, Neal); the remake routes its pro wire across the water to make the cul-de-sac a loop.
12. THPS4's new verbs stay off the tape routes: the remake tapes in College, San Francisco and Alcatraz need grinds, QP transfers, wallride + wallie, a window rail and (College) a portal door, while skitching drives goals (bike-messenger race here, the Professor's car, Ranger Ron) and the wallplant appears only in Save Painter Neal!!! (PS34; THG). Big maps are handled by flythroughs, portals and 10:00 runs rather than by harder moves.

## Original vs remake

- Tape: none in the 2002 PS2 career; the PS1 port drew one on the hotel's upper ledge (VG); the remake places it above an upper-terrace planter of the "Boardwalk View Hotel" (LP34 1:14:35).
- S moves from a walkway QP launch (original) to a QP on the hotel's spiral level (remake); K from the curved steps to a wooden rail in the square; A, T and E keep the roof-QP → wire → roof-jump chain (THG vs PS34).
- C-O-M-B-O becomes a pro goal on wires from the tram-stop kicker (remake), similar to the original's pro wires from a stone slope.
- BS Boneless the 3 pier signs becomes BS Boneless a (single) Pier Sign; Feed the sea lions becomes Get Rid of the Rude Dudes (seagulls); Save Painter Neal adds a wallplant on the ball (THG vs PS34).
- Race to get the camera becomes Retrieve the Stolen Camera; the manual setup keeps the named gaps On the Way, Cleared the Gap and Made It! (PS34).
- Remake adds cash, stat points, hidden deck (ramp with arrows), an IG logo behind glass, and a panda on a roof.

## Transfer to this game

1. Tiered-terrace crystal: build a three-tier building facing the spawn; tier 1 by a curved ramp, tier 2 by a quarter-pipe transfer, tier 3 only by wallride + wallie (or a grind hop along a sign). Put the crystal 2-3 m above the tier-3 planter so a wallie (not a plain ollie, ≈ 1.78 m) is required.
2. Show it early: make the crystal visible from tier 2 against the facade so the player plans the last move; keep the fall short (one tier, ≈ 4 m) so retries are cheap.
3. Horizontal alternative: a slanted sign slab as a grind rail from the tier-2 roof edge to the planter, crossing one gap with a grind ollie (≈ 2.4 m pop).
4. Water fail plane: put a mid-level collectible on a cable over water; falling costs a respawn, raising stakes without raising height.
5. Guard gate: a wallride-triggered switch that opens a ledge line for a few seconds (alarm and returning guard) works as a timed trick gate in our toolkit.
