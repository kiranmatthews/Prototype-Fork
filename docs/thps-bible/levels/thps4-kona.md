Layers: bowl bottoms < park ground and street course < pool copings, hut roofs (≈ 3 m) and halfpipe decks (≈ 3.5-4 m) < power lines above the halfpipe (≈ 5-7 m) < large-building roof (≈ 6-8 m) and the GIGA KONA tower (PS1 vert goals at 30 and 60 feet ≈ 9 and 18 m). The park is a loop: the snake runs flow down into the pools and the open area, and paths and handrails lead back up.

Kona is the first pure skatepark of THPS4: the hard-to-reach layer is above the ramps (wires over the halfpipe, hut roofs, the large-building roof), not inside buildings.

## Spawn and first view

**Original.** "Kona USA" loads (LP4 1:06:24) onto the top of the snake area: concrete with teal-blue coping falls away ahead, and the first person in view is Martin Ramos, who offers the Snake Run Slalom (LP4 1:06:32). THG's directions all start "head down the pathway that you're facing": the S sits above the QP on that path's bend, so the first letter is on the natural first line.

**Remake.** Kona is a competition: the "Groove on this!" card (LP34 1:40:50) leads straight into a 1:00 heat (LP34 1:41:00). There are no letters or tape; the hard-to-reach items are cash, a hidden deck above the pool, an IG logo on a halfpipe wire and the platinum goal's birds on wires (PS34).

**PS1.** VG marks START at the left edge by the S, with the first snake run ahead.

## Guidance and sightlines

- The first line from the spawn (the path ahead) holds the first letter (THG; LP4 1:22:40).
- White stains on the halfpipe mark where to air for each bird (THG) - a rare explicit aim mark.
- The large building's roof is the only high vantage, and CC1 notes the PS1 tape is visible from it.
- Remake: as a competition level, guidance is the heat timer and the judges; collectibles are found by free exploration (PS34).
- Hidden on purpose: rental pads behind ramps and a building, the bum under ramps, the PS1 tape on the roof, the remake panda behind a dumpster.

## Design logic

1. In a pure skatepark the hard-to-reach layer is above the ramps: wires over the halfpipe (birds, IG logo), hut roofs between pools, and the one building roof.
2. The PS1 tape uses the only roof in the park and the only fence-wallride-to-wire route, so the tape still demands a non-ramp skill in a ramp level.
3. Letters tour terrain types (QP, benches, hut roof, box, vert) rather than a route, which suits a compact park; the run is short (record 32.69 s).
4. Aim marks (stains under birds) make an overhead target readable from the ramp.
5. Kona has two competition goals in the original (street and vert); the remake turns the whole level into a competition, so the tape is dropped and collectibles stay as optional exploration.
6. Hiding behind structures (rental pads, bum, panda) is Kona's "secret" grammar: the park has few closed rooms, so occlusion replaces doors.
7. Failure is always cheap: everything lands in a bowl or on flat ground, so difficulty comes from timing and combo links, not falls.
8. Pool-to-roof transfers (A on the hut, Misty Flip over the big hut, remake cash over a hut) teach a vert-to-street exit that later levels use for roofs.
9. The LP4 records size the park's "hard-to-reach" layer in seconds: waivers 27 s, S-K-A-T-E 33 s, rental gear 36 s, birds 76 s, slalom 78 s (LP4 1:23:20). In a compact park a hidden item is never more than about a minute of riding away.
10. The PS1 port makes height itself the goal (vert tricks 30 and 60 feet up at GIGA KONA) and puts the music CD high on the Giga Kona transfer: in a park the hardest pickup is simply the highest air.
11. Remake pickups sit where the original's goals were (bird wires → IG logo wire and platinum birds; hut transfer → cash over the hut), so the competition level keeps the original's hard spots as optional collectibles.

## Original vs remake

- Level role: original career park with 21+ goals including two competitions; remake competition level with three 1:00 heats and a platinum goal (PS34; LP34 1:41:00-1:44:00).
- Tape: none in either main version; the PS1 port hid one on the large building roof (CC1). Kona is absent from the remake tape list (PS34).
- Save the birds on the power lines (7 birds, lip tricks) becomes the platinum goal Save 4 Bird Groups (grind or lip four wires) (THG vs PS34).
- Original letters, waivers, rental gear and bum spots are not present as goals in the remake; the remake adds 4 cash, 2 stat points, a hidden deck above the pool, an IG logo on a halfpipe wire, and a panda behind a dumpster (PS34).

## Transfer to this game

1. Overhead-wire crystal in a park: string a cable 2-3 m above a halfpipe deck and put the crystal on it; mark the launch spot on the ramp surface (paint or decal), as Kona's bird stains do. Our lip stall and grind snap (2.1 m) make a wire 2-3 m above the coping reachable from a pumped vert air.
2. Pool-to-roof hop: a small hut between two bowls whose roof is ≈ 3 m above the bowl coping; air out of the bowl side to land on the roof (crystal shard or letter there).
3. One-roof secret: give a skatepark one building with a roof reachable only by wallride up a fence into a cable; put the crystal at the end of the roof ridge so the player must grind the ridge and jump.
4. Hide behind, not inside: in open parks put optional pickups behind ramps or dumpsters; cheap to find, rewards camera curiosity.
5. If a level is a timed competition, keep the crystal off the scoring path but near it (above a halfpipe) so it can be grabbed between tricks.
