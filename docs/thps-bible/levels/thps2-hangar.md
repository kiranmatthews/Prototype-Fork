# THPS2 — The Hangar (Mullet Falls, Montana)

THPS2's first career level: an aircraft hangar with two rooms (halfpipe and plane; helicopter) and two hidden annexes opened by grinding aircraft parts. The secret tape sits on the outdoor helipad behind a door that opens only after the player grinds the helicopter's rotor. Evidence: the VG isometric render with gap, letter, goal-object and tape labels; one complete LP2 run that clears every goal in a single 2-minute session (7:23–9:28), sampled at 1 s around every pickup; the RPGClassics shrine walkthrough of the PS1 version; Push Square and Thumbsticks for THPS 1+2, plus LP12 for the remake. All distances and heights are estimates (see Footprint evidence). Observed facts carry an LP2/VG citation; design readings are labelled as inference.

**Check against the earlier spatial study** (`docs/warehouse-flow/research-maps.md`, `research-principles.md`): Its Hangar reading holds. Two rooms of distinct silhouettes share one vocabulary; the roll-in points at the halfpipe and its arched rail; the divider is crossable in several places; the hanging lights form an upper network; plane, helicopter and halfpipe each carry a hangtime gap; the helicopter opens the outdoor tape area and a second propeller opens the wind tunnel. That study sampled only a 1+2 scoring session. This file adds the PS1 routes: the tape trigger and route timed in LP2, the letter-to-gap pairing, the wind tunnel's continuity with the halfpipe channel, and the cash split between the two secrets. One nuance to its 'helicopter activation is a discovery payoff, not a prerequisite for circulation': true for movement, but the tape and 100% both depend on the two triggers.

**Vertical layers (heights are estimates)**

- Ground: concrete floor of both rooms; snowy helipad outside.
- Low: wooden kickers beside the helicopter, plane wing ramps, low barrier in front of the plane, helipad curbs (≈ 0.5–2 m).
- Moving/prop layer: Skycrane rotor blades at ≈ 2–2.5 m, level with the kicker tops (LP2 8:30).
- Vert: the halfpipe (≈ 3.5–4 m by convention), perimeter quarter pipes, the start-side quarter pipes, the helipad quarter pipe, the wind-tunnel quarter pipes and back wall.
- Raised rails: the rail above the back quarter pipe (Raildrop; ≈ 1.5–2.5 m over the coping) and the High Steppin' rail.
- Overhead: hanging light bars in both rooms (Lil/Big Light Hopper, Light Corner), reached from vert airs or side ramps.

**Vert and street areas**

- Vert: halfpipe (centre of Z2), back-wall quarter pipe, perimeter quarter pipes in both rooms, wind tunnel (Z7).
- Street: plane obstacle and barrier (Z3), divider frame and glass (Z4), kicker pair around the helicopter (Z5).

**Secret areas and how they open**

- Helipad (Z6): grind a Skycrane rotor blade, the helicopter flies off and the west door opens (shrine; PS12; LP2 8:30–8:40).
- Wind tunnel (Z7): grind the propeller on the wall at the back of the halfpipe, and that wall breaks open (shrine; PS12; LP2 shows it open at 8:56).

**Loops**

- Halfpipe room ring: roll-in → halfpipe → back-wall quarter pipe and raildrop → plane → south side → halfpipe.
- Cross-room loop: Chopper Hop over the glass → helicopter room → divider openings or the big lights back to the halfpipe room (LP2 8:17–8:53).
- Annex spurs: west door ↔ helipad (out and back through the same door); halfpipe east end ↔ wind tunnel (continuous channel).

Landmarks: halfpipe with arched rails; F4U-type fighter plane; Sikorsky Skycrane-type helicopter; MULLET AIRFIELD sign on the back wall; glass divider; hanging light bars; propeller on the east wall; snowy helipad with painted H (outside).

**Route (original PS1, observed in LP2 8:20–8:43; the LP2 run reaches the helicopter room from the halfpipe room via Chopper Hop at 8:17):**

1. **kicker** — helicopter room floor → one of the two wooden kickers beside the Skycrane. The kickers are the Skycrane Hangtime ramps; LP2 8:20 shows a heelflip off one
2. **grind** — kicker lip → a rotor blade. Ollie onto a blade (≈ 2–2.5 m above the floor, level with the kicker top; LP2 8:30) and 50-50 it; the $50 beside the helicopter sits on this line (LP2 8:31, timer 0:54–0:53). The helicopter lifts off on its own and the west door opens (shrine; PS12)
3. **ride** — helicopter → west wall: wooden ramp up to the door between two quarter pipes. ≈ 15 m west (VG). LP2 8:33–8:34: the doorway already shows a starry sky
4. **grind/vert-air (optional E)** — west quarter pipe → the sign rail above the door. The E hangs over the door (VG Air Over The Door); LP2 collects it and completes S-K-A-T-E at 8:35–8:36 on the way out
5. **bank** — door ramp → helipad. Roll up the wooden ramp through the door onto the snow (LP2 8:38–8:40)
6. **vert-air** — helipad, ≈ 20–25 m from the door → tape. Straight air off the only quarter pipe; LP2 kickflip at 8:42–8:43, 'Find the secret tape' at timer 0:41

**Route in THPS 1+2 (LP12 0:40:36–0:40:51; PS12):**

1. Grind a helicopter blade (PS12); the helicopter flies off (Thumbsticks: crashing out through the wall).
2. Air up the yellow-striped west quarter pipe and grind the giant NEVERSOFT sign for the E (LP12 0:40:44–0:40:46).
3. Roll out through the opened door onto the painted helipad (LP12 0:40:48).
4. Air the snowy quarter pipe: the tape at timer 0:21, ≈ 2 s after leaving the hangar (LP12 0:40:49–0:40:51).

## Guidance and sightlines

- The roll-in frames the halfpipe, the S and the arched rail in one view: the first goal is shown before the player can steer (LP2 7:24–7:27).
- The three hangtime targets are the three biggest silhouettes; the goal text names objects the player already sees.
- The helicopter is the only large prop with thin, grindable parts at kicker height; a $50 icon beside it sits on the blade line (VG; LP2 8:20–8:31).
- The E hangs over the door that opens. After the trigger, chasing the E puts the open doorway, showing night sky, in front of the player (LP2 8:34–8:36).
- The tape is hidden on purpose until the player is outside: it is the only glowing object on a dark pad, ≈ 20 m ahead, above the only ramp (LP2 8:41).
- The wind tunnel is advertised by the propeller on the halfpipe's end wall; once open, its cold blue-white light contrasts with the brown hangar (LP2 8:59; LP12 0:40:18 blue).
- Barrels sit at ground level beside every landmark (door, glass, start, halfpipe floor and pit) and act as a novice tour; pilot wings sit one step up, on wallride spots, lights and transfers.

## Design logic

1. Spawn-to-letter in 5 seconds: the first letter floats over the centrepiece on the exact line spawn points at, so the first input teaches that the roll-in is a launch (LP2 7:24–7:28).
2. Each letter sits on a named gap of a different landmark: S on Halfpipe Hangtime, K on Raildrop, A on Wingtip Hangtime, T on Chopper Hop, E on Air Over The Door. Collecting letters teaches the level's gap vocabulary one obstacle at a time.
3. The tape is a knowledge test, not an execution test: one grind on the helicopter and one easy air. The original's goal list gives no hint that the helicopter is interactive (difficulty 2 once known).
4. Goals converge on the trigger: 'Hit 3 Hangtime Gaps' sends the player to the helicopter's kickers, the same ramps that reach the blades, and the helicopter's $50 lies on the blade line.
5. The E is a breadcrumb: it marks the door before it opens and pays the player for looking at it after it opens.
6. Two secret areas with two jobs: the helipad holds the tape and nothing else, while the wind tunnel holds $250 of the ≈ $450–500 cash plus four gaps. 100% therefore requires discovering both.
7. Both secrets are opened by an aircraft part (rotor, propeller) on an aircraft-themed prop, so the unlock grammar is learnable inside the level's own fiction.
8. The secrets extend the long axis at both ends (helipad west, tunnel east), adding ≈ 50% footprint without new routes inside the box. The tunnel continues the halfpipe's channel, so entering it costs no momentum.
9. Height layering stays close to the floor: kickers, plane wing and blades ≈ 1–2.5 m, vert ≈ 3.5–4 m, raised rails and hanging lights above (estimates). Letters sit at the top of vert airs (S, A, T, E) or on the high rail (K); the lights carry only cash and connections.
10. Failure is cheap everywhere: every miss lands on open floor or back on the ramp in the same room, and the tape quarter pipe stands on a flat, hazard-free pad.
11. Density supports single-run completion: LP2 clears all ten goals, the tape and all cash in one 2:00 run (7:23–9:28), with pickups usually less than 10 s apart.
12. As career level 1 the Hangar introduces the THPS2 secret-area idea (an object trigger opening a hidden room) with the gentlest possible execution; in this study, School II then gates its tape by speed and Venice leaves its tape in plain sight (inference across the three files).
13. Nosegrind Over The Pipe gates a named trick on the first rail the player sees, teaching that goals can ask for a specific trick, not only a location.

## Original vs remake

- Score goals: Pro 25,000 → 50,000, Sick 75,000 → 100,000; a High Combo goal (7,500) is added (LP2 checklist vs PS12).
- S: the original LP2 run takes it with a halfpipe hangtime; PS12 (1+2) says to grind the arched rail to get it. Probably the same position with a different suggested line (inference).
- T: original = a quarter-pipe transfer over the glass divider (Chopper Hop, LP2 8:17); 1+2 = use the quarter pipe to smash through the glass (PS12), and LP12 0:40:30 shows glass shards around Chopper Hop. Breakable glass turns a transfer into a smash-through line.
- E: original = over the west door between quarter pipes (VG; the shrine calls it the quarter pipe facing the helicopter); 1+2 = on the giant NEVERSOFT sign over the door, reached by grinding the sign (PS12; LP12 0:40:45). The letter moves onto a grindable sign, making the breadcrumb a rail.
- Tape: same trigger and place in both. Grind a blade and the helicopter flies (1+2: crashes out through the wall per Thumbsticks), the door under the sign opens, and the tape is at the top of the snowy quarter pipe. LP12 0:40:48–0:40:51: out the door onto a painted H pad with an orange ring, tape about 2 s later.
- Wind tunnel: same propeller trigger (PS12); the remake lights it blue (LP12 0:40:18).
- 1+2 loads extra collectibles into both secrets: stat points inside the wind tunnel and above the helicopter, the alien plushie in the helipad's left corner, the Vicarious Visions logo above the suspended lights. The secrets' reward value rises without changing their triggers.
- Gap names: VG 'Instrument Landing' is 'Instrumental Landing' in PS12; PS12 adds 'Wing To Rail' (500), which has no VG label.

## Transfer to this game

1. Rotor-grind gate: make a large landmark machine (crane jib, ceiling fan, rotor) a travelling rail or trick rail reachable from two kickers beside it; grinding it opens a trick gate to an annex. Put the crystal there ≈ 1.5–2.5 m above a small quarter pipe's coping, inside this game's vert-air range.
2. Breadcrumb over the gate: hang a visible pickup row or crate line directly over the gate that will open, so the player looks at the doorway before and after the unlock.
3. Goal convergence: if the level has named-air challenges, put one of them on the kickers that reach the trigger so ordinary play parks the player beside the secret.
4. Opening-line pickup: put a non-crystal collectible over the first halfpipe straight out of spawn, taken with one vert air or spine transfer within 5 s, to teach that the spawn slope is a launch.
5. Second machine, second annex: a breakable wall at the end of the main halfpipe's axis, opened by grinding a propeller-like trick rail, leading to a lip-trick channel (quarter pipes both ends) as a reward room. Keep it continuous with the halfpipe so momentum carries in.
6. Keep crystal execution easy and failure cheap when the difficulty is discovery: flat apron, single quarter pipe, no hazards; a miss returns the player to the ramp within 2–3 s.
