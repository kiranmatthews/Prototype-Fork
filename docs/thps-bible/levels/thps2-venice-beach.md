# THPS2 — Venice Beach (Venice Beach, California)

THPS2's fifth career level: a beachfront plaza of concrete tables and palms, ringed by roofs, a pool, a vert pit and beach-side quarter pipes. The secret tape hangs above a cable (The High Wire) strung across the plaza between utility poles. In the original longplay it is taken 31 s after spawn by circling the roof ring (pool → round roof → cables → highest roof) and dropping off the top roof through it; THPS 1+2 guides describe the other way, ollieing off a table to grind the wire. Venice has no unlock triggers: the tape is in plain sight and gated by roof-chaining and speed. Evidence: the VG render; three LP2 runs (34:37–41:00) with 1-s sampling of the tape route; the RPGClassics shrine; Push Square, Thumbsticks and Screenrant for 1+2, and LP12.

**Vertical layers (heights are estimates)**

- Boundary: beach sand and sea (out of bounds).
- Ground: plaza, walkways, beach-edge lap; sunken pool and pit.
- Low: tables (≈ 0.7 m), benches, planters, ledges; stair sets (5, 9 and 20 steps ≈ 0.85, 1.5 and 3.4 m).
- Terraces: start walkway, south-east terrace (≈ 3–3.5 m over the foot of the 20-set).
- Roofs: north-west and beach-side roofs (≈ 4–5 m), round roof (≈ 5–7 m), central roofs stepping up to the highest roof (≈ 7–9 m), joined by vents used as ramps.
- Wires: radial cables on the round roof and the High Wire over the plaza (≈ 5–6 m at the poles).

**Vert and street areas**

- Vert: pool, south pit (VB Skinny / Fatty), beach-edge quarter pipes, plaza wooden and narrow quarter pipes, rooftop quarter pipes, Venice Ledge court quarter pipes.
- Street: plaza tables, benches and boards; stair sets and rails (Massive 20 Set, Seaside Handrail, Venice Ledge); fences.

**Secret areas and how they open**

- None. Every district is open from the start; the only event is the magic bum moving after each ollie.

**Loops**

- Roof ring: pool → low roof → round roof → central roofs → highest roof → (drop to plaza or Roof 2 Ramp) → plaza → back to the pool via the start steps.
- Beach lap: north-west quarter pipes → west quarter pipes → south roof and pit → Venice Ledge court → start.
- Plaza figure: tables, benches and narrow quarter pipe inside the ring, crossed by every other loop.

Landmarks: table plaza with palms and the High Wire poles; round building with radial cables; highest roof with its quarter pipe; pool beside the start; graffiti walls; Venice Ledge stairs; beach and ocean (boundary).

**Route (original PS1, observed in LP2 run 1, 34:38–35:09):**

1. **ledge** — spawn (turn left) → stair ledge into the pool. BS tailslide down the ledge beside the stairs (LP2 34:39–34:41)
2. **manual + vert-air** — pool floor → pool wall. Manual across, then a varial heelflip out of the wall for spray can 1 (LP2 34:42–34:45, '1 of 5 spray cans' at 34:43)
3. **transfer** — pool's far wall → low roof. No comply + BS 50-50 up onto the roof: Wee Lil' Roof Gap (LP2 34:46–34:48). Nice Mid Size and Siiiiiick Roof Gaps are longer versions of the same exit
4. **ollie** — low roof → round building's roof. Rim of the round roof; letter E at 34:51 (timer 1:46)
5. **wire** — round roof edge → central roofs. Air off the rim, FS 50-50 up a cable to a white cylindrical vent, then FS tailslide and FS 50-50 along roof rails, landing in a manual (LP2 34:52–35:01)
6. **vert-air** — quarter pipe on the highest roof → T. Varial heelflip for the T, then a 540 varial heelflip + stalefish (LP2 35:02–35:06)
7. **ride** — quarter pipe → plaza-side edge of the highest roof. ≈ 15–20 m roll; the plaza, poles, wire and glowing tape are framed below (LP2 35:07)
8. **ollie (drop)** — roof edge → tape and then the plaza. Fly over the wire through the tape: 'Find the secret tape' with the Big Drop icon at timer 1:29, landing for Big Drop 1,200 (LP2 35:08–35:09)

**Ground-up route described for THPS 1+2 (PS12, Thumbsticks; not seen in LP2):**

1. From the start, go straight down into the plaza; the tape is visible above the wire (PS12).
2. Build speed (Thumbsticks: special meter, a ramp, land and skate back toward the second table on the left).
3. Ollie off a table and catch the wire in a grind; the tape is on the wire's line (PS12: 'quite precise'; Screenrant: max Speed, Air and Ollie stats help).

## Guidance and sightlines

- The plaza and its two utility poles lie straight ahead of spawn; the cable is the plaza's only overhead line and the tape glows above it (PS12; LP2 35:07).
- The pool beside the spawn walkway visibly backs onto low roofs, inviting the roof ring (LP2 34:39–34:48).
- Letters on roof edges and rims read against the sky and the sea (E at the round roof's rim, LP2 34:49–34:51; T above the top roof's quarter pipe, LP2 35:02).
- From the highest roof the whole plaza and the wire are framed below, and the roof edge points at the tape: the shrine's hint is to look around after the T.
- Spray cans float above perimeter quarter pipes and teach where the VB transfer pairs are.
- The magic bum relocates to the next spot after each ollie, so the goal itself guides the player around the level (PS12).
- Heights are the only hazard seen: overshooting a roof drops the player to a lower layer (shrine warning at the E). LP2 shows no water or kill hazard; the beach is the outer boundary on VG.

## Design logic

1. A two-way tape: the High Wire hangs between the plaza (tables) and the top roof, so the tape can be taken bottom-up (table → wire grind, the 1+2 guides' route) or top-down (drop from the highest roof, the LP2 route). One placement serves a short precise route and a long forgiving one.
2. The tape is the first teaser: on the main sightline from spawn, ≈ 30 m away (PS12), long before the player knows how to reach it. The roof ring around the plaza is the answer.
3. The roof ring is the level's spine: pool → low roof → round roof → cables → highest roof gathers one can, two letters and the tape in 31 s (LP2). The letters E and T mark it.
4. The pool next to spawn works as an elevator: three named exits (Wee Lil', Nice Mid Size, Siiiiiick Roof Gap) grade the same move by distance from the wall.
5. Graded thresholds on single objects recur: planters (two pops), the narrow quarter pipe (Up!/Up!!/And Away!!!), boarded tables (He Could Go.../All The Way.../Touchdown!!!), pool exits. One object yields several difficulty steps.
6. Cables are a navigation layer: radial cables on the round roof carry the player between roofs (LP2 34:55–34:58), and the tape sits on the plaza's cable. Wire grinds are introduced as connectors before the tape asks for one (1+2 route).
7. No unlocks at career position 5: after the Hangar's trigger-gated tape and School II's speed-gated roof, Venice's tape is in plain sight and is gated only by chaining roofs at speed.
8. Failure cost scales with height: plaza misses are cheap, but losing the roof ring costs ≈ 20–30 s, so the top-down route gives one attempt per climb. The plaza route (Ramp 2 Roof → Vent 2 Roof) is the designed way back up.
9. Goal objects teach the perimeter: spray cans on four perimeter vert spots and the pool; the bum's five spots cover the pit, the south-east, the north fence, the banner courtyard and spawn.
10. Cash rewards hard corners: three $250 icons sit at the plaza sculpture, the round roof and the south pit, and the 12 icons total $1,500 (VG; matches the shrine's '$500 + $1500').
11. Scores scale with the career: Pro 100,000 and Sick 200,000, against the Hangar's 25,000/75,000, assume the long roof lines and manual chains the layout offers.
12. Letter placement maps the two roof chains: E and T on the central/round roofs, S and K on the north-west roofs, A on the south-east terrace. Collecting S-K-A-T-E means touring every raised zone.

## Original vs remake

- Goals unchanged except for the added High Combo 20,000 (PS12).
- Letters: PS12 (1+2) describes a single roof chain S → K → A → T → E (fence onto a building; K over a gap; grind the graffitied wall to the next roof for A; kicker to a roof with T over a quarter pipe; drop to the lower roof for E above a short ramp). In the original, A sits on the south-east terrace's curved rail (VG; LP2 36:13) and E on the round roof's rim (LP2 34:51). The remake appears to move A into the roof chain (inferred from text; not verified in LP12).
- Tape: the 1+2 guides all describe the ground-up route. PS12: from a table, ollie up and grind the wire, which is precise. Thumbsticks: boost off the ramp, land, skate back to the second table on the left and ramp off it. Screenrant: max Speed, Air and Ollie stats help. LP2's original run uses the top-down roof drop instead. 1+2 keeps the top-down option: its street stat point is reached by ollieing off the end of the highest roof toward the wire.
- Lighting: midday in the original (LP2) vs sunset in 1+2 (LP12 0:55–1:02), with denser graffiti. The wire still crosses the plaza's sky (LP12 0:55:38).
- Gap list: PS12 adds On The Fence and Fence To Roof (not labelled on VG); VG's Huge Ramp 2 Roof is absent from PS12.
- 1+2 adds stat points (by the wire, on the roofs, above a banner rail and over a beach fence), the Vicarious Visions logo (up a wooden quarter pipe onto a wall, then a ramp) and the alien plushie (end of the wooden beach path).

## Transfer to this game

1. High-wire crystal: string a rope or travelling rail from a roof edge down to a pole over a picnic plaza and hang the crystal ≈ 1 m above it near the high end. Bottom-up: from a ≈ 0.8 m table a full ollie peaks at ≈ 2.6 m, and the 2.1 m snap radius catches a wire up to ≈ 4.5 m, so drop the wire's low end to ≈ 4 m and let the grind carry up to the crystal. Top-down: the roof ring ends at an edge facing the crystal; ollie off through it.
2. Pool-as-elevator beside spawn: a bowl whose far wall transfers onto a low roof, with three graded exits (near, mid, far from the wall) that later serve as score gaps.
3. Roof ring with breadcrumbs: place 2–3 secondary pickups (crates or letters) on the ring before the crystal's launch roof (rim of a round roof, a rooftop quarter pipe) so the ring is learned before it is needed.
4. Visible tease: put the crystal on the spawn sightline ≈ 30 m away, above the plaza, so the level's first question is 'how do I get up there?'.
5. Moving pickup tour: a collectible that hops to the next of five fixed spots each time it is touched, leading the player through the districts in a fixed order.
6. Height-scaled failure: keep plaza retries cheap (table and wire within 3–5 s) and give the top-down route a designed lift back up (quarter pipe → roof → vent kicker) of ≈ 20 s.
