Loops: Z1 → Z3 → Z2 → Z6 → Z1 is the outer street loop (hydrants, LP2 run 1). The vertical loop is
station (Z4) → tracks → Banks (Z5) → exit kicker or low wall → street (Z1). The Banks are a dead-end
pocket with one-way exits.

## Spawn and first view

- You start on the road in Z1 facing along it, with the park's low stone wall on the left and the El's
  concrete pillars overhead (LP2 0:29:20, 0:26:34). Joey's Sculpture is ahead and to the right (RPGC; PS12).
- **Visible from spawn:** the **S** floats above the park wall and entrance on the left, a few seconds
  ahead. The opening line is wallride the park wall → BS tailslide → "Park Entrance Gap" → keep grinding,
  and it collects the S by 1:50 (LP2 0:26:36–0:26:42).
- **Not visible:** the tape. It is in the Banks, a walled pocket below and beyond the El. The only
  big-scale hint from the start is the El overhead, which leads there.
- The start road invites three things: the wall line (S), a right turn down to the pit (Joey's, about 6 s),
  and a 180° turn to the station escalator (the subway).

**Route (observed in LP2 run 2, original PS1; the clock counts down from 2:00):**

1. 1:59 Spawn on the Z1 road. Turn right and roll down the slope into the pit (Z2). *Optional detour
   used in LP2:* 50-50 **Joey's Sculpture** at 1:54 (the goal completes at 1:52).
2. 1:50–1:44 Roll back up past the "25th Street Station" sign and ride the **escalator** up (a $50
   on it). PS12 lists "Going Down?" as a manual *down* this escalator.
3. 1:43–1:41 On the elevated platform (≈ 2 storeys up), turn onto the **subway tracks** and grind them
   (BS 50-50, $50).
4. 1:41–1:35 Grind or ride the twin tracks downhill. "Ride the Rails" scores at 1:36 and the **Grind
   the Subway Rails** goal completes at 1:35. The tracks descend past a hotel facade.
5. 1:34–1:25 Nose-manual between the rails down the descending El. Its far end drops into the Banks.
6. 1:24 Ollie off the end of the track and land on the Banks' brick plaza ($100, the VG
   "Big Air Out of the Banks" spot).
7. 1:22–1:16 Ride up the **curving concrete road** (a parapet on each side, about 30–40 m long,
   climbing ≈ 3 m). A BS 50-50 on its edge picks up a $50 at 1:19. The Brooklyn Bridge comes into
   view ahead.
8. 1:15 At the road's broken end, two rebar bars stick out and droop toward the plaza. Grind the
   **right bar** (BS tailslide).
9. 1:13 Ollie across to the **left bar**: "Re-Rebar" scores and a $50 hangs on the hop.
10. 1:12 Grind the left bar (FS tailslide); the tape is now on screen, glowing ahead.
11. 1:11 Ollie off the end of the bar: **FIND THE SECRET TAPE**, collected in the air.
12. 1:10 Land on the brick plaza beside the benches. PS12's "Rebar to Rail Gap" means you can land on
    the rail below instead and keep the line going.

Alternative entries (no tokens needed): (a) from the start road, ollie a **low brick wall**
perpendicular to it into the Banks area (RPGC, original); (b) grind up a **knocked-down lamp pole**
leaning on the curb or fence near a street light (absolutcheats, original; thumbsticks describes a "pipe
leaning against a fence" in the remake). Exits: the blue kicker "Big Air Out of the Banks" over the wall
(PS12; absolutcheats "jump on the left-hand side of where you entered"), or a grass verge and low wall
back onto the street, as in LP2 0:30:41–0:30:46.

### Cash icons (needed for "100% Goals and Cash")

VG shows 11 icons ($900): Banks — $100 at the river fence ("Banks Fence Gap"), $50 at Re-Rebar (on the
tape line), $50 by "Banks Road Gap", $50 on the curving road ("Take It to the Bridge"), $100 where the
tracks land ("Big Air Out of the Banks"); park and streets — $250 at the statue ("Pigeon Puddin'"),
$50 by the snack bar, $50 on the escalator, $50 on the El by "Kick It", $100 on the east building by
the awning QPs, $50 on the east street ("The Easy Way"). RPGC lists the 100% goal as "$500 + $1000",
and its Philadelphia figure ($2000) matches that level's icon total exactly, so one NY icon ($100) may
be missing from the VG render (open question). Four of the five Banks icons sit on the tape's approach
and line: the LP2 tape run collects four of them.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **Shown:** the S at spawn, and every other letter and token on park features you can see from the
  park's paths. The El overhead from the start is a standing landmark that leads to the station and
  then to the Banks.
- **Signposted by goals:** the checklist names "Grind the Subway Rails", and the tokens open the subway,
  so the goal list itself points the player toward the El, which ends in the Banks.
- **Hidden on purpose:** the Banks sit below and behind walls and fencing. The tape stays out of sight
  until the player is on the rebar line (LP2 0:30:08), and the second bar's start is hidden until you
  are nearly on it (halfglassgaming). The broken road ends in mid-air with rebar pointing outward, and
  that "unfinished" silhouette is the guide to the line.
- **Warning signs:** a BIG DROP icon appears at the Banks river fence (LP2 0:30:22); the taxi and the
  trains are the dangers.

## Design logic

1. **The tape is in the level's hidden district, not hidden in a corner of the main map.** Finding and
   entering the Banks is the challenge; the local line is short (13 s from the landing).
2. **Several entries with different prices:** free but knowledge-gated (a low wall, a fallen pole), or
   earned (5 tokens open a scenic, fast descent). Experts and completionists both get in.
3. **Goal chaining.** Tokens unlock the subway, the subway-rails goal lies on the way, and the tracks
   land you at the foot of the tape's road. LP2 clears Joey's, the subway rails and the tape inside one
   49 s opening.
4. **A construction motif marks the tape line.** A road that stops in mid-air with rebar sticking out
   says "go to the end": the silhouette is the signpost.
5. **The skill test is one blind transfer.** The second bar starts earlier than it looks, so you must
   hop early and lean in. The test is knowledge plus timing, not speed or height.
6. **The tape sits inside a scoring line.** After it you can land on the rail below ("Rebar to Rail
   Gap") and keep going ("Bench-Hoppin'"), and four of the five Banks cash icons are strung along the
   approach.
7. **Failure is cheap locally and expensive globally.** A miss costs ≈ 8–10 s to re-climb the road;
   leaving and coming back costs 30–40 s of a 120 s run.
8. **The pocket has its own exits** (a blue kicker over the wall, a low wall to the street), so a
   player who enters is never trapped.
9. **Letters teach the park first.** All five letters are in the park, the S is visible from spawn, and
   LP2 collects all five within 30 s. The easiest goal is nearest.
10. **The tokens use the park's furniture** (rock, bridge, counter, QP, ramp), and their reward is
    mobility, not just cash.
11. **Perimeter goals drag you round the outer loop.** The hydrants run along the streets, against
    traffic.
12. **Goal cash rises with distance:** tape $1200 > subway rails $1100 > Joey's $900 > tokens and
    SKATE $800 > hydrants $700.
13. **Moving hazards add timing** on both access routes: a taxi on the start road, trains on the El.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Broken-road rebar crystal.** Build a curving ramp that climbs ≈ 3 m and ends in mid-air. Two
   short rails jut from its end and droop: rail A straight, rail B starting 1–2 m to the side and hidden
   behind the parapet. Put the crystal ≈ 1–1.5 m above B's far end and ≈ 1 m beyond it. The grind ollie
   (≈ 2.4 m pop) reaches it and a plain ollie off the end does not. The 2.1 m snap radius makes the
   blind hop forgiving but not automatic.
2. **Earned fast route plus free hard route.** Five tokens (or one trick gate) open a long elevated
   rail that descends straight to the foot of the crystal's ramp. Keep a token-free entry: a fallen pole
   grindable at 30°, or a wall reachable with an on-foot double jump (≈ 4.6 m).
3. **Moving hazard on the access rail.** Run a travelling rail or a moving-platform "train" along the
   descent, so the rail route needs timing as well as balance.
4. **Pocket with exits.** Give the sub-area a kicker or speed pad that throws the player back over its
   wall to the main street. A miss inside the pocket then costs about 10 s, and leaving costs a
   deliberate choice.
5. **Silhouette as signpost.** Use an "unfinished" structure (a broken slab with rebar, a cut-off
   bridge) whose open end points at the crystal. Keep the crystal out of sight until the player is on
   the final rail.
6. **Scoring line beyond the crystal.** Put a landing rail or bench row under the crystal so a skilled
   player can carry the combo on.
