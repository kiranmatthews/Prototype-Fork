The island is one big hill, so speed comes from going down and height costs a climb.

Secret areas and how they open: the cell-block interior (PS2: door in the exercise yard to the roof and roof/door routes; PS1: trap door in the roof, portal door to the roof; remake: doors open after Release the Prisoner), the water-tower tank (spine transfer through its open roof), the south rooftops (door captions).

## Spawn and first view

**Remake.** The flythrough shows the S over a grind rail by a staircase, the closed cell-block doors with a guard ("Release the Prisoner"), and the tape beside a ruined wall with a pipe and the bay behind (LP34 1:22:50-1:23:02). The run starts at the dock (LP34 1:23:08): the ferry is behind the player, the tunnel to the switchbacks is to the left, the pro COMBO ramp is straight ahead (PS34). The tape is not reachable at first: its route runs through the cell block, which opens only after Release the Prisoner.

**Original.** Loading leads to the dock area by a stone building with an arched doorway (LP4 0:46:26); Ranger Ron and his tram stand nearby and the first goal taken is his skitch launch (LP4 0:46:32-0:47:26). The water tower and cell block sit on the skyline above.

**PS1.** VG marks START at the top of the island in front of the cell block, beside the C of C-O-M-B-O, with goals 5 and 6 there.

**Route — TAPE-R**

1. **unlock** — five wheelie bins around the island → cell-block keys. Knock over the 5 bins; the key is in the last (LP34 1:24:08 "1 of 5 ... no cellblock keys here", 1:27:50 "5 of 5 You found the cellblock keys!").
2. **unlock** — closed cell-block doors right of the Lighthouse Courtyard door → open cell block. Approach the doors with the key; the cut-scene releases the prisoner (LP34 1:27:56 "COMPLETE! Release the Prisoner").
3. **ride** — yard / Rooftop - North Side → cell-block interior. Enter the cell block (LP34 enters from the north rooftop and drops in, 1:28:44-1:28:48; PS34 uses the opened doors).

5. **ledge** — top-tier walkway railing → end wall. Ride or grind the railing along the top tier (LP34 1:28:59-1:29:03 "BS Tailslide + Kickflip").

7. **ollie** — rail end → tape. Jump at the end into the tape above the water tower (LP34 1:29:10 "COMPLETE! Get the secret tape"; PS34 "jump at the end").
8. **ledge** — air over the tank → yard wall. Land in a grind on the yard wall beside the tower (LP34 1:29:11-1:29:12).

## Guidance and sightlines

- Zone captions on entry ("Switchbacks - North Side", "Lighthouse Courtyard", "Rooftop - North Side") name areas so text directions work (LP4 1:00:09, 1:01:27; LP34 1:28:45; PS34).
- The flythrough shows the tape beside a ruined wall with a pipe and the bay (LP34 1:23:02), the same view the player gets when leaving the left window.
- The water tower is a skyline landmark from the yard and the switchbacks (LP34 1:28:39-1:28:42).
- Closed doors with a guard are shown in the flythrough (LP34 1:22:56) and sit beside the courtyard the letter line uses, so the lock is seen long before the key.
- Inside the cell block, light through the two open windows at the end of the top tier draws the eye; the tape is visible through the left one (LP34 1:29:05).
- Bins carry their own text feedback ("1 of 5 ... no cellblock keys here!", LP34 1:24:08), so the search has a visible counter.
- Hidden on purpose: the cell-block interior, the tank interior, the south-rooftop loop, the tall tower platform.

## Design logic

1. The remake gates the tape behind another goal (Release the Prisoner): the tape is the reward for opening the level's biggest locked building, and its route uses that building once.
2. The search goal (5 bins) is spread across the island, so by the time the cell block opens the player knows the island; the tape then needs only ≈ 30 s.
3. Inside-out route: climb a closed interior (tiers, spiral stairs) and exit through a window to the outside landmark. It reverses the usual outside-in climb and gives a dramatic reveal of the bay.
4. The water tower is the island's secret-holder in every version: PS1 tape on its roof, PS2 pro spine into its open tank, remake spine goal plus tape beside it.
5. Height comes from the island itself: the tape is "high" because the cell block sits on the summit; no extra structure was needed.
6. Letters stay on one readable line in the remake (one rail for S-K-A, a slope jump, a QP), whereas the original spreads them down the hill; the remake keeps the teaching letters simple and moves the challenge to the tape.
7. Falling into the bay is the main failure (LP4 "You big drip!"); routes near the shore are low-cost practice, routes on the summit cost a climb.
8. PS1 relies on portals (to the cell-block roof) and a warp link (VG) to make a tall island navigable on weaker hardware; the remake replaces them with opened doors and roof captions.
9. Pro COMBO in the original ends by flying through a window, the same move the remake tape uses: a late-career original idea became the remake's tape.
10. Remake runs are 10:00; LP34 used ≈ 4:50 for every regular goal including the gated tape (1:23:08 → 1:29:10).

## Original vs remake

- Tape: none in the PS2 career; PS1 put it on the water-tower roof via a kicker-rail from the cell-block roof (CC1); the remake puts it outside the cell block's top-tier window above the water tower, behind Release the Prisoner (PS34; LP34).
- Find the Guard's Keys (16 keys) and Distract the Guards (bins) merge into Release the Prisoner (5 bins with a key) (THG vs PS34).

## Transfer to this game

1. Gated crystal: lock a tall building with a door that a search goal (five bins, last one holds a key) opens; put the crystal just outside an upper window of that building, so the route is interior climb → window → rail → jump. Use a travelling-rail-free static rail out of the window; our grind ollie (≈ 2.4 m pop) clears the crystal height above the rail.
2. Inside-out climb: interior quarter pipes and a spiral handrail up three tiers (≈ 3-4 m each) give vert air and grind practice in a closed room before the exposed exit.
3. Landmark cable network: hang several cables from a water tower to roofs and walls (PS1 layout), then put lesser collectibles on the cables and the crystal at the tank.
4. Spine into a tank: a drained pool beside a tower whose tank has an open top; spine transfer from the pool rim into the tank (our spine transfer works pipe to pipe).
5. Make failure geographic: put early collectibles near the shore (cheap water reset) and the crystal on the summit (costly climb), so difficulty rises with altitude.
6. Caption zones on entry so directions in hints can name them, as the remake does.
