Route (designed "easy way", G1 p062; observed in LP1 5:13–5:40):

1. **From the spawn, ride down the start canopy and drop right** (WEB thumbsticks: "drop down and to the
   right"). LP1 grinds table 1 below the canopy first (5:17, "1 OF 5 LUNCH TABLES").
2. **Ride up the long concrete ramp along the side of the gym** (bank). It is marked with chevrons and has a
   handrail (LP1 5:21–5:23, ≈ 3 s of climbing).
3. **Pop over the hazard-striped coping at the roof edge onto the gym roof** (lip). LP1 5:24–5:26.
4. **Get on top of the big AC unit "using either ramp"** (kicker/grind, G1 p062). LP1 50-50s its edge (5:29–5:31,
   "50 50 GRIND 255").
5. **Ride over the orange pipe on the far side of the AC and grind it down to the awning** (grind). G1: "hit the
   rail there leading to the awning by simply riding over it and pressing Triangle". LP1 5:33–5:34 ("50 50 GRIND
   210").
6. **Land on the awning and ride its full length downhill** (LP1 5:35–5:38, ≈ 4 s). In 1+2, holding a manual
   down it scores Down The Awning (100).
7. **Ollie big at the end of the awning** (ollie). The tape is collected in the air (LP1 5:39, "HIDDEN TAPE!",
   timer 1:30).
8. **Land in the curved quarter pipe / kicker court below** and carry on into the S-K-A-T-E line (K is a few
   metres away; S is on the stair rail you just rode over).

Alternative ways onto the awning:

- **Roof-lip grind** (G1 p062, "more difficult"): grind the lip of the quarter pipe that runs round the roof
  edge; when you reach the pipe leading to the awning, ollie onto it and ride it down. Earns a rail-to-rail
  combo bonus.
- **Roof to roof** (G1 p062, "very tough"): jump from the gym roof to the east roof with the three bonus icons
  (Roof 2 Roof gap), then use the small ramp there to ollie onto the awning. G1: "press down to slow down and
  stop as to not go over the edge". G1 p066 defines the Roof To Awning Gap (750) as this ramp launch after the
  Roof 2 Roof gap.
- **AC to awning jump** (1+2, PS12): jump from the AC straight to the awning instead of grinding the pipe;
  PS12 calls this the Roof To Awning Gap (750).
- **TAS wallride shortcut** (TAS1 0:53–0:57): from the plaza beside the top of the stairs, a fastplant into a
  wallride puts the skater on the side of the awning near its top ("FASTPLANT + WALL RIDE 625 X2"); it rides
  down and collects the tape at timer 1:51. This skips the gym roof entirely; no guide describes it.
- **Remake drop** (LP12 0:09:18–0:09:19): from the gym roof the human player drops onto the awning without the
  pipe, then rides ≈ 6 s down it.

## Guidance and sightlines

- **The awning is the sign.** A long, bright green canopy over the stairs, visible from the spawn and from the
  whole S-K line. The tape glints at its end, over the kicker court where the letter line passes.
- **The spawn rehearses the finish.** The level starts on a smaller green canopy whose end you ollie off
  (Gimme Gap). The tape asks for the same move at a larger scale.
- **The way up is plain but off the line.** The concrete ramp to the gym roof has chevrons and a handrail, next to
  the spawn, but it leads away from the main lines; G1 opens the tape page with "you're going to have to head up
  to the roof area".
- **Objects on the roof invite exploration.** The AC unit with its ramp, the funboxes and, in 1+2, a textbook
  and stat points above the AC give reasons to be on the roof before the player knows the pipe goes to the
  awning.
- **Bonus icons mark expert lines.** Three 500s on the 1,000-point gaps, three 200s on the east roof (the hard
  awning approach).
- **Hidden on purpose:** the pipe from the AC to the awning is only obvious once you stand on the AC; the east
  roof is out of sight behind the gym roof's coping.

## Design logic

1. **Above the main line, not beside it.** The tape hangs over the S-K line, the most travelled part of the
   level; it is seen often but cannot be reached from where it is seen. This is the series' first true
   visible-but-hard tape.
2. **A line above a line.** The awning roofs the stair rail: the upper route (roof → pipe → awning) and the lower
   route (stair rail → kicker gap) run in the same direction and end at the same court. The tape rewards
   finding the upper copy of a line the player already knows.
3. **The spawn pre-teaches the finish.** The first input of the level is "ride down a canopy and ollie off the
   end" (Gimme Gap); the tape's last step is the same move on the long awning.
4. **Descending routes for a two-minute run.** The climb is short (one ramp); the long part of the route is
   downhill (pipe and awning), which supplies the speed for the final ollie and keeps the route fast.
5. **Three graded ways in.** AC + pipe ("easy"), roof-lip grind to the pipe (rail-to-rail bonus), roof to roof via
   the three icons ("very tough"). The same destination serves three skill levels, and the harder ways pay gaps.
6. **The finish lands on the main line.** After the tape you drop into the kicker court, a few metres from K and
   table 2, so the tape costs little time in a run that also collects letters and tables. LP1 takes the tape
   first and then sweeps the letters.
7. **Failure is soft at the end, firm in the middle.** Missing the tape lands in a quarter pipe; falling off the
   awning or pipe costs the climb (≈ 15–20 s).
8. **Letters teach the level's main line.** S-K-A-T-E is a single east-to-west line with no backtracking; each
   exit aims at the next letter (G1 p060–p061).
9. **Tables are grind targets on the letter line.** Four of the five tables sit on or next to the letter line,
   so the two tapes combine (G1 p058). The fifth, at the end of the Hall Pass, rewards the left-hand route.
10. **Difficulty step from Warehouse.** Warehouse's tape needed one jump from spawn. School's needs a climb to a
    second layer, a descending grind and a canopy ride: more surfaces, more knowledge, the same moderate speed.
11. **Scale grows with the career.** A large, open campus with a ring of routes (G1 p055) after a one-room
    warehouse; the tape route is ≈ 3 times longer.
12. **The remake scores the tape route.** 1+2 adds Down The Awning (manual the awning), redefines the Roof To
    Awning Gap as the AC → awning jump, and places a textbook and stat points above the AC. The tape route
    became a scored, signposted line.

## Transfer to this game

1. **Canopy crystal.** Lay a long sloping canopy (a narrow grindable platform, ≈ 3–4 m wide) over a stair-rail
   line, from a roof down to a plaza; hang the crystal ≈ 1.5–2 m beyond and above its lower end, over a quarter
   pipe so a miss is a soft landing. The downhill canopy supplies the speed; a full ollie (≈ 1.78 m high) is the
   last move.
2. **Pre-teach at spawn.** Start the level on a short canopy whose end launches onto a grindable planter, so the
   crystal's final move is the first thing the player does.
3. **Roof access by a marked ramp.** Give the roof one plain ramp with chevron markings next to the spawn, leading
   away from the main line; put a pickup or trick gate on the roof object (the AC unit's role) to draw players
   up.
4. **Descending grind link.** Connect the roof object to the canopy with a long descending pipe (rail); landing a
   grind at the top and riding it down is the skill check. Offer a harder alternative: a jump from a second roof
   via a small kicker.
5. **Keep walkers off.** The original tape height (≈ 4–6 m over the plaza) would be reachable on foot here: a
   4.6 m double jump plus a ≈ 1.8 m body touches pickups up to ≈ 6.5 m above any standable surface. Either raise
   the canopy end so the crystal is ≥ ≈ 7–7.5 m above the plaza, or hang it over the quarter pipe's curved
   transition where there is nothing to stand on below, ≥ 3 m from any ledge-grab edge.
6. **End on the main line.** Land the crystal jump next to the level's main trick line so the detour costs a few
   seconds in a timed run.

## Open questions

- Heights of the gym roof, AC unit and awning are estimates; no source gives them.
- Whether the 1999 gym had an enterable interior: my sources show only its roof.
- PS12's remake table list differs in wording from G1's; whether tables moved is unverified.
- G1 prints no points for Roof 2 Roof, F Rail-to-Rail, H Roof Rails, N Insane Rail To Rail Combos or T Rail To
  Rail; some are combos, not named gaps.
- Swim Team Gap is absent from PS12's remake list; it may have been removed or omitted.
- The TAS wallride onto the awning may depend on N64 collision; no PS1 source mentions it.
