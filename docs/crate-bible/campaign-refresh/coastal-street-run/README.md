# Beach Street and Rooftop Deliveries · 14 November 2026

Beach Street is a forced kite ride. Cargo follows its steering line and rewards completed turns, established landing exits and checkpoint departures. The fast route contains no explosives, extra launchers or masks that would imply protection from a fatal kite crash. The separate bonus owns the harder crate-order puzzles.

The seven longer main sections each contain ten crates. Four short checkpoint-to-checkpoint canal sections contain six: keeping their launch and receiving windows open takes priority over forcing ten boxes into every fast crossing. Three-high columns and two-column walls use the existing kite-contact smash rule at every riding speed. This is a mode-specific layout, not an ordinary walking crate course. The 159-box gem tally comprises 94 cargo, ten checkpoints and 55 bonus boxes.

Crabs and turtles each debut alone on a broad shoulder. Later pairs repeat those silhouettes and leave the suggested passing lane clear. The former spiker and spinner appearances are replaced by established counters; no enemy sits on a canal launch or landing. Geometry, wind, tow speed, camera, animation, freight cycles, hurdles and native art are unchanged.

Native play caught and corrected cargo after the automatic kite release, a group missed during a fast direction change, and a reward near the bonus-pad bypass. Browser keyboard steering also exposed an early post-hop stack; moving it from z=232 to z=245 lets the player settle into its lane. The final complete native campaign route collects all 104 main/checkpoint boxes and the crystal, passes all thirteen jumps and finishes without a crash while bypassing the bonus pad. The gem correctly remains unearned because the linked bonus has not been collected. Separate native failure trials cover planters, hurdles, canals and checkpoint recovery. No no-death condition is added to the gem; an all-box attempt still needs to collect the forward-only ride's cargo.

Rooftop Deliveries groups opening stock, the finite retreat payload, both switch-owned high galleries and the permanent finish rewards. Fifteen upper rewards now sit in formations on their existing materialized floors. The bonus retains its advanced optional attack sequence: spiker, turtle, two timed spinner openings, charger, airborne foe and ranged sentry. Its waiting floors, launch tools, phase windows, lift, crumble receiver and independently wired switches are preserved. All three lives retain risk: the intake launch, the TNT support and the exposed approach beyond the Nitro wall.
