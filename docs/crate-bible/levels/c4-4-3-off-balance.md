## Puzzle rooms and dependency chains

1. **Strong crate on a TNT (S3-a).** Correct: light the TNT (spin the lower crate, per GR; in the run it was lit while
   Crash stood on the strong crate above), hop to the wall top, and let the blast clear the strong crate. Body-slam the
   other strong crates. Wrong: stand beside the stack or spin into the TNT. Loss: a life. Design: the explosive is the
   way up and the way through at once.
2. **Grid on a TNT bridge (B-a → B-c).** Correct: empty the grid by hand, then cross the row stepping on iron,
   lighting TNT behind you. Wrong: light the first TNT under the grid, or stop on a lit TNT. Loss: fruit, or the
   attempt. Design: one row is simultaneously the floor, a timer and 9 crates.
3. **TNT springboard to an off-camera crate (S10-b → S10-c).** Correct: bounce off the TNT toward the right wall
   first, then break it. Wrong: break it on arrival. Loss: 1 crate (all-box only), invisible on screen.
4. **32 deferred Nitro (S12-a).** Correct: avoid all of them and hit the switch at the door. Wrong: let Aku Aku or
   an enemy set some off early. GR warns this inflates the running count, so the sign no longer exposes missed crates.
5. **Hidden-gem drop (S7-a).** The two crates by the wind tunnel mark the edge to drop off. The gem path holds no
   crates, and in the run the player died after taking it (≈ 1:58:00) to get back.

## Design logic of this level

1. **Two masks, one crate grammar.** Akano turns floating crates into hover targets (S4-a, S6-a, S7-b, S11-a).
   Lani-Loli turns rows into phase crates that can hold real crates (S9-a). Both are introduced with an easy crate
   right after the pickup.
2. **TNT as the tool of choice.** The level uses TNT as a step (S3-a), a bridge (B-c), a springboard (S10-b) and a
   chain key. It is the most varied TNT use in the three levels I mapped.
3. **Nitro as navigation, not crate puzzles.** Thirty-two Nitro (17 % of the total) line balconies and tunnels.
   Their only crate role is to be paid at the door, so the all-box goal never asks the player to touch them.
4. **The bonus is a bridge.** Iron crates are footing and TNT crates are the count. The bonus teaches what the main
   route then expects: keep moving over explosives.
5. **Count traps hug checkpoints and the camera edge.** Crates left of C1 (S1-c), right of C2 (S2-d), above the
   camera (S10-c), above C8 (S10-e). Each one is a two-step detour from a safe spot.
6. **Wall-runs are paced with landing crates.** Each chevron wall ends on a landing with crates (S1-b, S8-a, S10-a).
   The crate is the confirmation that the wall-run was completed.
7. **Checkpoint cadence.** Eight checkpoints in about 7½ minutes. The densest pair is C7 → C8, about 20 s apart,
   around the springboard trap.
8. **Clear goal vs all-box goal.** The clear route needs none of S1-c, S2-a, S2-d, S3-b, S6-a, S7-b, S10-c or S10-e.
   That is 15+ crates whose only purpose is to send the all-box player sideways or upwards.

## Transfer to the skate game

1. **TNT/steel grind bridge.** Our crate rows are grind rails, explosives and steel count as part of the ledge, and a
   TNT in the row detonates when ridden onto. Grinding at ≥ 6.5–7 m/s outruns the blast. A floating row of
   `tnt, steel, steel, tnt, steel, steel, tnt …` (9 TNT) over a pit becomes a speed-gated one-way bridge that pays 9
   crates. A slow rider (≤ 5.5 m/s) dies, so put a downhill or a kicker before it.
2. **Grid on the bridge head.** Put a 3×3 grid of plain crates on the first three TNT. Its top row (3 crates, equal
   height) is itself a grindable row, so a skater can grind-collect the top layer before dropping onto the TNT bridge.
3. **Strong crate on TNT as a step.** On foot: `multi-hit on tnt` at the foot of a 4–5 m ledge. A stomp lights the
   TNT (3 s fuse). The player double-jumps from the multi-hit top (2.30 m + 4.6 m reach) to the ledge, and the blast
   clears the multi-hit for the count.
4. **Springboard count trap.** A TNT 3 m below an off-camera crate. Crate-stomp bounce is 14 m/s ≈ 3 m above the
   lid, so the crate sits 2.5–3 m above the TNT top, against a wall. Breaking the TNT first loses the crate.
5. **Door payout.** Put the green `nitrobang` at the end gate and show the n/total at the gate before it is pressed,
   as Crash 4's exit sign does.
