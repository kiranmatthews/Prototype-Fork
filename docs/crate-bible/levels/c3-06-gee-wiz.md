## Puzzle rooms and dependency chains

**P1 — ? on TNT, mask first (S3-d → S3-e).** Setup: an Aku on the left, then a ? sitting on a TNT.
Correct: take the Aku; stomp the ?, then stomp the TNT and get clear (or, masked, just break through).
Tempting wrong: spinning the stack from the side — the spin hits the TNT at ground level and it blows
at once. Loss: a life (or the mask); for the all-box gem the TNT itself must be broken (mask hint 6).
Why it works: the safety net is placed one beat before the test.

**P2 — Iron column on a timer (S2-a → S2-b).** Setup: 1UP on three iron crates with a Nitro beside
them; a slot crate in the row ahead. Correct: super body slam the column without touching the Nitro,
then move on promptly and break the slot before it turns to steel. Wrong: lingering at the column.
Loss: the slot's reward (a steel slot crate cannot be broken); a careless slam next to the Nitro costs
a life. Why it works: two clusters coupled by a timer, so mastery of the new move is measured in time.

**P3 — Nitro gauntlet and the green ! (S4-b, S4-c, S4-e, S4-f).** Setup: 10 Nitros in S4 (beside a
frog, beside the bonus lift, with the green !, and a 2×3 block at the gate). Correct: avoid all,
spin the green ! after the power stone. Wrong: skipping the ! (it sits behind two berserkers and in
front of a third). Loss: all 15 Nitros of the level. Why it works: the biggest single count trap of
the level is placed at the end, where the player is most tempted to just run.

**P4 — Bonus pit before the switch (B-d → B-e).** Setup: a ? low in the pit under the outline
staircase; the ! that materialises the outlines is reachable first. Correct: drop for the ?, return,
then hit the !. Wrong: hitting the ! first — the materialised blocks close over the pit. Loss: 1 box
(all-box). Why it works: the order is the puzzle; the room teaches it without lives at stake.

**P5 — Goat and the three stacks (S1-j).** Normal play: spin the three bases (the stacks are not
explosive). TT: the goat is the tool — slide it into the stacks or stand on it to spin the tops.

## Design logic of this level

1. **Mechanic gate right after the unlock.** Boss 1 gives the super body slam and stage 6 opens with
   a 2×2×2 iron cube beside the path, no enemy or explosive in reach (TEACH); S2 re-uses it as a
   column under a 1UP with a Nitro beside it and a timer ahead (TEST).
2. **"Explosives hidden among normal crates" is the stage's crate theme** (F3 intro): 15 Nitros and
   1 TNT, almost all touching or next to ordinary crates (W+N, ?+W+W+N, N beside the iron column,
   ? on TNT) rather than standing alone in walls.
3. **Enemy–explosive adjacency.** Nitros sit next to frogs (S1-e, S4-b) and next to a berserker
   (S3-b): frogs move toward Crash and berserkers swing wide, so the fight itself pushes the player
   toward the Nitro.
4. **Mask before hazard.** S1-d precedes the first Nitro mixes; S3-d precedes the ? on TNT.
5. **Slot crates as timers.** Three slots (S1-f, S2-b, S3-f); mask hint 4 explicitly links the iron
   column to the slot ahead, so the slot's countdown spans two clusters.
6. **Density curve.** S1 31 crates (incl. 8 iron + 9 stacked), S2 17, S3 8 (enemies dominate), S4
   opens with the 10-crate reward block after C3 and then becomes a Nitro gauntlet (10 Nitros).
7. **The count trap is at the exit.** 8 of the 15 Nitros sit in the last two clusters; without the
   green ! the player loses 15 boxes at the goal. TT deliberately skips it (TA7).
8. **Stacks are uniform and readable.** Most structures are 2-stacks or 3-stacks in rows; the only
   large block is the reward at S4-a. Complexity comes from neighbours (Nitro, enemies, slot timers),
   not from the stack shapes.
9. **Enemies as tools in Time Trial.** The goat breaks 1-s boxes when slid into (TT 08–09); one frog
   is left alive at the start as a timing cue (TT 01).
10. **Bonus grammar.** Three striped sandwiches, then arrow-to-floating-crate twice (single, then a
    2×3 block), then an outline exit with a pit crate that must be taken before the switch.

## Transfer to the skate game

1. **Unlock sandbox.** When a new crate-breaking move appears (our body slam, shock radius 3.4 m),
   put a 2×2×2 cluster (2.3 m cube) of slam-only/multi-hit crates beside the path with nothing
   dangerous within ~6 m: one slam from above covers the cluster, teaching the area effect.
2. **Life on a reinforced column with a Nitro beside it.** Life crate on a 3-stack (top 3.46 m):
   double jump (4.6 m) reaches it on foot; put one Nitro 1 crate to the side so a sloppy slam or a
   board plow (Nitro kills on touch) is punished. Board riders must dismount: the column top is a
