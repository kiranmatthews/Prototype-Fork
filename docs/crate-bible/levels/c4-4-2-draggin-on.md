# Crash 4 — 4-2 Draggin' On

Sources: GR 4-2 (Gameranx "All Crates & Hidden Gem" guide, id 210422); gamepressure.com Draggin' On walkthrough
(139 boxes, 22-box bonus, hidden gem behind a lion, tape at the big bell); LP1 1:46:25–1:52:08 (archive.org PS4
completionist longplay, Part 1), read at 2 fps and 1 s (bonus) at 854×480, plus HUD counter crops.

Evidence level: the total 139 (HUD 0/139 at 1:46:50) and the bonus 22 (bonus HUD, with its per-crate steps) were
read directly. So were the bonus → main transfer (98 → 104/139 at 1:50:12–14) and HUD 79/139 at 1:49:18. GR's
tallies agree with every HUD anchor (82 pre-bonus + C5 = 83, + 22 = 105). Crate types are legible for the opening
columns, the TNT and fire-crate clusters, the bonus Nitro blocks and the pagoda row. Long whirlwind stretches
(S3-c, S5-b, S6) are only resolved to group level. The run is deathless: all six gems plus the N. Sanely Perfect
relic (LP1 1:52:06–08). The blue gem (break no crates) was not attempted.

**Grind rails: none.** The brief expected rail grinding on dragons. Every frame of the run shows a vertical temple-tower climb:
stone courtyards, plank ledges bolted to tower walls, ropes, narrow gold-band ledges and floating platforms. The
"dragons" are green spectral serpents that sweep across the walls in fixed arcs as a hazard. You never ride them,
and nothing in the level is a rail. GR never mentions rails, and gamepressure describes bells, lions and climbing
platforms. The rail questions are answered below with the closest real structures: crate placement along the
whirlwind hover line and along the gold-band ledges.

Devices: **Akano mask** (purple "dark-matter" whirlwind, picked up as a black orb with purple eyes): a continuous
spin that smashes crates and enemies and gives extra hang time and a higher double jump. Many crates float on its
hover arcs. **Gong monks**: spectral monks in arched wall alcoves strike gongs, sending out ring shockwaves that push
Crash back. **Guardian lions** (GR "golems"): white/green lion spirits beaten with the whirlwind. **Spectral
dragons**: sweeping wall hazards. **Ropes** (swing/climb), a **lift**.

## Puzzle rooms and dependency chains

1. **Opening TNT column (S1-a/S1-b).** Spin the wood columns, stomp the settled TNT, step back. Spinning the TNT
   costs a life. Teaches the fuse on a flat, safe floor.
2. **TNT springboard (S2-a).** Stomp, bounce into the two floating crates before the 3 s blast. Lighting it and
   walking away destroys only the TNT, so 2 crates are lost (all-box only).
3. **Bonus Nitro walls (B-h).** Route around 9 Nitro. The green ! at the end pays them. Touching one ends the attempt.
4. **Four floating TNT (S7-a).** Turn the whirlwind off and light one. The chain clears all four.
5. **Blue gem (whole level).** The opposite instruction: break nothing. The opening TNT column, the checkpoints and
   every crate on a ledge become obstacles. The whirlwind has to be switched off before each crate group.

## Design logic of this level

1. **No vehicle, no rails: verticality replaces speed.** The level is a climb. Crates mark ledges, and floating
   crates mark where the hover can take you.
2. **The mask defines HIGH.** Before Akano (S1–S2a), every crate is in normal reach or a TNT bounce away. After it,
   crates float on hover arcs (S2-c, S4-b, S4-c). The mask is taught by a single floating crate right after it is granted.
3. **TNT taught, then reused three ways.** As a stack hazard (S1-a), as a springboard (S2-a), as a chain row (S4-a,
   S7-a). Each use sits right after a checkpoint.
4. **Hazard crates stacked on reward crates.** Fire over fruit (S2-b), fire-fire over ? (S3-b). The reward is in
   the same column as the danger, off the main line.
5. **Count traps beside checkpoints and in alcoves.** Two crates right of C2 (S3-a), a crate in a lion alcove
   (S7-b), 12 behind a wall (S6-a). The all-box goal sends the player sideways, while the clear goal goes straight up.
6. **Bonus beside a checkpoint.** C5 sits by the bonus pad. GR's blue-gem advice uses the bonus as a save point,
   because entering it saves progress without breaking a checkpoint crate.
7. **Nitro only in the bonus.** The main route uses TNT as its explosive. The bonus's 9 Nitro (41 % of it) are paid
   by one switch. In the bonus, the explosive is a navigation problem, not a crate puzzle.
8. **Density.** S3 (30 crates in about 30 s of whirlwind) is the densest stretch: a reward for the mask.
   The wall climbs (S2, S4) are sparse and precise.

## Transfer to the skate game

1. **Air-arc crates instead of rails.** Our vert launch (32 m/s) gives long air. Place 2–3 crates floating along the
   ballistic arc of a quarter-pipe exit, 1–1.5 m apart vertically. They are taken only by launching on the right line.
   This is the hover-arc analogue of S2-c/S4-c.
2. **TNT springboard on foot.** A TNT on a ledge under two floating crates at 2.5–3 m above its lid: crate-stomp
   bounce is 14 m/s ≈ 3 m. The player stomps it (starting the 3 s fuse), bounces twice, and escapes the 2.75 m blast.
3. **Twin opening columns.** At a level start, two 3-stacks (`tnt/wood/wood` beside `wood/wood/wood`) plus one
   multi-hit crate. Board rule: plowing the wood at ≥ 13 m/s is safe, but the TNT on top drops. Rolling into the
   TNT at 3–12 m/s lights it safely, while ≥ 13 m/s kills. The stack teaches the speed rule before any enemy.
4. **Ledge single-files.** On narrow ledges (≤ 1.2 m), put crates in a single file 2–3 crates long. These are
   equal-height rows, so they become grind ledges in our game. A skater can grind-collect what the Crash player walks through.
