## Puzzle rooms and dependency chains
- **P1 Ice line-up (S1-d, again at S3-b).** Setup: boxes (or Nitros) on a slippery patch where steering is gone. Correct sequence: choose your lane on the snow before the ice (left for S1-d, centre for S3-b). As each box breaks, hold toward the next (p095L). Tempting wrong move: reacting once on the ice. You slide past the second box (S1-d) or into a Nitro (S3-b). Loss: all-box (one ? missed means a reset); at S3-b, a life. Why it works: the same terrain is used twice, first with a soft penalty, then a lethal one.
- **P2 Pre-lit TNT timing (S2-c, S2-e, S3-a, S4-c).** Setup: TNT that light themselves before you arrive. Correct: cruise without the speed burst, pass after the blast or between the gate pair while it fires, and keep away from the blast side (left at S4-c). Tempting wrong move: dashing to "beat" the TNT, or steering at it as if it were a normal box (POINT 6). Loss: a life. The TNT count themselves either way. Why it works: TNT becomes a time-based gate that reads instantly on a vehicle where you cannot stop.
- **P3 Conflicting safe line vs box line (S2-b).** Setup: orca hole 1 pushes you right, but the ? is on the left just past it. Correct: jump on the orca-safe side, then steer left in the air or on landing. Wrong move: staying right, which misses the box. Loss: all-box only. Why: the clear route and the all-box route differ only by a lateral commitment.
- **P4 Nitro field exit leads into the reward (S4-a → S4-b).** Correct line: centre → right → jump the right-end Nitro → land on the right edge, which is exactly where the 3-box line starts. Why: the designers reward surviving by putting you on the boxes, with no extra steering.
- **P5 End-of-level Nitro switch (S4-f).** The 12 Nitros cannot be broken safely by riding. They count only if you hit the green ! on foot after the ride. Forgetting it loses the gem even after a perfect ride.

## Design logic of this level
1. **Every box is a steering instruction.** Under forced scroll, a box's lane matters more than its type. The level alternates sides almost box by box (S1-a R-L-C; S2-b L, S2-c R, S2-d L; S3-c left pair, S3-d right pair; S4-e L-R-L), which produces a slalom beat about every one to two seconds of riding.
2. **Landing markers.** Single boxes sit on the far bank of jumps (S1-c, S2-b, S3-c, S4-d) and C3 sits where a pond jump lands. On a vehicle you aim the jump at the box, so the box doubles as a visual target for the landing.
3. **Ice removes steering, and boxes on ice test pre-alignment.** The ice groups (S1-d boxes, S3-b Nitros) only work if you commit to a lane before the patch. Lesson first, lethal version second.
4. **Pre-lit TNT are timed gates, not collectibles.** All six TNT self-detonate and count themselves. Their job is to deny a lane for a moment (S2-e, S4-c) or to form a gate you ride through as it fires (S3-a). POINT 6's "no acceleration" turns the speed burst into a risk.
5. **Collectible density is flat; explosive density rises.** Non-explosive boxes per section: 10 / 5 / 5 / 7 (C and 1UP included). Explosives per section: 0 / 3 / 6 / 9. Difficulty grows through hazards, not through more pickups.
6. **The reward follows the hazard's exit.** After the Nitro zone, the three-box line sits on the side where the safe line leaves the field (S4-b). After C1, the 1UP sits on the line you already hold.
7. **Count traps are lateral, not hidden.** With no secrets, the all-box difficulty comes from placements that contradict the obvious line (S2-b), from the final zig-zag (S4-e), from a box missing on the F2 map (S4-d), and from the off-bear green ! (S4-f).
8. **Checkpoints split the boxes evenly.** C1 after 10 boxes, C2 after 18, C3 after 29 of 45. Each sits in a calm beat: between seals, after a TNT, on a landing.
9. **Groups are lined up along the route, not across it.** Multi-box groups run along a single lane (S2-c along the right edge, S3-c/S3-d pairs one behind the other, S4-b's diagonal line), so one held lane collects the whole group at speed. Only S1-a and S4-e are spread across lanes, as deliberate slaloms.
10. **Totems couple boxes to hurdles.** A box beside a standing totem followed by fallen totems (S1-b) creates "collect, then jump at once" timing; the POINT 12 chicane rehearses the L-R-L of the final slope.
11. **Lost boxes cost a section.** You cannot ride back, so a missed box means dying or restarting at the last checkpoint (stated for the sister stage on F2 p097L; same mechanics here). The all-box goal becomes four "perfect segment" runs.

## Transfer to the skate game
1. **Lane-slalom singles on the cruise line.** Place plain wood/? crates alternating lanes about 2–3 m apart laterally, one every 18–24 m (1.5–2 s at 12 m/s cruise). Crates break only at ≥13 m/s; below 12.5 m/s the rider bails and the crate survives. A slalom of crates is therefore also a "keep your charge" test. Keep steel and Nitro off this line.
2. **Landing-marker crate after each gap.** Put a single wood/? crate 1–2 m past the far lip at the landing point of a full-charge ollie. At charged speed the rider plows it on landing. Do not put a crate before the lip, because a 1.7 m ollie only clears one crate and the take-off would be blocked.
3. **Groups along the lane, ready to grind.** Bear It lines groups up along the route (S2-c, S3-c/d, S4-b). In our game, two or more touching crates with equal tops form a rail. A 3-crate line parallel to the route (≈3.5 m long) on the exit side of a hazard is collected by one grind, as in S4-b. Put a mask or ? at the far end, which breaks at the dismount.
4. **Pre-alignment pad.** Use a low-traction ice pad (edge grinding stays at its default) with two crates offset one lane and ~2 crate lengths apart diagonally. The entry lane must be chosen before the pad (S1-d). Repeat it later with Nitros framing a centre lane at least 3.3 m from each Nitro (blast 2.75 m + 0.5 m kill margin; S3-b).
5. **Flanking TNT gate.** Put two TNT at the road edges at least 6.6 m apart (centre-to-centre), so a centred rider is never within radius + 0.5 m if either goes off (S3-a). Never put TNT on the cruise line: at ≥13 m/s board contact detonates it and kills.
6. **Nitro field with a memorised exit and a reward on the exit side.** 6–8 Nitros across a 9–10 m road leaving one S-shaped 3 m line. The line exits to one edge, where a 3-crate grind row starts (S4-a → S4-b). Place the `nitrobang` (green !) on foot or on a slow stretch after the run, so the all-box goal needs a deliberate last action (S4-f).
7. **Checkpoint plus life in a calm beat.** Put C1 between two slow, jumpable enemies and a life crate straight after it on the held line (C1 + S2-a). Split the crates evenly between checkpoints (about 10 / 8 / 11 / 16).
