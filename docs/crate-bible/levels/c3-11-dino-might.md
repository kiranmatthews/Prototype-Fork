## Puzzles and dependency chains
1. **Green ! dependency (S2-d, S3-f, Y-h, Y-i → Y-G).** Ten Nitro, four of them on the main path, can only be cleared
   by the green ! at the end of the yellow course. The all-box gem therefore needs the yellow gem even though most
   boxes are on the main path. What is lost by skipping: the gem only.
2. **Iron-and-TNT stacks (Y-d, Y-f, Y-g).** Correct: take the 1UP from the top of Y-f first; slam Y-g from in
   front so its bottom irons break, step back while the dropped TNT burns. Wrong: slamming on top of a TNT stack
   kills you; letting the TNT drop under the 1UP destroys the life. Loss: a life or a death; all boxes still count.
3. **Arrow column → steel bridge (S3-e → S3-f → S3-g).** Bounce off the steel arrow, each bonk breaks the next
   crate, the last bonk hits the !, which builds a steel bar over two Nitro leading to a floating ?. Wrong: walking
   on to the Nitro under the bar. TT: skip the bounces (slide + double jump, then a right-angled arrow bounce).
4. **Route order.** P3 NOTE: when you first have the yellow gem, run the main path to the lava area *first*, then
   come back for the platform — the yellow course drops you at the lava area and you would otherwise forget the
   boxes between C1 and there. F3: take the yellow course first, then walk back from the lava area (POINT 4).
5. **Bonus step-before-slam (B-e/B-f) and reach slam (B-g).** Use the 2×2 iron block as a step to the floating ?
   before slamming it; slam from the platform edge to break the floating irons over the gap.
6. **Chase auto-collection (S4).** The triceratops breaks every crate behind you and they count; crates in the
   chase are pacing and time-trial decisions, not all-box risks.

## Design logic of this level
1. **Two-visit level.** First visit: crystal and main path; the yellow gem course (26 boxes, secret gem, green !,
   stage-32 warp) is a return-visit route. Main-path Nitro make the all-box goal visibly incomplete until then.
2. **An earned power is the lesson.** The yellow course is iron-and-TNT stacks for the super body slam (the
   first boss's reward); the bonus is 17 iron crates teaching slam radius and step-before-slam.
3. **Stack grammar escalates:** striped-based 3-stacks (S1) → time-crate stacks (S2) → iron/TNT 3- and 4-stacks
   with a life on top (yellow) → floating column over an arrow (lava area).
4. **Big fruit walls at section starts.** The 3×3 wall after C2 and the pyramid at the lava area repeat Tomb Time's
   front-loaded deposits.
5. **The ! builds terrain, not boxes.** The only steel ! adds a steel bridge; the outline cells are not boxes. A
   designer reading F3 alone would over-count by six.
6. **Checkpoint placement follows the two routes.** C1 sits on the yellow platform so both routes restart there;
   the yellow course has its own C after its hardest stacks; C3 sits right before the chase.
7. **Chase sections carry crates that the chaser collects.** Crates there pace the run and serve the time trial;
   none is a skill check for the all-box goal.
8. **Allies as crate-breakers.** Baby T smashes crates and grass; in time trial it is slower than running.
9. **Hazard spacing:** crates sit in the gaps between geysers, lava pools and Crafish, never on the lava.

## Transfer to the skate game
1. **Switch in route B that clears hazards in route A.** Put a `nitrobang` at the end of an optional branch; put 2–4
   Nitro beside the main line (not on it) so riders see the unfinished count. The main line stays rideable.
2. **Arrow column over a pit.** A steel arrow under a floating column of three crates (≈ 4.5–6 m up, the held
