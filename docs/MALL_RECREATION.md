# Mall · New York

Source-owned reconstruction of the original 1999 THPS Mall for the fork's existing skater. Select **Mall · New York** in MENU, or open `/?playtest&level=mall`. Add `&lite` for the fast renderer. The shared Geometry Lab and published `public/levels.json` are unchanged by this task.

Arrow keys/WASD steer; hold Space to build speed and release to ollie; E grinds; F spins. K/L move between the five checkpoint stops. M shows the developer controls if MENU is hidden.

## Fidelity still requiring evidence

The route tests demonstrate playable connections and safe landings. They do not prove a dimension-exact recreation. The bench form and centered placement now match the original footage. Its precise dimensions, the first deck length, combo poles and escalator side profile remain estimates. The water trough is now playable, while the exact side-access contours need further comparison. The new receiver bank, deck thicknesses and supports are reference-derived estimates. The fountain intermediate landing is now functional, but its exact profile and the exit upper passage still need source comparison. The courtyard wall transitions are now present, but their exact profile, the blue-bank curvature and flared ends, and the planter/ledge arrangement still need comparison. Fountain materials, storefront massing and the concourse silhouette remain visibly simplified. Hall lengths and widths, deck elevations, transition radii, beam heights and precise folded-entry/exit contours remain estimates. The entry topology now includes both source quarter returns, but its metric dimensions still need calibration. The supplied cutaway hides some surfaces and supplies no measurement scale. An original PS1 Mall mesh/collision export, or calibrated plans and views, would resolve those uncertainties. The goal remains open.
