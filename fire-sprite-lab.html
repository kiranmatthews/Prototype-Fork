About the shared sprite set

Three independently drawn families: narrow, medium and broad. Every animation frame was generated in its own imagegen call, informed by the supplied Crash 4 recordings. Arrangements share those drawings and change their hue. Step or scrub the unblended cels; switch to a single flame to inspect the cycle. The heat haze is the game’s existing Jak framebuffer pass.
