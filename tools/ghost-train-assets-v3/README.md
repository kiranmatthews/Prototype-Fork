# Derelict ghost train bathhouse kit

Four original low-poly Meshy T2 models and their Meshy textures support the
campy abandoned bathhouse revision. Geometry previews, initial textures and
two corrective retextures consumed **80 existing Meshy credits**. No credits
were purchased. `tasks.json` records the named provider operations; raw
provider responses and signed download URLs remain in the ignored
`.img2threejs/ghost-train-v3` authoring cache.

The packed kit contains 5,900 triangles. `pack_assets.py` reuses the established
Meshy packer and preserves the supplied surfaces and UVs. Wall/facade atlases
are 1024 px; rubbish and boiler atlases are 512 px. The arch generation arrived
as a deep, closed facade, so it is placed as a side alcove at uniform scale.
The playable doorways use open native masonry and retain camera clearance.

The tile material is sampled from the actual Meshy panel through its triangle
UVs. `texture-provenance.json` records the projection, crop and hashes. Runtime
dampness, water stains, original graffiti, green steam and light halos are
authored presentation effects; they are not additional provider models.

The ImageGen reference sheet is `docs/ghost-train-concepts/derelict-baths-v3.png`.
It is a concept mockup, separate from actual browser gameplay evidence.
