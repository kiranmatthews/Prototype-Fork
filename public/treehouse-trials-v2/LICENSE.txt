Treehouse Trials V2 scenery subset reused for Carlisle Coast

Created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai/) and OpenAI image generation for the
project owner. The owner explicitly authorized reuse for Carlisle Coast.

This directory publishes only fern-a, groundcover-a, crown-b and tree-b from the
in-progress Treehouse Trials V2 kit. Their packed GLB bytes are unchanged. The
Carlisle level authors independent placement, scale, rotation and runtime tint.
The original model/task hashes and packing evidence are retained in each model
manifest and this directory's subset provenance.json. Reuse consumed no further
generation credits. This does not publish the remaining Treehouse asset kit.
