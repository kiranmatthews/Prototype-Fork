# Three Fingers Hand Rig

**Hand Rig by Andy Cuccaro** — licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source product: [Three Fingers Hand Rig](https://andycuccaro.gumroad.com/l/three-finger-hand-rig).

The shipped GLB is a browser-specific derivative of the owner's supplied free
`.blend`: Catmull-Clark level 2, the authored left hand plus a positive-scale
right-hand mirror, one rigid palm root plus twelve deforming digit bones per
hand, and at most four normalized weights per vertex. Its textureless materials
do not use either source UV set or any of the nine constant colour layers, so
those buffers are excluded while positions, normals, joints, weights, skins,
and triangle corners remain exact. See `provenance.json` for hashes and the
conversion record.
