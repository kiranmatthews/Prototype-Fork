# Three Fingers Hand Rig

**Hand Rig by Andy Cuccaro** — licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source product: [Three Fingers Hand Rig](https://andycuccaro.gumroad.com/l/three-finger-hand-rig).

The shipped GLB is a browser-specific derivative of the owner's supplied free
`.blend`: Catmull-Clark level 2, two positive-scale mirrored hands swapped to
keep the thumbs rider-forward in the inward-palm rest, one rigid palm root plus
twelve deforming digit bones per hand, and at most four normalized weights per
vertex. See `provenance.json` for hashes and the exact conversion record.
