Fire Sprite Lab v5 — source assets

Flame02 and Flame03 by Thomas Iché / Unity Technologies, Unity Labs Paris.
Released under CC0 1.0 in “Free VFX image sequences and flipbooks”, 28 November 2016.
Source: https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Downloads:
https://unity3d.com/files/labs/downloads/vfx/assets01/Flame02/Flame02-flipbooks.zip
https://unity3d.com/files/labs/downloads/vfx/assets01/Flame03/Flame03-flipbooks.zip

Original TGA sheets are retained. Runtime WebP sheets preserve their native
dimensions, alpha and visible RGB. The shared material remaps colour and
opacity; arrangement, rate and hue vary between exhibits.

The separate paint-master.png is a project image generated with the built-in
image_gen tool. Its six sampled colours are shared by all source frames.
It is not part of the Unity CC0 release. See provenance.json for its hash,
original workspace path and prompt. Crash Bandicoot 4 recordings were visual
references only; no game footage or extracted game textures are shipped.

The basin uses a separate source: Small Camp Fire by JangaFX, released as
CC0 (Public Domain) on https://jangafx.com/software/embergen/download/free-vdb-animations
Download: https://www.mediafire.com/file/9xx9icyomy2txv7/SmallCampfireVDB.zip/file
Its flames channel is rendered through a fixed orthographic camera with
uniform scale. Only constant transparent padding is cropped for packing;
the flame is not stretched, warped or assembled from repeated torch sprites.
Source hashes and render settings are retained in
art/fire-sprites/basin-source/provenance.json. Runtime source/interval metadata
is in basin/cycle.json. The shared imagegen palette remains the same.
