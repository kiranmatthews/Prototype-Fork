Elevation grid obtained from the independent Bryce Canyon Conservancy atlas:
https://brycecanyon.co/landmarks.html
https://brycecanyon.co/assets/landmark-collection/wall-street/model.json
https://brycecanyon.co/assets/landmark-collection/wall-street/detailed.glb.gz
The atlas describes an upper-envelope study of measured airborne returns,
not a closed rock scan. Native 0.625m spacing, 263,169 vertices and 524,288
triangles are retained. No atlas imagery or display material is copied.
Vertex colours, scene rotation, translation and placement are modified.
Survey elevation geometry is used as a scenic backdrop, not as game data or
a survey-grade representation of the walking surface. Meshopt compression
was verified lossless against all uncompressed buffers. See survey-manifest.json.

All plant and tree assets come from the project's existing Country Club,
Custard Creek, Treehouse and Carlisle libraries and retain their existing
licences. Full authored geometry is used at all viewing distances.

Canyon pine: derived from public/country-club-art/umbrella-pine.glb.
The complete native mesh's trunk and leaf pieces are rearranged into tapered
conifer branch whorls, with additional branches and duplicated leaf pieces.
All original LOD0 triangles, original leaf UVs and the original atlas survive.
The final 24,763-triangle mesh is retained at every viewing distance.
See pine-manifest.json for source/output hashes and the reproducible authoring
script tools/desert-canyon/shape-library-pine.py (Python with Pillow).
