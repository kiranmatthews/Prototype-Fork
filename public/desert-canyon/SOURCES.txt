The atlas describes an upper-envelope study of measured airborne returns,
not a closed rock scan. Native 0.625m spacing, 263,169 vertices and 524,288
triangles are retained. No atlas imagery or display material is copied.
Vertex colours, scene rotation, translation and placement are modified.
Survey elevation geometry is used as a scenic backdrop, not as game data or
a survey-grade representation of the walking surface. Meshopt compression
was verified lossless against all uncompressed buffers. See survey-manifest.json.

All plant and tree assets come from the project's existing Country Club,
Custard Creek, Treehouse and Carlisle libraries and retain their existing
licences. Full authored geometry is used at all viewing distances.

Canyon pine: derived from public/country-club-art/umbrella-pine.glb.
The complete native mesh's trunk and leaf pieces are rearranged into tapered
conifer branch whorls, with additional branches and duplicated leaf pieces.
All original LOD0 triangles, original leaf UVs and the original atlas survive.
The final 24,763-triangle mesh is retained at every viewing distance.
See pine-manifest.json for source/output hashes and the reproducible authoring
script tools/desert-canyon/shape-library-pine.py (Python with Pillow).

Exposed valley continuation (7 November 2026)
Visual reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zwf8svpX__E
Original authored terrain, undercut strata and river contours use the existing
MIT RockGenerator SimplexNoise implementation, seed 20261107. No game files,
video frames or third-party landscape images are shipped. The full native
landscape is baked by tools/desert-canyon/build-vista.mjs into spatial cells;
all vertices, faces, UVs and colours remain at every viewing distance.
The CC0 ambientCG Rock030 displacement map supplies fine relief. Trees reuse
the full canyon conifer and original Country Club umbrella pine, with original
atlases. See vista/manifest.json for geometry counts and per-cell hashes.

Imagegen transformation iteration (7 November 2026)
golden-hour-sky.png, sandstone-strata.png and trail-gravel.png were generated
with OpenAI's built-in image_gen. The sky and sandstone received a second
iteration based on native-resolution gameplay review. Exact prompts and
concept/gameplay distinctions are in docs/desert-canyon/transformation-iterations.json.
They contain no source-game textures or extracted video imagery.

grass-cluster.glb batches four complete LOD0 copies from the existing licensed
public/carlisle-coast-fidelity/grass-tuft-b.glb. All 672 LOD0 triangles per tuft,
attributes, UVs and original embedded atlases survive; the final patch has
2,688 triangles at every viewing distance. The unused source LOD1 is not a
part of the native surface. See grass-cluster-manifest.json and the reproducible
tools/desert-canyon/cluster-library-grass.py script.

Muted cactus and dry weed set — 7 November 2026
Five separate reference images generated with OpenAI's built-in image_gen in
Crash 4 environmental styling. Prompts: tools/desert-canyon/plant-references/prompts.json.
Final native meshes generated from those references using the signed-in Tripo
Studio web app in Chrome: Smart Mesh P2.0, Triangle topology, 2K reference
textures with Remove Lighting enabled. No HD Model or Ultra Mesh generation.
Five mesh generations at 100 plan credits and five textures at 10: 550 total.
Requested triangle targets: 8,000 saguaro, 5,000 each smaller species. Actual
exports: 8,074 / 5,202 / 4,341 / 5,388 / 5,495 triangles, all below 30,000.
Each shipped source GLB is byte-identical to its downloaded native export.
No simplification or distance LODs. One atlas material per plant.
Task IDs, source hashes, dimensions and counts: plants/manifest.json and
tools/desert-canyon/tripo-plants.json. The original Blender authoring script
and earlier review images document the superseded first iteration only.

Cartoon sandstone and clay — 7 November 2026
painted-sandstone.png and painted-clay.png are OpenAI built-in image_gen
albedo textures, replacing photographic grit in the shared canyon material.
Exact prompts and the separate art-direction concept are recorded in
docs/desert-canyon/cartoon/prompts.json. No source-game textures are included.
The existing MIT RockGenerator noise/displacement technique remains the basis
of the nine procedural rock assets; authored profiles now use rounded shelves,
wide caps, broad flutes and restrained erosion. Native vista fins use rounded
cross sections and broad bevelled beds. Sparse smooth pebbles replace dense
angular scree at the same total triangle budget. The 76 underlying landscape
terrain cells are byte-identical; route, sky, lighting and plant placement
remain unchanged. All authored geometry remains at every viewing distance.
No Tripo generation or credits were used for this rock/material iteration.
