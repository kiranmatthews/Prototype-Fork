Section 01 / 13

The customs quay

A working seventeenth-century Caribbean port: limewashed ochre warehouses, shuttered arcades, terracotta roofs, palms, heavy mooring timbers and warm brass lamps beside the broad timber quay.

Section 02 / 13

The broken cargo jetty

Three storm-broken boardwalk spans on visibly rooted barnacled piles, a timber crane and suspended pallet, barrel stacks, a narrow optional salvage spar and small moored cargo lighters.

Section 03 / 13

Beneath the fort

A weathered limestone fort crowns the rocky headland; a narrow boardwalk turns beneath it into a timber-braced smugglers sea gate. Sunset over the open sea gives way to cool cave shade.

Section 04 / 13

The smugglers fissure

An old mining passage cut in wet layered limestone, crooked oak braces, bronze lanterns, rope and supply clutter. A narrow tidal fissure interrupts the worn trail; the far bank remains clearly visible.

Section 05 / 13

The hidden crystal grotto

An intimate secret alcove with restrained turquoise mineral seams, an abandoned expedition cache, damp stone shelves and old lantern glow. Curious Goonies treasure-hunt atmosphere.

Section 06 / 13

The moonpool and the Drowned Crown

The huge broken royal galleon is revealed inside a cathedral-sized limestone sea cavern. Three ragged ivory sails, ornate stern, worn hull strakes, turquoise water, pale shafts through roof fissures. A supported salvage gangway leads onto the ship.

Section 07 / 13

The abandoned afterdeck

Human-scale oak deck planks, ship cannon, coiled rope, bronze lanterns, broken carved bulwarks and heavy rigging. Warm wood against the cool cavern; clear central traversal around the mast.

Section 08 / 13

The captain's last chart

An intimate raised captain's chart cabin: aged carved oak, tarnished gilding, leaded amber stern windows, parchment sea chart and compass on a heavy table, a small treasure chest and warm hanging lanterns.

Section 09 / 13

Through the broken hull

Splintered giant ship ribs and a descending broken hatch lead into the torn keel, turquoise water below. A narrow grounded timber crossing, distant lit cargo hold, high fallen mast and dangling anchor make multiple paths visually legible.

Section 10 / 13

Below the waterline

The cavernous cargo hold with heavy curved ribs, overhead oak deck beams, stacked barrels and sea chests, warm lamps and rippling turquoise reflected light. A broad readable floor leads toward the bow hatch ramp.

Section 11 / 13

The secret powder cache

A hidden stone-and-timber powder cache visible through the shattered port hull, old sealed barrels, a small open treasure chest, a glowing turquoise jewel and subtle gold glints in dark rock.

Section 12 / 13

The escape bridge

A frayed but structurally anchored rope-and-plank escape bridge stretches from the broken galleon's bow to a rocky treasure tunnel. One clearly visible broken span, sagging cables, massive cave depth and moonlit water below.

Section 13 / 13

The king's ransom

The final treasure vault: timeworn carved stone, a broad rising dais, rich but carefully grouped gold heaps, jewelled treasure chests, warm lanterns, an old royal skull-and-crown portal and a bright distant sea exit framing the finish.
