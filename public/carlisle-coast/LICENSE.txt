Carlisle Coast original generated environment kit

The project owner commissioned these original concept references through OpenAI
image generation and the 3D scenery models through their existing Meshy credits.
No third-party model geometry or textures were copied from the reference video.

Created with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai/) and OpenAI image generation for the
project owner. The account plan tier is not recorded by the task outputs;
publication retains the conservative Meshy attribution basis, CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original authoring sources and provider responses remain in the ignored
.img2threejs/carlisle-coast directory. Exact task handles, consumed credits,
reference/source hashes and packed geometry/texture budgets are recorded in
provenance.json. No credentials or signed download links are published.
