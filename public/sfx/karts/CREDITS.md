Retrieved 26–27 October 2026. Engine and fire replacements are 24 kHz mono
PCM16, high-pass filtered (135/100 Hz), with a broad treble shelf, soft saturation,
and 300/180 ms equal-power loop crossfades. Peak normalized to 0.84; loop boundaries
selected after resampling to avoid clicks. Engine retains 3.7 seconds of real motor
rattle; fire retains 2.68 seconds of crackle. Engine pitch and volume vary with
throttle, speed, airborne load and distance. Fire volume follows distance.

Other adaptations: decoded/resampled to 24 kHz mono PCM16, peak-normalized to
0.85, 10 ms loop-seam crossfades for skid/boost-loop; one-shot silence trimmed
with 5 ms edge fades. Skids and explosion encoded as 64 kb/s AAC; short thruster
loops retain exact PCM loop boundaries. Original authors do not endorse this game.

Landing adaptation: 0.72-second body impact with transient compression, short
pitch-shifted chassis-rattle layers at 65/137 ms, a synthesized suspension thud,
and edge fades. It plays on actual airborne touchdown independently of damage;
impact strength and proximity control its volume.
