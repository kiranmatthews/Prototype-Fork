# Third-party assets

Everything else in this repository is written here. These are not, and this is
where they came from.

## Scenery library

The sixty-two scenery meshes behind the SCENERY palette's six "library"
families — trees, plants, boulders, rocks, trunks and temple slabs — are baked
into `src/prop-data.ts` by `tools/bake-props.mjs`. All of them are **CC0 1.0
(public domain)**; credit below is offered because it is deserved, not because
it is required.

| Source | Author | Licence | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Nature Kit 2.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | trees, palms, bushes, grasses, bamboo, hanging moss, rocks, boulders, logs, stumps, statues, obelisks, plinths |
| [Graveyard Kit 5.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | square pillar, obelisk, carved altar, broken wall, rubble, long dead trunk |
| [Modular Ruins Pack](https://poly.pizza/) | Quaternius | CC0 1.0 | collapsed wall, stub column, loose bricks, inlaid floor |
| Big Leaf Plant | reyshapes (via Poly Pizza) | CC0 1.0 | elephant ear |
| Cobblestone tile | Kay Lousberg (via Poly Pizza) | CC0 1.0 | cobbled path |

Only the GEOMETRY is used. None of these ship with a texture in this game:
each source material is mapped onto a surface role (bark, sawn wood, leaf,
stone, dirt), painted with this game's own surface textures, and tinted from
its own palettes. Positions are requantised to 16 bits,
normals to 8, and UVs are regenerated by box projection — so what is committed
is a derived, re-scaled, re-materialised form of the originals, not a copy of
the distributed files.

To rebuild it, download the kits and run:

```
node tools/bake-props.mjs <dir-of-glbs>... > src/prop-data.ts
```

## HUD display face

The HUD, the crate stencils and every plate on screen are set in **Roo**,
shipped as `public/fonts/Roo.ttf` (`.otf` alongside it). On the HUD it is
rendered by the roo-web SVG renderer (`src/roo-text.js`, MODIFIED from the
drop to match this game's reference art — see its header — wrapped by
`src/rootext.ts`); on the crate faces it is painted flat into the
texture canvas, keyline only, no gradient or bevel.

An earlier HUD cut its lettering from three hand-drawn alphabet sheets, baked
into a raster atlas. That path is gone — the atlas, its data table and the
baker were removed when Roo took over. The original sheets are still kept at
full resolution in `art/hudfont-sheet{1,2,3}.png` because they are the only
copy of that artwork; nothing in the build reads them any more, so they can be
deleted if that artwork is not wanted.

## HUD icons

Same story: the crate and fruit counters, the life face and the mask crate's
crossbones sticker are all drawn artwork, kept at full resolution in
`art/hud-*.png`.

```
node tools/bake-hudicons.mjs
```

That writes `public/{crate,apple,roo,crossbones}.png`. The sources are 640–1024px
renders of things the game draws at 30–100px, so the baker floors each one's
alpha, trims to the artwork's own edges where a crop is safe, and fits the
longest side to the size that's actually used. Together that takes the four from
1.49MB to 226KB.

Two of the passes are deliberately selective. The crate arrives with a faint
wash of low-alpha pixels across its whole canvas — its corner sits at alpha 27 —
which draws as a dim grey square over bright scenery, so its floor is high;
the apple's faint edge is genuine feather and keeps a low one. And the
crossbones is NOT trimmed: its ink is 552x473 but the crate face stretches it
into a square box, so cropping to the ink would stretch the bones 17% taller.

The sky paintings are left at full size on purpose. They are not oversized —
each is wrapped twice around the sky dome, so a 90-degree view already upscales
about 1.4x to a 1280px screen, and halving them would visibly soften the
largest thing on screen. They also carry the horizon as an absolute pixel
constant (`SKY_HORIZON_PX` against `SKY_IMG_H`), so a resize would move the mist
band as well as blur it.
